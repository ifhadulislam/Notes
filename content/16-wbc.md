# White Cell Disorders, Leukemias & Plasma Cell Neoplasms

## Non-neoplastic Leukocyte Disorders

| Disorder | Causes |
|---|---|
| ==Neutrophilia== | ==Acute bacterial infections==, tissue necrosis (MI, burns), ==steroids (demargination)==, stress, smoking, CML |
| ==Eosinophilia== | ==Allergy (asthma, hay fever), parasites (tissue-invasive helminths)==, drugs, ==Hodgkin lymphoma==, Churg–Strauss, Addison, ==Loeffler==, hypereosinophilic syndrome (PDGFRA) |
| ==Basophilia== | ==Myeloproliferative neoplasms (CML)== |
| ==Monocytosis== | ==TB, brucellosis, SABE, malaria==, recovery phase, IBD, CMML |
| ==Lymphocytosis== | ==Viral (EBV, CMV, hepatitis), pertussis (very high), TB, brucellosis==, CLL |
| ==Neutropenia / agranulocytosis== | ==Drugs (most common — clozapine, carbimazole, sulfonamides, chemotherapy)==, viral, typhoid, aplastic anemia, SLE, ==Felty syndrome==, ==benign ethnic neutropenia==, ==cyclic neutropenia (ELANE — every 21 days)==, Kostmann (HAX1) |

### Leukemoid reaction vs CML :

| Feature | ==Leukemoid reaction== | ==CML== |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Severe infection, TB, hemolysis, tumours | ==BCR-ABL== |
| TLC | > 50,000/µL | Often > 100,000/µL |
| Differential | ==Mostly mature, "left shift" (bands, metamyelocytes)== | ==Full spectrum, ↑ myelocytes & neutrophils ("bimodal peak")== |
| ==LAP score (NAP)== | ==↑ (high)== | ==↓ (low)== |
| ==Basophils== | Normal | ==↑ (basophilia)== |
| Toxic changes | ==Toxic granules, Döhle bodies, cytoplasmic vacuoles== | Absent |
| Splenomegaly | Absent / mild | ==Massive== |
| ==Philadelphia chromosome== | Absent | ==Present (95%)== |

- ==Leukoerythroblastic picture== (immature WBCs + nucleated RBCs + teardrops) : ==marrow infiltration (metastatic Ca, myelofibrosis, TB, myeloma)==, severe hemolysis/bleeding.

### Reactive lymphadenitis & infectious mononucleosis :

- ==Infectious mononucleosis (EBV)== — ==fever, pharyngitis, posterior cervical lymphadenopathy, splenomegaly, atypical lymphocytes (> 10%, CD8 T cells — "ballerina-skirt" cytoplasm indented by RBCs)==; ==heterophile antibodies (Paul–Bunnell, Monospot)==, EBV VCA-IgM; ==ampicillin rash==; splenic rupture.

## Leukemia — General

| Feature | Acute leukemia | Chronic leukemia |
|---|---|---|
| Blasts | ==≥ 20% in marrow / blood (WHO)== | < 20% (chronic phase) |
| Cells | Immature (blasts) | Mature-appearing |
| Onset | Abrupt; ==bone pain, fever, bleeding, infections== | Insidious; often incidental |

### Acute leukemias — diagnosis :

| Test | ==AML== | ==ALL== |
|---|---|---|
| Blast morphology | Larger, more cytoplasm, ==granules, Auer rods==, 3–5 nucleoli | ==Small, scant cytoplasm, condensed chromatin==, 1–2 nucleoli |
| ==Myeloperoxidase (MPO)== | ==+ (≥ 3% blasts)== | ==−== |
| ==Sudan black B== | + | − |
| ==Non-specific esterase (NSE, α-naphthyl acetate)== | ==+ in monocytic (M4, M5) — fluoride-sensitive== | − |
| ==PAS== | − (except erythroleukemia — M6, block) | ==+ (block / coarse granular positivity)== |
| ==TdT== | − | ==+ (precursor B & T)== |
| Markers | ==CD13, CD33, CD117, CD34, MPO==; CD14/CD64 (mono); CD41/CD61 (mega) | ==B-ALL : CD19, CD10 (CALLA), CD22, cytoplasmic CD79a; T-ALL : CD3, CD7, CD1a, CD2, CD5== |

## Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL)

- ==Most common cancer of childhood (peak 2–5 years)==; ==B-ALL (85%)==, T-ALL (15% — adolescent males, ==mediastinal (thymic) mass==, high WBC).
- Features : ==bone pain & tenderness (marrow expansion)==, ==CNS involvement (headache, cranial nerve palsy) — sanctuary site==, ==testicular involvement (relapse)==, lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, pancytopenia.
- Associations : ==Down syndrome (↑ 10–20×)==, ataxia telangiectasia, Bloom, radiation.

| Prognostic factor | Favourable | Unfavourable |
|---|---|---|
| Age | ==2–10 years== | ==< 1 year (KMT2A / MLL rearrangement), > 10 years (adolescents, adults)== |
| TLC at diagnosis | < 50,000/µL | ==> 50,000/µL== |
| Cytogenetics | ==Hyperdiploidy (> 50 chromosomes), t(12;21) ETV6-RUNX1 (most common translocation in children)== | ==t(9;22) Philadelphia (adults — 25%), t(4;11) KMT2A-AFF1, hypodiploidy, Ph-like (BCR-ABL1-like), iAMP21== |
| Response | ==Rapid early response, MRD negative at end of induction== | MRD positive |
| Others | Female, B-cell | ==T-cell (historically), CNS / testis involvement== |

## Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)

- ==Adults (median ~65 y)==; most common acute leukemia in adults; ↑ with benzene, radiation, ==alkylating agents (5–10 y; 5q/7q)==, ==topoisomerase II inhibitors (etoposide — 1–3 y; 11q23 KMT2A)==, MDS/MPN, ==Down syndrome (AML-M7 < 3 y)==, Fanconi.

| ==NPM1 mutated== (without FLT3-ITD) | NPM1 (cytoplasmic) | ==Good== — most common mutation in normal karyotype AML; =="cup-like" nuclei== |

| ==FLT3-ITD== | FLT3 | ==Poor== (midostaurin, gilteritinib) |

## Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (MPN)

- ==Clonal stem-cell disorders with ↑ production of one or more mature myeloid lineages==; ==constitutively activated tyrosine kinases==; ==splenomegaly (extramedullary hematopoiesis)==; may progress to ==spent phase (myelofibrosis)== or ==acute leukemia==.

| Disease | Mutation | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==CML== | ==BCR-ABL1 — t(9;22)(q34;q11) — Philadelphia chromosome (shortened 22)== | ==p210 (CML), p190 (Ph+ ALL)==; ==massive splenomegaly (dragging sensation)==, ↑↑ WBC with myelocytes, ==basophilia, ↓ LAP==, ↑ platelets; phases : ==chronic → accelerated → blast crisis (2/3 myeloid, 1/3 lymphoid)==; ==imatinib (TKI)==; monitor by ==RT-PCR (BCR-ABL transcript)== |
| ==Polycythemia vera (PV)== | ==JAK2 V617F (> 95%)==, JAK2 exon 12 | ==↑ RBC mass (panmyelosis — ↑ WBC, platelets too)==, ==↓ EPO (vs secondary)==; ==aquagenic pruritus (histamine — basophils)==, ==plethora, thrombosis (Budd–Chiari)==, ==erythromelalgia==, gout, hypertension, peptic ulcer; ==spent phase (myelofibrosis), AML (2%)==; ==phlebotomy, aspirin, hydroxyurea== |
| ==Essential thrombocythemia (ET)== | ==JAK2 (50%), CALR (30%), MPL (5–10%)== | ==Platelets ≥ 450,000/µL==, ==large megakaryocytes in clusters (staghorn nuclei)==; thrombosis & bleeding (acquired vWD); ==erythromelalgia==; diagnosis of exclusion (iron deficiency, inflammation) |
| ==Primary myelofibrosis (PMF)== | ==JAK2 (50%), CALR (30%), MPL== | ==Marrow fibrosis (reticulin, collagen — TGF-β, PDGF from megakaryocytes)== → ==dry tap==; ==leukoerythroblastic smear + teardrop cells (dacrocytes)==; ==massive splenomegaly (extramedullary hematopoiesis)==; ==atypical, clustered megakaryocytes (cloud-like nuclei)==; osteosclerosis; ruxolitinib |
| Chronic neutrophilic leukemia | ==CSF3R== | Neutrophilia |
| Systemic mastocytosis | ==KIT D816V== | ==Urticaria pigmentosa (Darier sign)==, ↑ tryptase |
| Myeloid neoplasms with eosinophilia | ==PDGFRA (FIP1L1-PDGFRA), PDGFRB, FGFR1== | ==Imatinib-responsive (PDGFR)== |

:::mnemo MPN mutations
"==CML — BCR-ABL== · ==PV — JAK2== (almost always) · ==ET & PMF — JAK2 / CALR / MPL==". ==Triple-negative ET/PMF== — worse.
:::

:::pearl
- ==Polycythemia vera vs secondary polycythemia : EPO ↓ in PV, ↑ in secondary (hypoxia, tumours)==; ==splenomegaly only in PV==; ==↑ WBC & platelets only in PV==.
- ==Gaisböck syndrome (relative / stress polycythemia)== — ↓ plasma volume; obese hypertensive smokers.
:::

## Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia / Small Lymphocytic Lymphoma (CLL/SLL)

- ==Most common leukemia in adults in the West (> 60 y)==; rare in Asia.
- ==Mature B cells : CD5 + CD19 + CD20 (dim) CD23 +==, ==dim surface Ig==; ==absolute B-lymphocytosis ≥ 5000/µL (monoclonal)==.
- Smear : ==small mature lymphocytes with clumped "soccer-ball" chromatin + smudge (basket) cells==.
- Lymph node (SLL) : ==diffuse effacement with pale proliferation centres (prolymphocytes, para-immunoblasts) — pathognomonic==.
- Complications : ==hypogammaglobulinemia (infections — most common cause of death)==, ==autoimmune hemolytic anemia (warm) & ITP==, ==Richter transformation (to DLBCL — 5–10%)==.
- Genetics : ==del 13q14 (most common — good, miR-15/16)==, ==trisomy 12== (intermediate), ==del 11q (ATM — poor)==, ==del 17p / TP53 (worst)==; ==unmutated IGHV (ZAP-70 +, CD38 +) — poor==.
- Staging : ==Rai (0–IV), Binet (A–C)==; ibrutinib (BTK), venetoclax (BCL-2).

| ==Mycosis fungoides / Sézary syndrome== | ==CD4 + skin T-cell lymphoma==; ==Pautrier microabscesses (epidermis)==; ==cerebriform nuclei==; ==Sézary : erythroderma + leukemic phase (Sézary cells)== |
| Large granular lymphocytic leukemia | CD8 / NK; ==neutropenia, RA (Felty-like), pure red cell aplasia==; STAT3 |

## Plasma Cell Neoplasms

### Multiple myeloma :

- ==Malignant proliferation of plasma cells in marrow== with ==monoclonal Ig (M-protein) — IgG (55%) > IgA (25%) > light chain only (Bence Jones)==; ==> 60 y, men, African descent==.
- Pathogenesis : ==IL-6 (growth factor)==; ==translocations to IgH (14q32) — t(11;14) cyclin D1 (most common), t(4;14) FGFR3/MMSET, t(14;16) MAF==; del 17p (poor); hyperdiploidy.
- ==RANKL ↑, DKK1 (inhibits osteoblasts) ↑== → ==lytic bone lesions ("punched-out" — skull, vertebrae, ribs)==, pathological fractures, hypercalcemia; ==bone scan negative (no osteoblastic activity) — use skeletal survey / PET-CT / MRI==; ==ALP normal==.

:::mnemo Myeloma — "CRAB" (+ SLiM)
==C==alcium ↑ · ==R==enal failure · ==A==nemia (normocytic) · ==B==one lesions (lytic) — plus ==S==ixty % marrow plasma cells, ==Li==ght chain ratio ≥ 100, ==M==RI > 1 focal lesion (myeloma-defining events).
:::

- Diagnosis : ==clonal marrow plasma cells ≥ 10% + CRAB / SLiM==; ==M-spike on serum protein electrophoresis (gamma region)==, ==serum immunofixation==, ==serum free light chain ratio==, ==24-h urine (Bence Jones — heat test : precipitates at 40–60 °C, redissolves at 100 °C)==.
- Smear : ==rouleaux formation (↑ ESR)==; plasma cells (==Mott cells — grape-like Russell bodies; flame cells (IgA); Dutcher bodies (intranuclear)==).
- ==Myeloma kidney (cast nephropathy)== : ==light chain casts with giant-cell reaction in distal tubules==; also ==AL amyloid, hypercalcemia, light-chain deposition disease, Fanconi syndrome==.
- Infections (↓ normal Ig — ==encapsulated bacteria==) — most common cause of death.
- ==β2-microglobulin & albumin — ISS staging==.

| Related disorder | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==MGUS== | ==M-protein < 3 g/dL, marrow plasma cells < 10%, no CRAB==; ==1% per year → myeloma== ; most common plasma cell dyscrasia (> 50 y : 3%) |
| Smoldering myeloma | M-protein ≥ 3 g/dL or plasma cells 10–60%, no CRAB |
| ==Solitary plasmacytoma== | Bone (→ myeloma) or extramedullary (upper respiratory tract — better) |
| ==Waldenström macroglobulinemia (lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma)== | ==IgM M-protein → hyperviscosity (visual disturbance, retinal "sausage" veins, headache, bleeding), cryoglobulinemia, cold agglutinins==; ==MYD88 L265P (> 90%)==; ==lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly==; ==no lytic lesions==; Dutcher bodies |
| ==POEMS syndrome== | ==P==olyneuropathy, ==O==rganomegaly, ==E==ndocrinopathy, ==M==-protein (λ), ==S==kin changes; ==osteosclerotic myeloma==, ↑ VEGF |
| Heavy chain diseases | ==α (IPSID — Mediterranean), γ (Franklin), μ== |
| Primary (AL) amyloidosis | λ light chains — see Amyloidosis |

:::green Rank boosters — leukemias
- ==Most common leukemia : children — ALL; adults (overall/India) — AML (acute), CML (chronic in India), CLL (West)==.
- ==Chloroma (myeloid sarcoma)== — ==green colour due to MPO==; orbit, t(8;21).
- ==Juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia== — ==NF1, PTPN11 (Noonan), RAS==; ↑ HbF, monocytosis.
- ==CMML== — persistent monocytosis ≥ 1000/µL, MDS/MPN overlap.
- ==Transient abnormal myelopoiesis== — Down neonates (GATA1), resolves; 20–30% later AML-M7.
- ==Tumour lysis syndrome== — ↑ K⁺, ↑ PO₄, ↑ uric acid, ↓ Ca²⁺ → AKI; Burkitt, ALL; ==rasburicase, allopurinol==.
- ==Gingival hyperplasia== : AML-M4/M5, phenytoin, cyclosporine, nifedipine.
:::
