# Hematopoiesis & Red Cell Disorders

## Hematopoiesis

| Period | Site |
|---|---|
| ==3rd week – 2 months== | ==Yolk sac== (mesoblastic) |
| ==6 weeks – birth (peak 3rd–6th month)== | ==Liver (main fetal site)==, spleen |
| ==From 5th month onward== | ==Bone marrow== (sole site after birth) |
| Adult | ==Axial skeleton — vertebrae, ribs, sternum, skull, pelvis, proximal femur/humerus== |

- ==Hematopoietic stem cells : CD34 +==; regulation by ==EPO (kidney — peritubular interstitial cells, hypoxia → HIF)==, ==TPO (liver — platelets)==, ==G-CSF, GM-CSF, IL-3, IL-5 (eosinophils), IL-7 (lymphoid)==, ==SCF (KIT ligand)==.
- ==Extramedullary hematopoiesis== : thalassemia, myelofibrosis, marrow infiltration — liver, spleen, paravertebral masses.
- Bone marrow : ==M:E ratio 2:1 – 4:1==; ==biopsy — cellularity (100 − age)%==; ==aspirate — morphology==; ==dry tap — myelofibrosis, hairy cell leukemia, metastases, aplastic anemia==.

### Normal adult values :

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Hemoglobin | Men 13.5–17.5 g/dL; women 12–15.5 g/dL |
| ==MCV== | ==80–100 fL== |
| ==MCH / MCHC== | 27–32 pg / ==32–36 g/dL (↑ only in spherocytosis)== |

## Anemia — Classification & Approach

:::tree
Anemia (by MCV)
  ==Microcytic (< 80 fL)==
    Iron deficiency
    Thalassemia
    Anemia of chronic disease (late)
    Sideroblastic (lead, B6)
  ==Normocytic (80–100 fL)==
    ==↑ Retic== : hemolysis, acute bleed
    ==↓ Retic== : aplastic, ACD, CKD, marrow infiltration
  ==Macrocytic (> 100 fL)==
    ==Megaloblastic== : B12, folate, drugs
    Non-megaloblastic : liver disease, alcohol, hypothyroidism, MDS, reticulocytosis
:::

### Differentiating microcytic anemias :

| Test | ==Iron deficiency== | ==Anemia of chronic disease== | ==β-thalassemia trait== | Sideroblastic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Serum iron | ↓ | ↓ | Normal | ↑ |
| ==TIBC (transferrin)== | ==↑== | ==↓== | Normal | Normal / ↓ |
| Transferrin saturation | ↓ (< 16%) | ↓ / N | Normal | ↑ |
| ==Serum ferritin== | ==↓ (< 15 ng/mL — first to fall, most specific)== | ==↑ / Normal (acute phase)== | Normal | ↑ |
| Marrow iron | ==Absent== | ==↑ in macrophages, ↓ in erythroblasts== | Normal | ==Ringed sideroblasts== |
| ==RDW== | ==↑== | Normal | ==Normal== | ↑ (dimorphic) |
| ==Mentzer index (MCV/RBC count)== | ==> 13== | — | ==< 13== | — |
| ==HbA2== | Normal / ↓ | Normal | ==↑ (> 3.5%)== | Normal |

- ==Soluble transferrin receptor (sTfR)== — ↑ in iron deficiency, normal in ACD.
- ==Free erythrocyte protoporphyrin / ZPP== ↑ in iron deficiency and lead poisoning.

## Iron Deficiency Anemia

- ==Most common anemia worldwide== & in India (NFHS : > 50% women).
- Iron absorption : ==duodenum (DMT1 for Fe²⁺, ferroportin exit)==; regulated by ==hepcidin (↓ ferroportin)==.

Causes :

1. ==Chronic blood loss — most common cause in adults== :
    - GI : ==hookworm (most common in tropics — Ancylostoma)==, peptic ulcer, ==colon carcinoma (elderly men — must exclude)==, hemorrhoids, NSAIDs
    - Genitourinary : ==menorrhagia (women)==
2. ==Increased demand== — pregnancy, infancy, adolescence
3. ==Dietary deficiency== — vegetarian diet (non-heme), infants on cow's milk
4. ==Impaired absorption== — ==celiac disease==, gastrectomy, achlorhydria, ==H. pylori==, PPIs

### Stages & features :

:::hflow
==Storage iron depletion (↓ ferritin)== > ==Iron-deficient erythropoiesis (↓ serum iron, ↑ TIBC)== > ==Microcytic hypochromic anemia==
:::

- Smear : ==microcytic hypochromic RBCs, anisocytosis (↑ RDW), pencil cells (elliptocytes)==, target cells (few); thrombocytosis.
- Clinical : ==koilonychia (spoon nails), pica (pagophagia — ice)==, angular stomatitis, glossitis, ==Plummer–Vinson==, restless legs.
- Response to oral iron : ==reticulocytosis in 5–10 days (peak)==, Hb rises 1 g/dL per week.

## Anemia of Chronic Disease (Inflammation)

- Chronic infections (TB, osteomyelitis), ==autoimmune (RA)==, malignancy, CKD.
- Mechanism : ==IL-6 → ↑ hepcidin → ↓ ferroportin → iron trapped in macrophages + ↓ absorption==; ↓ EPO response; ↓ RBC survival.
- ==Normocytic normochromic (or mildly microcytic)==, ↓ serum iron, ↓ TIBC, ==↑ ferritin==.

## Megaloblastic Anemia

- ==Impaired DNA synthesis (thymidine) with normal RNA/protein synthesis== → ==nuclear–cytoplasmic asynchrony== (nucleus immature, cytoplasm mature).

| Feature | ==Vitamin B12 (cobalamin)== | ==Folate== |
|---|---|---|
| Source | ==Animal products only== (vegans at risk) | Green leafy vegetables, fruits |
| Stores | ==2–5 years (liver)== | ==3–4 months== |
| Absorption | ==Terminal ileum== (with ==intrinsic factor== from parietal cells) | ==Jejunum== |
| Causes | ==Pernicious anemia (most common in West)==, ==gastrectomy==, ==ileal resection / Crohn==, ==Diphyllobothrium latum (fish tapeworm)==, blind loop (bacterial overgrowth), vegan diet, ==metformin, PPIs==, nitrous oxide, TC2 deficiency | ==Poor diet (alcoholics, elderly)==, ==pregnancy==, hemolytic anemia (↑ demand), ==drugs (methotrexate, trimethoprim, phenytoin, pyrimethamine)==, tropical sprue, celiac |
| ==Neurological symptoms== | ==Present — subacute combined degeneration (posterior + lateral columns)==, peripheral neuropathy, dementia | ==Absent== |
| ==Methylmalonic acid== | ==↑== | Normal |
| ==Homocysteine== | ↑ | ↑ |

:::flow Biochemistry
==B12 (methylcobalamin)== : homocysteine → methionine (methionine synthase) + ==N5-methyl THF → THF== (folate trap)
==B12 (adenosylcobalamin)== : methylmalonyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA — defect → ↑ ==MMA== → abnormal myelin (odd-chain fatty acids) → neuropathy
THF → ==dTMP synthesis== (thymidylate synthase) → DNA ; deficiency → megaloblastosis
:::

### Morphology :

- Peripheral smear : ==macro-ovalocytes (oval macrocytes, MCV > 100, often > 110)==, anisopoikilocytosis, ==hypersegmented neutrophils (≥ 5 lobes in > 5% or any 6 lobes) — earliest & most specific sign==, ==pancytopenia==, Howell–Jolly bodies, basophilic stippling, Cabot rings.
- Marrow : ==hypercellular==, ==megaloblasts (large, open "sieve-like" chromatin)==, ==giant metamyelocytes & band forms==, ==ineffective erythropoiesis (intramedullary hemolysis → ↑ LDH, ↑ indirect bilirubin)==.
- Clinical : ==lemon-yellow complexion (pallor + mild jaundice)==, ==beefy red tongue (atrophic glossitis)==.

### Pernicious anemia :

- ==Autoimmune gastritis (body/fundus)== — ==anti-parietal cell (90%, sensitive) and anti-intrinsic factor antibodies (specific)==; type I blocking antibody most common.
- ==Associations : Hashimoto, vitiligo, Addison, T1DM, ↑ gastric carcinoma, carcinoid==; HLA-DR5, ==blood group A==.
- ==Schilling test (historic)== — corrected by IF.

## Hereditary Spherocytosis (HS)

- ==Defects in vertical interactions of membrane skeleton : ankyrin (most common), band 3, spectrin, protein 4.2== → ==loss of membrane → spherocytes== → trapped in ==splenic cords== (cannot deform) → extravascular hemolysis.
- ==AD (75%)==; ==most common inherited hemolytic anemia in Northern Europeans==.
- Features : ==anemia, splenomegaly, jaundice==, ==pigment (bilirubin) gallstones (40–50%)==, ==aplastic crisis (parvovirus B19)==, hemolytic crises.
- Lab : ==spherocytes (no central pallor)==, ==↑ MCHC (only condition with ↑ MCHC)==, ↑ RDW, ==↑ osmotic fragility (incubated)==, ==EMA (eosin-5-maleimide) binding test — ↓ fluorescence (flow cytometry — most sensitive/specific)==, ==negative Coombs (vs AIHA)==.
- Treatment : ==splenectomy== (spherocytes persist, anemia corrected; ==Howell–Jolly bodies appear post-splenectomy==).

## G6PD Deficiency

- ==X-linked recessive==; ==most common enzyme deficiency== (~400 million); protects against ==P. falciparum malaria==.
- ↓ NADPH → ↓ reduced glutathione → ==oxidant damage to Hb → Heinz bodies (denatured Hb precipitates)== → ==bite cells (degmacytes)== removed by splenic macrophages; ==intravascular + extravascular hemolysis==.
- Triggers : ==infections (most common)==, ==drugs (primaquine, dapsone, sulfonamides, nitrofurantoin, rasburicase, chloroquine, methylene blue)==, ==fava beans (favism — Mediterranean variant)==, naphthalene (mothballs), DKA.
- Variants : ==G6PD A− (Africa — mild, self-limited, older RBCs)==, ==G6PD Mediterranean (severe, favism)==.
- Smear : ==bite cells, blister cells, Heinz bodies (supravital stain — crystal violet / brilliant cresyl blue)==.
- ==Enzyme assay falsely normal during acute hemolysis (reticulocytes have high G6PD) — repeat after 2–3 months==; screening — ==fluorescent spot test==.

| Feature | ==G6PD deficiency== | ==Pyruvate kinase deficiency== |
|---|---|---|
| Inheritance | X-linked | AR |
| Pathway | HMP shunt | Glycolysis (↓ ATP) |
| Hemolysis | Episodic (oxidant) | Chronic, extravascular |
| Smear | ==Bite cells, Heinz bodies== | ==Echinocytes (burr / prickle cells)== |
| 2,3-BPG | Normal | ==↑ (better O₂ delivery)== |

## Sickle Cell Disease

- ==Point mutation β-globin (chromosome 11) — glutamic acid → valine at position 6 (β6 Glu→Val)== → ==HbS==.
- ==Deoxygenated HbS polymerizes== → sickling → ==membrane damage, ↑ rigidity, microvascular occlusion==; ==irreversibly sickled cells==.
- Factors ↑ sickling : ==↑ HbS concentration, deoxygenation, acidosis, dehydration (↑ MCHC), ↓ HbF, ↑ transit time (slow flow — spleen, marrow)==. ==HbF inhibits polymerization (hydroxyurea ↑ HbF)==. ==HbC (β6 Glu→Lys)== in HbSC — milder.
- India : ==central India tribal belt (Odisha, Chhattisgarh, MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra)== — ==Arab–Indian haplotype (high HbF — milder)==.

### Clinical features :

1. ==Vaso-occlusive (pain) crises== — bones (==hand-foot syndrome / dactylitis — earliest in infants==), ==acute chest syndrome (most common cause of death in adults)==, priapism, stroke (children), ==avascular necrosis of femoral head==, retinopathy, leg ulcers
2. ==Sequestration crisis== — ==massive splenic pooling in children → shock== (before autosplenectomy)
3. ==Aplastic crisis== — ==parvovirus B19==
4. Hemolytic crisis; ==chronic hemolysis — pigment gallstones==, ↑ bilirubin
5. ==Autosplenectomy (fibrotic, shrunken spleen — Gamna–Gandy bodies) by adolescence== → ==infections by encapsulated organisms (S. pneumoniae, H. influenzae)==; ==Salmonella osteomyelitis==
6. Renal : ==papillary necrosis, hyposthenuria, hematuria==; ==renal medullary carcinoma (trait)==
7. Bone : =="hair-on-end" skull (less than thal), "H-shaped / fish-mouth" vertebrae==, marrow hyperplasia

- Lab : ==sickle cells (ISCs)==, target cells, HJ bodies (asplenia); ==sickling test (sodium metabisulfite — 2%)==, ==solubility test (dithionite)==; ==Hb electrophoresis / HPLC (confirmatory — HbS)==.
- ==Sickle cell trait (HbAS)== — usually asymptomatic; ==hematuria, hyposthenuria, renal medullary carcinoma, splenic infarct at high altitude, exertional rhabdomyolysis==; ==malaria protection==.

## Thalassemia

- ==Reduced or absent synthesis of normal globin chains== → ==imbalance — excess unpaired chains precipitate== → ==ineffective erythropoiesis (marrow) + hemolysis (spleen)==.

### β-thalassemia :

- ==Point mutations (splice site — most common in India : IVS 1-5 G→C)==; β⁰ (no β) / β⁺ (reduced). ==Chromosome 11==.

| Syndrome | Genotype | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==β-thalassemia major (Cooley anemia)== | β⁰/β⁰, β⁰/β⁺, β⁺/β⁺ | ==Severe transfusion-dependent anemia from 6–9 months== (HbF → HbA switch); ==excess α-chains precipitate== → ineffective erythropoiesis; ==marrow expansion — "crew-cut / hair-on-end" skull X-ray, chipmunk facies (frontal bossing, maxillary prominence)==; ==hepatosplenomegaly, extramedullary hematopoiesis==; ==iron overload (transfusions + ↑ absorption) → cardiac failure (most common cause of death), liver, endocrine (DM, hypogonadism)==; ==↑ HbF (up to 90%), ↑ HbA2, HbA absent / ↓== |
| ==β-thalassemia intermedia== | Variable | Not transfusion-dependent; moderate anemia |
| ==β-thalassemia minor (trait)== | β/β⁰ or β/β⁺ | Usually asymptomatic; ==mild microcytic anemia with ↑ RBC count, target cells, basophilic stippling==; ==↑ HbA2 (> 3.5%) — diagnostic==; ==Mentzer < 13==; ==NESTROF screening== ; often misdiagnosed as iron deficiency |

### α-thalassemia :

- ==Gene deletions (most common)==; ==4 α-genes on chromosome 16 (2 per chromosome)==; cis deletion (Asians) — risk of hydrops in offspring; trans (Africans).

| Genes deleted | Syndrome | Features |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Silent carrier | Normal |
| 2 | ==α-thalassemia trait== | Mild microcytic anemia, ==normal HbA2== |
| 3 | ==HbH disease (β4 tetramers)== | Moderate hemolytic anemia; ==HbH inclusions — "golf-ball" (brilliant cresyl blue)== |
| 4 | ==Hydrops fetalis (Hb Barts — γ4)== | ==Intrauterine death==; Hb Barts has very high O₂ affinity |

:::pearl
- ==Smear in thalassemia major : microcytic hypochromic, target cells, nucleated RBCs (normoblasts), basophilic stippling, anisopoikilocytosis==.
- ==HbE (β26 Glu→Lys)== — ==North-East India (Assam), Bengal== ; ==HbE/β-thalassemia== — common cause of thal intermedia/major in eastern India.
- ==Prenatal diagnosis : chorionic villus sampling (10–12 weeks)==; ==HPLC — screening test of choice for hemoglobinopathies==.
- Iron chelators : ==desferrioxamine (SC), deferiprone (oral — agranulocytosis), deferasirox (oral)==; ==T2* MRI== for cardiac iron.
:::

## Immune Hemolytic Anemias

| Feature | ==Warm AIHA== | ==Cold agglutinin disease== | Paroxysmal cold hemoglobinuria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Antibody | ==IgG== (Rh antigens) | ==IgM (anti-I / i)== | ==IgG (Donath–Landsteiner, anti-P) — biphasic== |
| Temperature | 37 °C | ==< 30 °C (cold)== | Binds cold, lyses warm |
| Hemolysis | ==Extravascular (spleen) — spherocytes== | ==Intravascular (complement) + extravascular (liver)==; ==RBC agglutination on smear==, acrocyanosis | Intravascular |
| Causes | ==Idiopathic (60%)==, ==SLE==, ==CLL==, lymphomas, ==drugs (α-methyldopa, penicillin)== | ==Mycoplasma pneumoniae (anti-I), infectious mononucleosis (anti-i)==, ==lymphomas (Waldenström)== | ==Syphilis (historic), viral infections in children== |
| ==Coombs (DAT)== | ==IgG ± C3== | ==C3 only== | C3 |

- ==Direct Coombs test (DAT)== — antibodies / complement ==on patient's RBCs== (AIHA, HDN, transfusion reaction). ==Indirect Coombs (IAT)== — antibodies ==in patient's serum== (cross-matching, antenatal screening).
- ==Drug-induced== : ==hapten (penicillin, cephalosporins)==, ==immune complex (quinidine)==, ==autoantibody (α-methyldopa — true autoimmune)==.
- ==Hemolytic disease of newborn== : ==Rh (anti-D, IgG) — 2nd pregnancy; severe; anti-D prophylaxis at 28 weeks & within 72 h postpartum==; ==ABO (O mother, A/B baby) — 1st pregnancy, mild, more common==; ==kernicterus, hydrops fetalis (Rh)==.

## Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria (PNH)

- ==Acquired mutation of PIGA gene (X-linked) in a hematopoietic stem cell== → ==deficiency of GPI-anchored proteins : CD55 (DAF), CD59 (MIRL — most important), C8-binding protein== → ==complement-mediated intravascular hemolysis==.
- ==Triad : hemolytic anemia + pancytopenia + venous thrombosis (hepatic — Budd–Chiari, portal, cerebral — most common cause of death)==.
- Nocturnal hemoglobinuria (mild respiratory acidosis during sleep); ==iron deficiency (hemosiderinuria)==.
- Associations : ==aplastic anemia==, ==MDS, AML==.
- Diagnosis : ==flow cytometry — absent CD55, CD59 on RBCs; FLAER (fluorescent aerolysin) on WBCs — gold standard==; ==Ham (acid serum) test & sucrose lysis test — historic==.
- Treatment : ==eculizumab (anti-C5)== — ↑ meningococcal risk (vaccinate).

## Aplastic Anemia

- ==Pancytopenia with hypocellular (fatty) marrow (< 25% cellularity)==, ==no splenomegaly==, no abnormal cells.

Causes :

1. ==Idiopathic (most common — ~65%)== — ==autoimmune T-cell (IFN-γ, TNF) destruction of stem cells==; telomerase mutations
2. ==Drugs== — ==dose-related : alkylating agents, antimetabolites, benzene==; ==idiosyncratic : chloramphenicol (most notorious), phenylbutazone, carbamazepine, gold, sulfonamides, felbamate==
3. ==Viral — seronegative hepatitis (non-A to E)==, EBV, HIV, ==parvovirus B19 (pure red cell aplasia)==
4. ==Whole-body radiation==
5. ==Inherited — Fanconi anemia (DNA crosslink repair — short stature, absent radii/thumbs, café-au-lait, ↑ chromosomal breakage with diepoxybutane / mitomycin C)==, dyskeratosis congenita (telomerase), ==Shwachman–Diamond==
6. ==PNH==, pregnancy

- Treatment : ==HSCT (young)==, immunosuppression (ATG + cyclosporine + eltrombopag).

| Feature | Aplastic anemia | Pure red cell aplasia |
|---|---|---|
| Lineages | ==All (pancytopenia)== | ==Erythroid only== |
| Causes | As above | ==Thymoma==, ==parvovirus B19 (giant pronormoblasts)==, ==Diamond–Blackfan (congenital — RPS19, macrocytosis, thumb anomalies, ↑ eADA)==, drugs, large granular lymphocytic leukemia |

## Myelodysplastic Syndromes (MDS)

- ==Clonal stem-cell disorders with ineffective hematopoiesis (cytopenias), dysplasia & risk of AML==; ==elderly (> 70 y)==; ==therapy-related (alkylating agents — 5–10 years; 5q−/7q−)==.
- ==Hypercellular marrow with peripheral cytopenias==; blasts < 20%.
- ==Dysplastic features== : ==ringed sideroblasts (≥ 15%), megaloblastoid erythroid precursors, nuclear budding==; ==pseudo-Pelger–Huët neutrophils (bilobed "pince-nez"), hypogranular neutrophils==; ==micromegakaryocytes (monolobed / small nuclei)==.
- ==5q− syndrome== : ==elderly women, macrocytic anemia, normal/↑ platelets, hypolobated megakaryocytes, good prognosis — lenalidomide responsive==.
- Genetics : ==SF3B1 (ring sideroblasts — good)==, TP53 (poor), TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A. Prognosis : ==IPSS-R==.

:::green Rank boosters — RBC
- ==Sideroblastic anemia== : ==ringed sideroblasts (iron-laden mitochondria around nucleus; Prussian blue)== — ==X-linked (ALAS2 — B6 responsive)==, ==alcohol, lead, isoniazid (B6), copper deficiency (zinc excess)==, MDS.
- ==Lead poisoning== : ==coarse basophilic stippling (ribosomal RNA — inhibition of pyrimidine 5′-nucleotidase)==, ↑ ZPP.
- ==Hereditary elliptocytosis== — ==horizontal (spectrin dimer–dimer) interactions==; ==Southeast Asian ovalocytosis== (band 3, malaria protection).
- ==Hereditary stomatocytosis== — mouth-like pallor; ==Rh-null==.
- ==Anemia of CKD== : ↓ EPO; normocytic, burr cells.
- ==Polycythemia (relative vs absolute)== : secondary — ==high altitude, COPD, cyanotic CHD, smokers (↑ COHb), EPO tumours (RCC, HCC, cerebellar hemangioblastoma, leiomyoma)==; primary — PV (low EPO).
- ==Reticulocyte stain : new methylene blue / brilliant cresyl blue (supravital)==.
:::
