# Cardiovascular System

## Blood Vessels — Atherosclerosis

- Disease of ==large and medium-sized elastic and muscular arteries== characterized by ==intimal lesions (atheromas / fibrofatty plaques)==.
- Most commonly involved vessels (descending order) : ==abdominal aorta (infrarenal) > coronary arteries > popliteal arteries > internal carotid arteries > circle of Willis==. ==Upper limb, renal, mesenteric== arteries usually spared (except at ostia).

### Risk factors :

Non-modifiable (constitutional) :

1. ==Age== (40–60 years — increasing incidence)
2. ==Male gender== (premenopausal women protected)
3. ==Genetics / family history== (most important independent risk factor; familial hypercholesterolemia)

Modifiable (major) :

1. ==Hyperlipidemia== (↑ LDL, ↓ HDL) — ==most important modifiable==
2. ==Hypertension==
3. ==Cigarette smoking==
4. ==Diabetes mellitus== (↑ MI 2×, ==↑ gangrene of legs 100×==)
5. Inflammation — ==↑ CRP (hs-CRP)==

Additional :

1. ==Hyperhomocysteinemia==
2. Metabolic syndrome, obesity
3. ==Lipoprotein(a)== (altered apo B with apo(a))
4. Factors affecting hemostasis (↑ PAI-1, fibrinogen)
5. Lack of exercise, stress (type A personality)

### Pathogenesis — "response to injury" hypothesis :

:::flow
==Chronic endothelial injury / dysfunction== (hyperlipidemia, HTN, smoking, turbulence — branch points) → ↑ permeability, leukocyte adhesion (VCAM-1)
==LDL accumulates in intima → oxidized LDL==
==Monocytes adhere, migrate → macrophages ingest oxLDL via scavenger receptors (CD36, SR-A) → foam cells==
==Fatty streak== (earliest lesion) ; inflammasome (cholesterol crystals) → IL-1
Platelet adhesion + cytokines (==PDGF, FGF, TGF-β==) → ==SMC migration from media to intima & proliferation== + ECM (collagen)
==Fibrofatty atheroma== : fibrous cap + necrotic core
:::

### Morphology :

| Lesion | Features |
|---|---|
| ==Fatty streak== | ==Earliest lesion==; flat yellow spots; ==lipid-filled foam cells==; seen in ==children < 1 year== (aorta); not raised, no flow disturbance |
| ==Atheroma (fibrofatty plaque)== | Raised white–yellow plaque; ==superficial fibrous cap (SMC, collagen)==; shoulder (macrophages, T cells, SMC); ==necrotic core (lipid, cholesterol clefts, foam cells, debris)==; neovascularization |
| ==Complicated plaque== | ==Rupture / ulceration / erosion → thrombosis==; ==hemorrhage into plaque==; ==atheroembolism==; ==aneurysm== (media atrophy); ==calcification== |

- ==Vulnerable (unstable) plaque== : ==thin fibrous cap, large lipid core, many inflammatory cells (macrophages — MMPs)== → rupture → acute coronary syndrome. ==Stable plaque== : thick cap, small core.

:::pearl
- Hallmark cell of atherosclerosis : ==foam cell (macrophage)==; key cell for plaque growth : ==smooth muscle cell==.
- ==Statins== stabilize plaque (↓ inflammation, ↓ LDL) in addition to lipid lowering.
- ==Mönckeberg medial calcific sclerosis== — calcification of ==media== of muscular arteries, ==does not narrow lumen==, > 50 years; ==radiologic "pipe-stem"==.
:::

## Arteriolosclerosis & Hypertension

| Type | Associated with | Morphology |
|---|---|---|
| ==Hyaline arteriolosclerosis== | ==Benign (essential) hypertension==, ==diabetes==, aging | ==Homogeneous pink hyaline thickening== of arteriolar walls (plasma protein leakage + ↑ ECM) → ==narrowed lumen== → ==benign nephrosclerosis (granular contracted kidney)== |
| ==Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis== | ==Malignant hypertension (> 180/120 mmHg)== | =="Onion-skin" concentric laminated thickening== (SMC + reduplicated basement membrane) + ==fibrinoid necrosis (necrotizing arteriolitis)== — "==flea-bitten kidney==" (petechiae) |

### Secondary causes of hypertension :

1. ==Renal== — ==renal artery stenosis (atherosclerosis in elderly, fibromuscular dysplasia in young women — "string of beads")==, GN, CKD, PKD, renin-producing tumours
2. ==Endocrine== — ==primary aldosteronism (Conn — most common endocrine cause)==, Cushing, ==pheochromocytoma==, acromegaly, thyroid disease, OCPs
3. ==Cardiovascular== — ==coarctation of aorta== (upper limb HTN, ==rib notching==)
4. Neurologic — raised ICP, sleep apnea
5. Pregnancy — preeclampsia

### Hypertensive heart disease :

- ==Systemic (left-sided)== : ==concentric LVH== (wall > 1.5–2 cm) without other cause + history/evidence of HTN; myocyte hypertrophy (==boxcar nuclei==), interstitial fibrosis → diastolic dysfunction, AF, CHF.
- ==Pulmonary (right-sided) — cor pulmonale== : RVH due to pulmonary hypertension from lung disease (==COPD — most common==, ILD, PE, kyphoscoliosis, OSA).

## Aneurysms & Dissection

| Type | Cause / features |
|---|---|
| ==Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA)== | ==Atherosclerosis==; ==infrarenal, above bifurcation==; > 50 y, men, smokers; ==rupture risk ↑ when > 5 cm== (surgery at 5.5 cm); ==inflammatory AAA== (IgG4) |
| ==Thoracic (ascending) aortic aneurysm== | ==Hypertension==, ==Marfan, Loeys–Dietz==, bicuspid valve, ==tertiary syphilis (tree-bark intima, obliterative endarteritis of vasa vasorum)== |
| ==Berry (saccular) aneurysm== | ==Circle of Willis== (anterior communicating artery most common); ==ADPKD==, Ehlers–Danlos, coarctation; rupture → ==subarachnoid hemorrhage== |
| ==Charcot–Bouchard (micro)aneurysm== | ==Small penetrating arteries (lenticulostriate)== — ==hypertension== → intracerebral hemorrhage (basal ganglia) |
| ==Mycotic aneurysm== | ==Infection of the wall== (septic emboli in IE, Salmonella) |
| Pseudoaneurysm (false) | Breach in wall with extravascular hematoma communicating with lumen (post-MI ventricular rupture, catheterization) |
| ==Kawasaki== | ==Coronary artery aneurysm== in children |

### Aortic dissection :

- Blood enters ==media== via intimal tear → separates layers.
- Causes : ==hypertension (> 90% in men 40–60 y)==; young — ==Marfan, Ehlers–Danlos==, pregnancy, iatrogenic.
- Underlying lesion : ==cystic medial degeneration (mucoid degeneration)== — fragmentation of elastic tissue + pooling of ground substance (no inflammation).
- Classification : ==Stanford type A (proximal — ascending ± descending; more common, more dangerous, surgery)== ; ==type B (distal — beyond subclavian; medical management)==. DeBakey I, II (ascending), III (descending).
- Presentation : ==sudden excruciating tearing chest pain radiating to back==, pulse deficit, ==widened mediastinum==; death from ==rupture into pericardium (tamponade)==, pleural or peritoneal cavity.

## Ischemic Heart Disease

- ==> 90% due to coronary atherosclerosis==; ==LAD (40–50%) > RCA (30–40%) > LCX (15–20%)==.

| Territory | Infarct region |
|---|---|
| ==LAD== | ==Anterior wall of LV, anterior 2/3 of interventricular septum, apex== — "==widow maker==" |
| ==RCA== | ==Posterior (inferior) wall of LV, posterior 1/3 septum, RV free wall==; ==SA node (60%), AV node (90%)== — bradyarrhythmias |
| ==LCX== | ==Lateral wall of LV== |

### Myocardial infarction — sequence of changes :

| Time | Gross | Light microscopy | EM / other |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–30 min | None | None | ==Reversible== — relaxation of myofibrils, glycogen loss, mitochondrial swelling |
| 30 min–4 h | None | Usually none; ==waviness of fibres at border== | ==Irreversible== — sarcolemmal disruption, mitochondrial amorphous densities |
| 4–12 h | Occasionally dark mottling | ==Early coagulation necrosis==, edema, hemorrhage | ==TTC stain : unstained (pale) infarct== |
| 12–24 h | Dark mottling | Ongoing coagulation necrosis; ==pyknosis, hypereosinophilia==; ==marginal contraction band necrosis==; ==early neutrophilic infiltrate== | |
| ==1–3 days== | Mottling with yellow-tan center | ==Coagulation necrosis with loss of nuclei and striations; brisk neutrophilic infiltrate== | ==Fibrinous pericarditis (day 2–3)== |
| ==3–7 days== | Hyperemic border, central yellow-tan softening | ==Beginning disintegration of dead fibres, dying neutrophils, early macrophage phagocytosis== at border | ==Highest risk of free-wall rupture (3–7 days)== |
| ==7–10 days== | ==Maximally yellow-tan and soft==, depressed red-tan margins | Well-developed phagocytosis; ==early granulation tissue at margins== | |
| 10–14 days | Red-grey depressed borders | Well-established granulation tissue, new vessels, collagen | |
| 2–8 weeks | Grey-white scar, progressive from border to core | ↑ collagen, ↓ cellularity | ==Dressler syndrome (2–10 weeks — autoimmune pericarditis)== |
| > 2 months | ==Scarring complete== | ==Dense collagenous scar== | ==Ventricular aneurysm== |

### Cardiac biomarkers :

| Marker | Rises | Peaks | Returns to normal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ==Troponin I / T (cTnI, cTnT)== | ==2–4 h== (hs-Tn 1 h) | 24–48 h | ==7–10 days (I), 10–14 days (T)== | ==Most sensitive & specific==; diagnosis of late MI |
| ==CK-MB== | 2–4 h | 24–48 h | ==48–72 h== | ==Best for re-infarction== |
| ==Myoglobin== | ==1–2 h (earliest)== | 6–12 h | 24 h | Not specific |
| LDH-1 (flipped LDH 1>2) | 24 h | 3–6 days | 8–14 days | Obsolete |

### Complications of MI :

1. ==Arrhythmias== — ==most common cause of death in first hours (VF)==; ==most common complication==
2. ==Contractile dysfunction / LV failure, cardiogenic shock== (infarct > 40% of LV)
3. ==Myocardial rupture (3–7 days)== :
    - ==Free wall rupture → hemopericardium, cardiac tamponade== (most common rupture; elderly women, first MI, no LVH)
    - ==Interventricular septal rupture → left-to-right shunt==
    - ==Papillary muscle rupture → acute mitral regurgitation== (posteromedial — RCA)
4. ==Pericarditis== — fibrinous (2–3 days); Dressler (weeks)
5. ==Right ventricular infarction== (with inferior MI) — hypotension, clear lungs, ↑ JVP
6. ==Infarct expansion, mural thrombus → systemic embolism==
7. ==Ventricular aneurysm== (late) — ==persistent ST elevation==
8. ==Papillary muscle dysfunction==, progressive late heart failure

:::green Rank boosters — MI & reperfusion
- ==Subendocardial infarct== — inner 1/3–1/2 of wall; ==NSTEMI==; ==hypotension, diffuse coronary disease==. ==Transmural== — STEMI.
- ==Reperfusion injury== : ==contraction band necrosis== (Ca²⁺ influx), ==hemorrhagic infarct==, ==myocardial stunning (reversible dysfunction for days)==, reperfusion arrhythmias, ==microvascular obstruction (no-reflow)==.
- ==Hibernating myocardium== — chronic ischemia, reduced function, recovers after revascularization.
- ==Sudden cardiac death== : ==most common cause — coronary atherosclerosis (lethal arrhythmia)==; young — ==HOCM==, myocarditis, long QT, Brugada, ARVC, anomalous coronary.
- ==Earliest histological change of MI on light microscopy : wavy fibres== (within hours); ==earliest EM change : glycogen depletion / mitochondrial swelling==.
- ==Takotsubo (stress) cardiomyopathy== — apical ballooning, catecholamines, women.
:::

## Rheumatic Fever & Rheumatic Heart Disease

- Acute immune-mediated multisystem disease following ==group A β-hemolytic streptococcal pharyngitis== (==not skin infections==) after ==2–3 weeks==; ==5–15 years==.
- Mechanism : ==molecular mimicry — antibodies & CD4+ T cells against streptococcal M protein cross-react with cardiac myosin, laminin, valve glycoproteins== (type II + type IV hypersensitivity).

### Jones criteria (revised 2015) :

| Major (J♥NES) | Minor |
|---|---|
| ==J==oints — migratory polyarthritis (large joints) — most common | Fever (≥ 38.5 °C) |
| ==♥ Carditis (pancarditis)== — clinical / subclinical (echo) | Arthralgia |
| ==N==odules (subcutaneous) — painless, extensor surfaces | ==↑ ESR (≥ 60) / CRP (≥ 3)== |
| ==E==rythema marginatum — trunk, evanescent | ==Prolonged PR interval== |
| ==S==ydenham chorea — late, may be isolated | |

- Diagnosis : evidence of preceding GAS infection (==↑ ASO, anti-DNase B==, throat culture) + ==2 major or 1 major + 2 minor==.

### Morphology :

- ==Aschoff body== (pathognomonic) : ==foci of fibrinoid necrosis surrounded by T lymphocytes, plasma cells, and plump macrophages — Anitschkow cells== (==caterpillar cells / owl-eye nuclei== — chromatin condensed into central wavy ribbon); multinucleate = ==Aschoff giant cells==. Found in ==any layer of heart (pancarditis)==.
- ==Pericardium== : ==fibrinous / serofibrinous "bread-and-butter" pericarditis==.
- ==Myocardium== : ==myocarditis — interstitial Aschoff bodies== → cardiac dilation, ==most common cause of death in acute RF==.
- ==Endocardium & valves== : ==verrucous vegetations== — small (1–2 mm), warty, ==along lines of closure==; ==MacCallum plaques== — subendocardial lesions in ==left atrium== (jet lesions).
- ==Chronic RHD== : organization → ==fibrous thickening, commissural fusion, shortening & thickening of chordae== → ==fish-mouth / buttonhole stenosis==; neovascularization; ==mitral (65–70%) alone > mitral + aortic (25%)== ; ==tricuspid less, pulmonary rarely==.

:::pearl
- ==Mitral stenosis — most common valvular lesion in RHD; virtually always rheumatic==.
- MS → ==LA dilation → AF, mural thrombi (left atrial appendage) → embolism==, pulmonary venous HTN → ==brown induration==, RVH, ==hemoptysis, hoarseness (Ortner — recurrent laryngeal compression)==.
- ==Anitschkow cells = activated macrophages==; ==Aschoff bodies are replaced by scar in chronic RHD==.
:::

## Infective Endocarditis (IE)

| Feature | ==Acute IE== | ==Subacute IE== |
|---|---|---|
| Organism | ==Staphylococcus aureus== (highly virulent) | ==Viridans streptococci (S. sanguinis, mutans)== (low virulence) |
| Valve | ==Previously normal valves== | ==Previously damaged valves (RHD, congenital, MVP)== |
| Course | Destructive, rapid (days), high mortality | Insidious (weeks–months); recovery after antibiotics |
| Vegetation | Large, ==destructive (ring abscess, perforation)== | Smaller, less destructive |

Other organisms :

1. ==IV drug users== — ==S. aureus, tricuspid valve==, also Pseudomonas, Candida
2. ==Prosthetic valve== — ==Staph. epidermidis (CoNS)== (early), streptococci (late)
3. ==Streptococcus gallolyticus (bovis)== → ==colon carcinoma== — colonoscopy mandatory
4. ==HACEK== group (Haemophilus, Aggregatibacter, Cardiobacterium, Eikenella, Kingella) — ==culture-negative IE==; also Coxiella, Bartonella, Brucella, fungi
5. Enterococci — elderly men after GU procedures

### Morphology & complications :

- ==Friable, bulky, destructive vegetations== (fibrin, inflammatory cells, ==bacteria==) on ==atrial surface of AV valves / ventricular surface of semilunar valves==; ==mitral > aortic==.
- Cardiac : ==valvular insufficiency (perforation), ring abscess==, suppurative pericarditis, conduction defects.
- Embolic : ==septic infarcts (brain, spleen, kidney), mycotic aneurysms==.
- Immune complex : ==focal segmental (embolic) GN — "flea-bitten kidney"==; ==Roth spots (retina), Osler nodes (painful, finger pads), Janeway lesions (painless, palms/soles — microemboli)==, splinter hemorrhages; clubbing, splenomegaly.
- ==Modified Duke criteria== : major — ==positive blood cultures (typical organism, 2 separate)==, ==echo evidence of endocardial involvement==; minor — predisposition, fever, vascular & immunologic phenomena.

### Non-infective vegetations :

| Type | Features | Associated with |
|---|---|---|
| ==Rheumatic (verrucous)== | ==Small, warty, along lines of closure==, non-destructive | Acute RF |
| ==Infective== | ==Large, irregular, friable, destructive==; bacteria | IE |
| ==NBTE (marantic)== | ==Small, sterile, bland, loosely attached== along lines of closure; ==no inflammation== | ==Hypercoagulable states, mucin-secreting adenocarcinoma (pancreas)==, chronic debilitating illness; ==embolize== |
| ==Libman–Sacks (SLE)== | ==Small–medium, sterile, on both surfaces (undersurface) of valves==, chordae; fibrinoid necrosis | ==SLE==, APS |
| Carcinoid heart disease | ==Fibrous plaques on endocardium — right heart (tricuspid, pulmonary)== | Serotonin from ==liver metastases of carcinoid== (inactivated in lung) |

## Congenital Heart Disease

| Category | Lesions |
|---|---|
| ==Left-to-right shunts (acyanotic, late cyanosis)== | ==VSD (most common CHD overall)==, ==ASD (most common in adults; secundum type)==, ==PDA==, AV septal defect (Down) |
| ==Right-to-left shunts (cyanotic — early)== | "==5 Ts==" : ==Tetralogy of Fallot (most common cyanotic)==, ==Transposition of great arteries (most common cyanotic at birth)==, ==Truncus arteriosus==, ==Tricuspid atresia==, ==TAPVC== |
| Obstructive | ==Coarctation of aorta==, pulmonary stenosis, aortic stenosis/atresia |

- ==Tetralogy of Fallot== : ==VSD + overriding aorta + RV outflow (pulmonary) obstruction (determines severity) + RVH== → ==boot-shaped heart (coeur en sabot)==, ==tet spells (squatting ↑ SVR)==.
