# Lymph Nodes, Lymphomas, Spleen & Thymus

## Lymph Node — Structure & Reactive Patterns

| Compartment | Cells | Reactive pattern & causes |
|---|---|---|
| ==Cortex — follicles (germinal centres)== | ==B cells==, follicular dendritic cells, tingible-body macrophages | ==Follicular hyperplasia== : ==RA, toxoplasmosis, early HIV, syphilis== |
| ==Paracortex== | ==T cells==, dendritic cells (high endothelial venules) | ==Paracortical hyperplasia== : ==viral (EBV — infectious mononucleosis), drugs (phenytoin), post-vaccination== |
| ==Medulla / sinuses== | Macrophages (histiocytes), plasma cells | ==Sinus histiocytosis== : ==nodes draining cancers==, ==Rosai–Dorfman (emperipolesis, S100 +)== |

### Reactive follicular hyperplasia vs follicular lymphoma :

| Feature | ==Reactive hyperplasia== | ==Follicular lymphoma== |
|---|---|---|
| Follicles | ==Varying size & shape, in cortex== | ==Uniform, back-to-back, throughout node== |
| Mantle zone | ==Well-formed== | Attenuated / absent |
| Germinal centre | ==Polarized (dark & light zones), tingible-body macrophages, many mitoses== | ==No polarity, no tingible-body macrophages, few mitoses== |
| ==BCL-2 in germinal centre== | ==Negative== | ==Positive (t(14;18))== |
| Clonality | Polyclonal (κ & λ) | ==Monoclonal (light-chain restriction)== |

### Lymphadenitis — specific :

| Type | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Acute nonspecific== | Pyogenic — swollen, tender; neutrophils in follicles; abscess |
| ==Tuberculous== | ==Caseating granulomas==; ==cervical (scrofula)==; matted nodes, sinuses, ==collar-stud abscess== |
| ==Sarcoidosis== | ==Non-caseating granulomas==, asteroid, Schaumann bodies |
| ==Toxoplasmosis (Piringer–Kuchinka)== | ==Triad : follicular hyperplasia + small clusters of epithelioid histiocytes (encroaching on follicles) + monocytoid B-cell hyperplasia (sinuses)== |
| ==Cat-scratch disease== | ==Bartonella henselae== — ==stellate (necrotizing) granulomas with central neutrophils (suppurative)== |
| ==Kikuchi (histiocytic necrotizing) lymphadenitis== | ==Young Asian women==; ==paracortical necrosis with karyorrhectic debris, no neutrophils==, crescentic histiocytes, plasmacytoid dendritic cells; self-limited; SLE mimic |
| ==Castleman disease== | ==Hyaline-vascular (unicentric — "lollipop" follicles, onion-skin mantle)==; ==plasma cell / multicentric — HHV-8, IL-6 (POEMS)== |
| ==Dermatopathic lymphadenitis== | ==Melanin-laden macrophages + Langerhans cells== — chronic skin disease |
| ==Kimura disease== | ==Young Asian men==, head & neck; ==eosinophils, ↑ IgE==, follicular hyperplasia |
| HIV | Florid follicular hyperplasia → ==follicle lysis== → lymphocyte depletion |

## Lymphoid Neoplasms — WHO Classification

:::tree
Lymphoid neoplasms
  ==Precursor B & T== (lymphoblastic)
    B-ALL / LBL, T-ALL / LBL
  ==Mature B-cell== (85–90%)
    CLL/SLL, follicular, mantle cell, marginal zone, DLBCL, Burkitt, hairy cell, myeloma
  ==Mature T & NK-cell==
    PTCL, ALCL, mycosis fungoides, ATLL, NK/T (nasal)
  ==Hodgkin lymphoma==
    Classical (4 types), NLPHL
:::

### Hodgkin vs Non-Hodgkin lymphoma :

| Feature | ==Hodgkin lymphoma== | ==Non-Hodgkin lymphoma== |
|---|---|---|
| Nodes | ==Localized to single axial group (cervical, mediastinal, para-aortic)== | ==Multiple peripheral nodes== |
| Spread | ==Orderly, contiguous== | ==Non-contiguous== |
| Mesenteric nodes, Waldeyer ring | ==Rarely involved== | ==Commonly involved== |
| Extranodal | ==Uncommon== | ==Common (40%)== |
| Neoplastic cells | ==Rare (< 1–5%) Reed–Sternberg cells in reactive background== | Sheets of neoplastic cells |
| B symptoms | ==Common (fever, night sweats, > 10% weight loss), Pel–Ebstein fever, alcohol-induced pain, pruritus== | Less |
| Age | ==Bimodal (young adults & > 55 y)== | Increasing with age |

## Hodgkin Lymphoma

- ==Reed–Sternberg cell (germinal centre B-cell origin)== : ==large, bilobed / multinucleated ("mirror-image"), owl-eye eosinophilic nucleoli with perinucleolar halo==.
- Immunophenotype (classical) : ==CD15 + , CD30 + , PAX5 (weak) +; CD45 (LCA) −, CD20 − (usually)==.
- ==EBV== — ==mixed cellularity (70%)==, ==lymphocyte-depleted (HIV)==; ==NF-κB activation, JAK2/PD-L1 amplification (9p24)==.

| Type | Frequency | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Nodular sclerosis== | ==Most common (65–70%)==; ==young women (adolescents)== | ==Lacunar cells (RS variant — retraction of cytoplasm)==; ==collagen bands dividing node into nodules==; ==mediastinal, cervical, supraclavicular==; ==EBV − (rarely)== ; excellent prognosis |
| ==Mixed cellularity== | 25%; ==most common in India / developing countries==; men, > 50 y | ==Classic RS cells, mixed background (eosinophils, plasma cells, histiocytes)==; ==EBV + (70%)==; B symptoms; advanced stage |
| ==Lymphocyte-rich== | 5% | Reactive lymphocytes, classic RS; good prognosis |
| ==Lymphocyte-depleted== | < 5% | ==Paucity of lymphocytes, abundant RS / pleomorphic variants==; ==older, HIV, EBV +==; ==worst prognosis== |
| ==Nodular lymphocyte predominant (NLPHL)== | 5% | ==LP cells — "popcorn" (lymphohistiocytic) cells — multilobed nuclei==; ==CD20 +, CD45 +, BCL6 +, CD15 −, CD30 −==; EBV −; ==may transform to DLBCL== ; not "classical" |

:::mnemo Hodgkin types
"==N==odular sclerosis — ==N==umero uno (most common), ==L==acunar" · "==M==ixed cellularity — ==M==ost EBV, ==M==ale" · "==L==ymphocyte ==D==epleted — ==D==eadliest" · "==P==opcorn — ==P==redominant (NLPHL)".
:::

- Staging : ==Ann Arbor (Lugano)== — I (one node region), II (≥ 2 same side of diaphragm), III (both sides), IV (disseminated extranodal); ==A/B symptoms==; ==bulky disease (X)==.
- Treatment : ==ABVD== ± RT; late effects : ==secondary AML, breast cancer (RT), lung cancer, cardiac disease, hypothyroidism==.

## Non-Hodgkin Lymphomas — Key Entities

| Lymphoma | Genetics | Immunophenotype | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Follicular lymphoma== | ==t(14;18) IGH-BCL2== | ==CD10 +, BCL6 +, BCL2 +==, CD20 + | ==Most common indolent NHL in West==; > 50 y; ==painless generalized lymphadenopathy==; ==centrocytes (cleaved) + centroblasts (grade by number)==; incurable but indolent; transforms to DLBCL |
| ==Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)== | ==BCL6 rearrangement (most common), BCL2, MYC== ; ==double-hit (MYC + BCL2) — high-grade== | CD20 +; GCB (CD10+) vs ABC (MUM1+, worse) | ==Most common NHL overall (& in India)==; ==rapidly enlarging mass, often extranodal (GIT, CNS)==; aggressive but curable (==R-CHOP==); ==EBV + in elderly / immunodeficient==; primary mediastinal (thymic B) — young women |
| ==Burkitt lymphoma== | ==t(8;14) IGH-MYC== (also t(2;8), t(8;22)) | ==CD10 +, BCL6 +, BCL2 −, Ki-67 ~100%== | ==Endemic (Africa — jaw/orbit, EBV ~100%)==, ==sporadic (ileocecal, abdomen; EBV 15–20%)==, ==HIV-associated==; ==starry-sky pattern (tingible-body macrophages)==; ==fastest growing human tumour==; ==tumour lysis syndrome==; children & young adults |
| ==Mantle cell lymphoma== | ==t(11;14) IGH-CCND1 (cyclin D1)== | ==CD5 +, CD23 −, cyclin D1 +, SOX11 +== | ==Older men==; generalized lymphadenopathy, splenomegaly, ==GI involvement — lymphomatous polyposis==; ==aggressive, incurable== |
| ==Marginal zone (MALT) lymphoma== | ==t(11;18) API2-MALT1== (H. pylori-resistant), t(14;18) IGH-MALT1 | ==CD5 −, CD10 −, CD20 +== | ==Extranodal sites of chronic inflammation== : ==stomach (H. pylori), salivary (Sjögren), thyroid (Hashimoto), orbit (Chlamydia psittaci), skin (Borrelia), small bowel (Campylobacter — IPSID)==; ==lymphoepithelial lesions==; ==regresses with H. pylori eradication== |
| ==SLL / CLL== | del 13q, +12 | ==CD5 +, CD23 +, CD20 dim== | ==Proliferation centres== |
| ==Lymphoplasmacytic (Waldenström)== | ==MYD88 L265P== | CD20 +, IgM | Hyperviscosity |
| ==Hairy cell leukemia== | ==BRAF V600E== | ==CD11c, CD25, CD103, annexin A1, TRAP== | Splenomegaly, pancytopenia |
| ==Peripheral T-cell lymphoma, NOS== | Heterogeneous | CD3 +, CD4 + often | Aggressive; ==most common T-cell lymphoma== |
| ==Anaplastic large cell lymphoma (ALCL)== | ==t(2;5) NPM-ALK (ALK + — children/young; better prognosis)== | ==CD30 + (strong), ALK + / −== | ==Hallmark cells (horseshoe / kidney-shaped nuclei, perinuclear eosinophilic Golgi)==; ==breast implant-associated ALCL (ALK −)== |
| ==Extranodal NK/T-cell lymphoma (nasal type)== | ==EBV +== | CD56 +, CD3ε (cytoplasmic) | ==Asians==; ==destructive midline facial lesion ("lethal midline granuloma")==, ==angiocentric with necrosis== |
| ==Enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma== | — | CD3 + , CD103 + | ==Celiac disease== — small bowel |
| ==Mycosis fungoides / Sézary== | — | ==CD4 +== | ==Pautrier microabscesses, cerebriform cells== |
| ==Adult T-cell leukemia/lymphoma== | ==HTLV-1 (Tax)== | ==CD4 +, CD25 +== | Hypercalcemia, skin, ==flower cells== |
| Lymphoblastic lymphoma (T) | NOTCH1 | ==TdT +, CD3 +== | ==Adolescent males, mediastinal mass== |

:::mnemo Lymphoma translocations — "8-14 Burkitt, 11-14 Mantle, 14-18 Follicular"
- ==Burkitt — t(8;14) MYC== (8 = "B-for-8 / MYC drives fastest growth")
- ==Mantle — t(11;14) cyclin D1== ("11 players line the mantle")
- ==Follicular — t(14;18) BCL-2== ("18 = adult → indolent lymphoma of adults")
- ==MALT — t(11;18) API2-MALT1==; ==ALCL — t(2;5) NPM-ALK==; ==APL — t(15;17)==; ==CML — t(9;22)==.
:::

### Histiocytic & dendritic cell neoplasms :

- ==Langerhans cell histiocytosis== — ==BRAF V600E (50%)==; ==CD1a, langerin (CD207), S100 +==; ==Birbeck granules (tennis-racket shaped — EM)==; ==coffee-bean (grooved) nuclei==, eosinophils.
    - ==Unifocal (eosinophilic granuloma)== — ==lytic bone lesion (skull), older children/adults==.
    - ==Multifocal unisystem (Hand–Schüller–Christian)== — ==triad : calvarial defects, diabetes insipidus, exophthalmos==.
    - ==Multisystem (Letterer–Siwe)== — ==< 2 years, skin rash (seborrheic), hepatosplenomegaly, lymphadenopathy, pancytopenia== — aggressive.
    - ==Pulmonary LCH — adult smokers==.
- ==Hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (HLH)== — ==fever, cytopenias, splenomegaly, ↑↑ ferritin, ↑ triglycerides, ↓ fibrinogen, ↑ sCD25, hemophagocytosis==; familial (perforin) or secondary (EBV, lymphoma).

## Spleen

### Splenomegaly — causes :

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| ==Massive (> 1000 g / crosses midline)== | ==CML, myelofibrosis, hairy cell leukemia, malaria (chronic — tropical splenomegaly syndrome), kala-azar, Gaucher, thalassemia major==, ==NCPF==, splenic lymphomas |
| Moderate (500–1000 g) | ==Portal HTN (congestive)==, acute leukemia, lymphomas, hemolytic anemias, IM, storage diseases |
| Mild (< 500 g) | ==Acute infections (typhoid, septicemia — "acute splenic tumour"), IE, SLE, RA (Felty)==, CHF |

:::mnemo Massive splenomegaly in India — "Kala-azar, CML, Malaria, Myelofibrosis, Thal"
"==K==ill ==C==ows ==M==ake ==M==ore ==T==roubles" — ==K==ala-azar · ==C==ML · ==M==alaria · ==M==yelofibrosis · ==T==halassemia (+ NCPF, hairy cell, Gaucher).
:::

- ==Hypersplenism== : splenomegaly + ==cytopenias + compensatory marrow hyperplasia== → corrected by splenectomy.
- ==Splenic infarcts== — ==emboli (IE), sickle cell, CML, myelofibrosis== — ==pale, wedge-shaped, subcapsular==.
- ==Gamna–Gandy bodies== — ==congestive splenomegaly, sickle cell== (iron + calcium on fibrous tissue).
- ==Sago spleen (amyloid in follicles) / lardaceous spleen (red pulp)== — amyloidosis.
- ==Spontaneous rupture== — ==infectious mononucleosis, malaria, typhoid==, leukemia.
- ==Post-splenectomy== : ==Howell–Jolly bodies, target cells, Pappenheimer bodies, thrombocytosis, leukocytosis==; ==OPSI (overwhelming post-splenectomy infection — S. pneumoniae, H. influenzae, N. meningitidis) — vaccinate==; ==babesiosis==, malaria severe.
- ==Asplenia / polysplenia== — ==heterotaxy syndromes (Ivemark)==, congenital heart disease.
- ==Accessory spleen (splenunculus)== — 20–35%; hilum — causes relapse after splenectomy for ITP.
- Most common primary splenic tumour : ==hemangioma==; malignant : ==lymphoma (splenic marginal zone — villous lymphocytes)==.

## Thymus

| Lesion | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Thymic hypoplasia / aplasia== | ==DiGeorge syndrome (22q11 — 3rd & 4th pouches)== — T-cell deficiency, hypocalcemia, cardiac defects |
| ==Thymic atrophy== | ==Physiological after puberty (fatty involution)==; stress, steroids, malnutrition, HIV |
| ==Thymic (follicular) hyperplasia== | ==Lymphoid follicles with germinal centres== — ==myasthenia gravis (65–75%)==, Graves, SLE, RA |
| ==Thymoma== | ==Neoplasm of thymic epithelial cells== + non-neoplastic immature T cells; ==> 40 y==; ==anterior mediastinum (most common anterior mediastinal mass in adults)==; ==WHO types A (spindle — best), AB, B1, B2, B3 (worst)==; ==thymic carcinoma (C — SCC, CD5 + , KIT +)== ; invasion (capsule) = most important prognostic factor |
| ==Thymic carcinoma== | Cytologically malignant; ==CD5, CD117 +==; aggressive |

### Thymoma — paraneoplastic associations :

1. ==Myasthenia gravis (30–45% of thymomas; 10–15% of MG patients have thymoma)== — ==anti-AChR==
2. ==Pure red cell aplasia==
3. ==Hypogammaglobulinemia (Good syndrome)==
4. SLE, polymyositis, ==Cushing==, ==paraneoplastic encephalitis==, ==GVHD-like (thymoma-associated multiorgan autoimmunity)==

:::pearl
- ==Anterior mediastinal masses — "4 Ts"== : ==T==hymoma, ==T==eratoma (germ cell), ==T==hyroid (retrosternal goitre), ==T==errible lymphoma (Hodgkin NS, lymphoblastic).
- Middle mediastinum : lymph nodes, bronchogenic cysts, pericardial cysts. Posterior : ==neurogenic tumours (schwannoma, neuroblastoma in children)==, esophageal.
:::
