# Bleeding Disorders & Blood Transfusion

## Approach to Bleeding Disorders

| Feature | ==Platelet / vascular (primary hemostasis)== | ==Coagulation factor (secondary hemostasis)== |
|---|---|---|
| Site | ==Skin & mucous membranes== | ==Deep tissues — joints (hemarthrosis), muscles== |
| Lesions | ==Petechiae, purpura, ecchymoses (small, superficial)==, epistaxis, gum bleeding, menorrhagia | ==Large hematomas, hemarthroses== |
| Bleeding after cuts | Immediate, profuse | ==Delayed, prolonged== |
| Examples | ITP, vWD, Glanzmann, scurvy | Hemophilia, vitamin K deficiency |

### Screening tests :

| Test | Measures | Prolonged in |
|---|---|---|
| ==Platelet count== | Number (150,000–450,000/µL) | Thrombocytopenia |
| ==Bleeding time (BT) / PFA-100== | ==Platelet function + vessel== (2–7 min) | ==ITP, vWD, Glanzmann, Bernard–Soulier, aspirin, uremia== |
| ==Prothrombin time (PT / INR)== | ==Extrinsic + common (VII, X, V, II, fibrinogen)== | ==Warfarin, vitamin K deficiency, liver disease, factor VII deficiency==, DIC |
| ==aPTT== | ==Intrinsic + common (XII, XI, IX, VIII, X, V, II, fibrinogen)== | ==Hemophilia A & B, heparin, vWD (low VIII), lupus anticoagulant==, factor XI / XII deficiency, DIC |
| ==Thrombin time (TT)== | Fibrinogen → fibrin | ==Heparin, hypo/dysfibrinogenemia, FDPs (DIC)==, dabigatran |
| ==Mixing study== | Corrects → ==factor deficiency==; does not correct → ==inhibitor (lupus anticoagulant, factor VIII inhibitor)== | |

:::pearl
- ==Factor XII deficiency → prolonged aPTT but NO bleeding== (↑ thrombosis tendency).
- ==Factor XIII deficiency → normal PT, aPTT, TT== — ==delayed bleeding, umbilical stump bleeding==; ==urea (5 M) clot solubility test==.
- ==Isolated ↑ PT== — factor VII deficiency, early warfarin, early vitamin K deficiency, liver disease (early).
- ==Monitoring : heparin → aPTT (or anti-Xa); warfarin → PT/INR (target 2–3)==.
:::

## Vascular Purpuras

1. ==Infections== — meningococcemia, rickettsiae, IE (vasculitis)
2. ==Drug reactions== — leukocytoclastic vasculitis
3. ==Scurvy, Ehlers–Danlos, Cushing / steroids, senile purpura== — weak vessel support
4. ==Henoch–Schönlein purpura (IgA vasculitis)== — palpable purpura
5. ==Hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia (Osler–Weber–Rendu)== — ==AD, endoglin (ENG), ALK1==; epistaxis, GI bleed, AVMs
6. ==Amyloidosis== — ==pinch purpura, periorbital (raccoon eyes)==

## Thrombocytopenia

Causes :

1. ==Decreased production== — aplastic anemia, leukemia, marrow infiltration, ==megaloblastic anemia==, ==drugs (thiazides, alcohol, chemotherapy)==, viral (HIV, measles), MDS
2. ==Decreased survival (immune)== — ==ITP==, ==drug-induced (heparin, quinine, quinidine, sulfonamides, vancomycin)==, SLE, ==post-transfusion purpura (anti-HPA-1a)==, ==neonatal alloimmune thrombocytopenia==, HIV, CLL
3. ==Decreased survival (non-immune)== — ==DIC, TTP, HUS==, prosthetic valves
4. ==Sequestration== — hypersplenism (cirrhosis)
5. ==Dilutional== — massive transfusion
6. ==Pseudothrombocytopenia== — ==EDTA-induced clumping== (check citrate sample / smear)

### Immune thrombocytopenic purpura (ITP) :

| Feature | ==Acute ITP== | ==Chronic ITP== |
|---|---|---|
| Age | ==Children (2–6 y)== | ==Adults — women 20–40 y== |
| Onset | ==Abrupt, 1–2 weeks after viral infection== | Insidious |
| Course | ==Self-limited (80% resolve in 6 months)== | Chronic, relapsing |
| Associations | Post-viral | ==SLE, HIV, HCV, CLL, H. pylori== |

- ==Antibodies against GpIIb/IIIa or GpIb-IX== → splenic destruction (==spleen — site of antibody production & destruction; normal-sized spleen==).
- ==Isolated thrombocytopenia, large platelets (megathrombocytes)==, ==normal PT/aPTT==; ==marrow — ↑ megakaryocytes (normal or increased)==; diagnosis of exclusion.
- Treatment : steroids, ==IVIG==, anti-D, TPO agonists (eltrombopag, romiplostim), ==rituximab==, splenectomy.

### Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT) :

- ==Type II — immune (IgG against heparin–PF4 complex)== → ==platelet activation → thrombocytopenia + paradoxical thrombosis (venous & arterial)==; ==5–14 days after heparin== (earlier if prior exposure); ==UFH > LMWH==.
- Diagnosis : ==4T score==, anti-PF4 ELISA, ==serotonin release assay (gold standard)==.
- Management : ==stop all heparin; use argatroban / bivalirudin / fondaparinux==; avoid warfarin initially (venous limb gangrene).

### TTP vs HUS :

| Feature | ==TTP== | ==HUS== |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | ==↓ ADAMTS13 (vWF-cleaving protease) — acquired autoantibody (most common) or congenital (Upshaw–Schulman)== → ==ultra-large vWF multimers== → platelet microthrombi | ==Endothelial injury — Shiga-like toxin (E. coli O157:H7) — typical==; ==complement dysregulation (factor H, I, MCP) — atypical== |
| Patients | ==Adult women== | ==Children (bloody diarrhea)== |
| Pentad / triad | ==Pentad : fever, MAHA, thrombocytopenia, neurological symptoms (prominent), renal failure (mild)== | ==Triad : MAHA, thrombocytopenia, acute renal failure (prominent)== |
| Coagulation | ==Normal PT, aPTT== (vs DIC) | Normal |
| Treatment | ==Plasma exchange (plasmapheresis)== + steroids, ==caplacizumab==, rituximab | Supportive (typical); ==eculizumab (atypical)== |

## Qualitative Platelet Disorders

| Disorder | Defect | Lab |
|---|---|---|
| ==Bernard–Soulier syndrome== | ==GpIb-IX-V (vWF receptor) — AR== | ==Giant platelets, thrombocytopenia==, ↑ BT; ==no aggregation with ristocetin (not corrected by normal plasma)== |
| ==Glanzmann thrombasthenia== | ==GpIIb/IIIa (fibrinogen receptor) — AR== | ==Normal count & size==, ↑ BT; ==no aggregation with ADP, collagen, epinephrine, thrombin; normal ristocetin agglutination==; ==absent clot retraction== |
| ==Storage pool disease (δ-granule)== | ==Hermansky–Pudlak (albinism)==, ==Chédiak–Higashi==, Wiskott–Aldrich | ↓ 2nd wave aggregation |
| ==Gray platelet syndrome== | ==α-granule deficiency (NBEAL2)== | ==Large grey (agranular) platelets==, myelofibrosis |
| Acquired | ==Aspirin (irreversible COX-1)==, ==clopidogrel (P2Y12)==, ==uremia==, myeloproliferative neoplasms, cardiopulmonary bypass | |

## Coagulation Factor Disorders

### Von Willebrand disease (vWD) :

- ==Most common inherited bleeding disorder (1%)==; ==mostly AD==; chromosome 12.
- vWF : ==platelet adhesion (GpIb to subendothelial collagen)== + ==carrier / stabilizer of factor VIII==.

| Type | Defect | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| ==Type 1== | ==Quantitative ↓ vWF (mild–moderate)== | ==Most common (70%)== |
| Type 2 (2A, 2B, 2M, 2N) | ==Qualitative==; 2A — loss of large multimers; ==2B — gain of function (↑ GpIb binding → thrombocytopenia)==; 2N — ↓ VIII binding (mimics hemophilia A) | 25% |
| ==Type 3== | ==Severe near-total absence (AR)== — low VIII → ==hemarthroses== | Rare |

- Lab : ==↑ BT (PFA-100), ↑ / normal aPTT (↓ VIII), normal platelet count==, ==↓ vWF antigen, ↓ ristocetin cofactor activity (vWF:RCo)==, ==abnormal ristocetin aggregation — corrected by normal plasma (vs Bernard–Soulier)==.
- Treatment : ==desmopressin (DDAVP — releases vWF from Weibel–Palade bodies; type 1)==, vWF concentrates, ==tranexamic acid==.
- ==Acquired vWD== — ==aortic stenosis (Heyde syndrome)==, LVAD, Wilms tumour, hypothyroidism.

### Hemophilia :

| Feature | ==Hemophilia A== | ==Hemophilia B (Christmas disease)== |
|---|---|---|
| Factor | ==VIII== | ==IX== |
| Inheritance | ==X-linked recessive== (30% new mutations) | X-linked recessive |
| Frequency | ==1 in 5000 males (80–85%)== | 1 in 30,000 |
| Common mutation | ==Intron 22 inversion (severe — 40%)== | Point mutations |

- Severity by factor level : ==severe < 1% (spontaneous hemarthrosis)==, moderate 1–5%, mild 5–40% (bleeding after trauma/surgery).
- Features : ==hemarthrosis (knee most common) → hemophilic arthropathy==, deep muscle hematomas (==iliopsoas — femoral nerve==), ==intracranial hemorrhage (major cause of death)==, ==no petechiae==.
- Lab : ==↑ aPTT (corrected by mixing), normal PT, BT, platelet count==; ==specific factor assay==.
- Complications : ==inhibitor development (alloantibodies — 20–30% in severe A)==; historic HIV/HCV via concentrates.
- Treatment : recombinant factor, ==emicizumab (bispecific — mimics VIIIa; prophylaxis even with inhibitors)==, gene therapy, DDAVP (mild A).

:::mnemo Hemophilia C & others
- ==Hemophilia C — factor XI deficiency — AR, Ashkenazi Jews==, mild bleeding after surgery.
- ==Vitamin K-dependent factors : "1972" — X, IX, VII, II== + protein C & S.
:::

### Vitamin K deficiency & liver disease :

| Condition | PT | aPTT | Platelets | Fibrinogen | Factor VIII |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ==Vitamin K deficiency== | ==↑↑== | ↑ | N | N | ==N== |
| ==Liver disease== | ↑↑ | ↑ | ↓ (hypersplenism) | ↓ (late) | ==N / ↑ (made in endothelium)== |
| ==DIC== | ↑ | ↑ | ==↓== | ==↓== | ==↓== |

- ==Hemorrhagic disease of newborn== : ==classic 2–7 days==; early (< 24 h — maternal drugs : anticonvulsants, warfarin, rifampicin); ==late (2–12 weeks — exclusively breastfed, malabsorption)== — ICH; prevented by ==1 mg IM vitamin K at birth==.

### DIC — lab (summary) :

- ==↓ platelets, ↑ PT, ↑ aPTT, ↓ fibrinogen, ↑ D-dimer & FDPs (most useful), schistocytes==. See Hemodynamics chapter for causes & pathogenesis.

## Thrombophilia — Laboratory

| Test | Detects |
|---|---|
| ==APC resistance (aPTT-based) + PCR== | ==Factor V Leiden== |
| PCR | ==Prothrombin G20210A== |
| Functional assays | ==Protein C, S, antithrombin deficiency== (test when off anticoagulation — ==warfarin ↓ C & S; heparin ↓ AT==) |
| ==dRVVT, LA-sensitive aPTT, anticardiolipin, anti-β2GPI (repeat at 12 weeks)== | ==Antiphospholipid syndrome== |

- ==Warfarin-induced skin necrosis== — ==protein C deficiency (short half-life) → transient hypercoagulable state== in first days; prevented by heparin bridging.

## Blood Groups & Transfusion

### ABO system :

| Group | Antigen on RBC | Antibodies in plasma | Frequency (India) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A | ==Anti-B (IgM)== | ~23% |
| B | B | Anti-A | ~32% (==commonest in North India==) |
| AB | A + B | None — ==universal recipient (RBCs)== | ~8% |
| O | None (H antigen) | ==Anti-A, anti-B (IgG + IgM)== | ==~37% (commonest overall in India)== |

- ==Universal donor of RBCs : O negative==; ==universal plasma donor : AB==.
- ==Bombay phenotype (Oh)== — ==absence of H antigen (FUT1)==, anti-H in serum → ==can receive only Bombay blood==; ==first described in Mumbai (Bhende, 1952)==; ==LAD-2== association.
- ==Rh system== : ==D antigen — most immunogenic after A & B==; ==Rh-negative ~5% in India== (15% Caucasians); anti-D is ==IgG (crosses placenta)== — formed only after exposure.
- ==Naturally occurring antibodies (ABO) = IgM (do not cross placenta)==; immune antibodies = IgG.

### Pre-transfusion testing :

1. ==ABO & Rh grouping== — ==forward (cell) grouping + reverse (serum) grouping==
2. ==Antibody screening== (indirect antiglobulin test with screening cells)
3. ==Cross-matching== — ==major cross-match : donor RBCs + recipient serum (most important)==; minor : donor plasma + recipient cells (not routinely done)
4. ==Type & screen== / electronic cross-match
5. ==Mandatory screening of donated blood (India)== : ==HIV 1 & 2, HBsAg, HCV, syphilis (VDRL/RPR), malaria== — ==NAT testing reduces window period==

### Blood components :

| Component | Storage | Indication / notes |
|---|---|---|
| ==Packed RBCs== | ==2–6 °C, 35 days (CPDA-1), 42 days (SAGM)== | Anemia; 1 unit ↑ Hb by ~1 g/dL |
| ==Platelet concentrate== | ==20–24 °C with continuous agitation, 5 days== | ==Highest risk of bacterial contamination (room temp)==; 1 unit (RDP) ↑ by 5,000–10,000; SDP (apheresis) ↑ 30,000–50,000 |
| ==Fresh frozen plasma (FFP)== | ==−18 to −30 °C (or colder), 1 year== | All coagulation factors; warfarin reversal (if PCC unavailable), DIC, TTP (plasma exchange), liver disease with bleeding |
| ==Cryoprecipitate== | −18 °C, 1 year | ==Fibrinogen (most important — DIC), factor VIII, vWF, factor XIII, fibronectin== |
| Whole blood | 2–6 °C, 35 days | Massive hemorrhage (rarely used) |

- ==Leukoreduction== — ↓ febrile reactions, ↓ CMV transmission, ↓ HLA alloimmunization.
- ==Irradiation (25 Gy)== — ==prevents transfusion-associated GVHD== — immunodeficient recipients, ==blood from first-degree relatives==, HLA-matched platelets, IUT, HSCT.
- ==Washed RBCs== — ==IgA deficiency (anaphylaxis)==, PNH, recurrent allergic reactions.

### Transfusion reactions :

| Reaction | Mechanism | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Febrile non-hemolytic== | ==Cytokines from donor leukocytes / anti-HLA antibodies== | ==Most common reaction==; fever, chills within 1–6 h; prevented by ==leukoreduction== |
| ==Allergic (urticarial)== | Recipient IgE against donor plasma proteins | Urticaria, pruritus; ==antihistamines== |
| ==Anaphylactic== | ==Anti-IgA in IgA-deficient recipient== | Hypotension, bronchospasm within minutes; ==washed products / IgA-deficient donor== |
| ==Acute hemolytic (immediate)== | ==ABO incompatibility (IgM) — clerical error (most common cause)== → complement → ==intravascular hemolysis== | ==Fever, chills, flank/back pain, hypotension, hemoglobinuria, DIC, AKI==; ==DAT +==; stop transfusion immediately |
| ==Delayed hemolytic== | ==Anamnestic IgG response (Kidd — Jk, Rh, Kell, Duffy)== | ==3–10 days== later; ==extravascular hemolysis==, jaundice, ↓ Hb, ==DAT +== |
| ==TRALI== | ==Donor anti-HLA / anti-HNA antibodies (multiparous female donors) → neutrophil activation in recipient lungs== | ==Within 6 h — non-cardiogenic pulmonary edema, hypoxemia, fever, hypotension==; ==leading cause of transfusion-related death== |
| ==TACO== | ==Volume overload== | ==Hypertension, ↑ JVP, cardiogenic edema, ↑ BNP==; elderly, cardiac disease; diuretics |
| ==Transfusion-associated GVHD== | Donor T cells in immunodeficient recipient | ==Fatal (> 90%)==; pancytopenia; prevented by ==irradiation== |
| ==Post-transfusion purpura== | ==Anti-HPA-1a (multiparous women)== | ==Severe thrombocytopenia 7–10 days after transfusion==; IVIG |
| ==Bacterial contamination== | ==Platelets (Staph. epidermidis), RBCs (Yersinia enterocolitica — grows at 4 °C, iron-loving)== | ==Septic shock== |
| ==Massive transfusion== (≥ 10 units / 24 h) | Dilution, citrate, cold | ==Hypocalcemia (citrate toxicity)==, ==hyperkalemia==, hypothermia, dilutional coagulopathy & thrombocytopenia, ==metabolic alkalosis (later)== |

### Transfusion-transmitted infections :

1. ==HIV 1 & 2== (window period : ELISA 3–4 weeks; NAT ~7–11 days)
2. ==Hepatitis B (most common TTI in India), hepatitis C==, HDV
3. ==Syphilis==, ==malaria==
4. CMV (leukocytes — prevented by leukoreduction), HTLV, parvovirus B19, EBV, West Nile, babesiosis, Chagas, variant CJD (prion)

:::green Rank boosters — transfusion
- ==Storage lesion== : ↓ 2,3-BPG, ↑ K⁺, ↓ pH in stored RBCs.
- ==Kell antigen== — most immunogenic after D; anti-Kell — HDN (suppresses erythropoiesis).
- ==Lewis antibodies== — IgM, do not cause HDN (Lewis antigens adsorbed, poorly developed at birth); ==P antigen — receptor for parvovirus B19==; ==Duffy null — resistance to P. vivax==.
- ==Autologous transfusion== — pre-deposit, hemodilution, cell salvage.
- ==Blood bag anticoagulant : CPDA-1 (citrate — anticoagulant, phosphate — buffer, dextrose — energy, adenine — ATP; 35 days)==.
- ==O-negative uncross-matched blood== in emergencies; ==switch to group-specific ASAP==.
:::
