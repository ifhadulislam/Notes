# Endocrine System

## Endocrine Control & Investigations

- ==Negative feedback== (hypothalamus → pituitary → target gland); disease = ==hyperfunction, hypofunction, or mass effect==.
- Investigations : ==basal hormone levels==, ==stimulation tests (for hypofunction — e.g., ACTH stimulation for Addison)==, ==suppression tests (for hyperfunction — dexamethasone suppression for Cushing, glucose suppression for acromegaly)==, imaging, ==FNAC (thyroid)==, autoantibodies.

## Pituitary

### Pituitary adenomas :

- ==Most common cause of hyperpituitarism==; ==30–50 y==; ==microadenoma < 1 cm, macroadenoma ≥ 1 cm==; ==GNAS (GH — gsp), MEN1, AIP (familial)==.
- Mass effects : ==bitemporal hemianopia (optic chiasm)==, headache, hypopituitarism, ==pituitary apoplexy (acute hemorrhage — emergency)==.
- Histology : ==monomorphic cells, loss of acinar architecture (reticulin stain)==; ==PIT-1 (GH, PRL, TSH), TPIT (ACTH), SF-1 (gonadotroph)== lineage transcription factors.

| Adenoma | Frequency | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Prolactinoma (lactotroph)== | ==Most common (30%)== | ==Galactorrhea, amenorrhea, infertility, ↓ libido==; ==psammoma-like calcification==; ==dopamine agonists (cabergoline, bromocriptine)== ; ==stalk effect — ↑ PRL from any mass (loss of dopamine inhibition)== |
| ==Somatotroph (GH)== | 2nd most common | ==Gigantism (before epiphyseal closure), acromegaly (after)==; ↑ IGF-1; ==failure of GH suppression by oral glucose (diagnostic)==; DM, cardiomyopathy, colon polyps; ==densely / sparsely granulated (fibrous bodies — keratin)== |
| ==Corticotroph (ACTH)== | 10–15% | ==Cushing disease==; ==Nelson syndrome (after bilateral adrenalectomy — enlarging adenoma + hyperpigmentation)==; ==Crooke hyaline change in normal corticotrophs (glucocorticoid excess)== |
| Gonadotroph | 10–15% | ==Usually non-functioning== — mass effects in middle-aged |
| Thyrotroph | 1% | Hyperthyroidism with ↑ TSH |
| Null cell | — | Non-functioning |

### Hypopituitarism :

1. ==Non-secretory pituitary adenomas (most common)==
2. ==Sheehan syndrome — postpartum necrosis (enlarged pituitary in pregnancy + hypovolemic shock)== → ==failure of lactation (first sign)==, amenorrhea, hypothyroidism, adrenal insufficiency
3. ==Empty sella syndrome==
4. ==Craniopharyngioma (children)==, other tumours, ==Rathke cleft cyst==
5. Trauma, surgery, radiation, ==apoplexy==
6. Infiltrations — ==sarcoidosis, TB, LCH (diabetes insipidus)==, hemochromatosis, ==lymphocytic hypophysitis (pregnancy)==

- ==Order of loss with compression : GH → FSH/LH → TSH → ACTH → PRL (last)== — "Go Look For The Adenoma Please".

### Posterior pituitary :

| Disorder | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Diabetes insipidus== | ==Central (↓ ADH — trauma, tumours, LCH, idiopathic)== vs ==nephrogenic (resistance — lithium, hypercalcemia, hypokalemia, AVPR2/AQP2 mutations)==; ==polyuria, dilute urine, hypernatremia==; ==water deprivation + desmopressin test== |
| ==SIADH== | ==Small cell lung carcinoma (most common cause)==, CNS lesions, pulmonary infections, ==drugs (carbamazepine, SSRIs, cyclophosphamide, vincristine)==; ==euvolemic hyponatremia, concentrated urine (inappropriately), ↑ urinary Na==; ==fluid restriction, tolvaptan==; avoid rapid correction (ODS) |

- ==Craniopharyngioma== : ==Rathke pouch remnant==; suprasellar; ==bimodal (5–15 y, > 65 y)==; ==adamantinomatous (children — CTNNB1, calcification, "wet keratin", machinery-oil fluid)== & ==papillary (adults — BRAF V600E)==; ==visual defects, DI, growth failure==.

## Thyroid

### Thyroid function :

| Condition | TSH | Free T4 | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Primary hyperthyroidism== | ==↓== | ↑ | Graves, toxic MNG, toxic adenoma |
| ==Primary hypothyroidism== | ==↑ (most sensitive screening test)== | ↓ | Hashimoto, iodine deficiency |
| Secondary hypothyroidism | ↓ / N | ↓ | Pituitary disease |
| Subclinical hypothyroidism | ↑ | N | Early Hashimoto |

### Hyperthyroidism (thyrotoxicosis) — causes :

1. ==Graves disease (most common — 85%)==
2. ==Toxic multinodular goitre (Plummer)== — elderly
3. ==Toxic (functioning) adenoma== — ==TSHR, GNAS mutations==; "hot" nodule
4. ==Thyroiditis (release of stored hormone — subacute, painless, postpartum)== — ↓ radioiodine uptake
5. Exogenous (factitious) — ↓ thyroglobulin, ↓ uptake
6. ==Struma ovarii, hCG (mole, choriocarcinoma)==, TSH adenoma, ==iodine-induced (Jod-Basedow), amiodarone==

### Graves disease :

- ==Autoimmune — TSH receptor-stimulating antibodies (TSI / TRAb — IgG)==; women 20–40 y; ==HLA-DR3, CTLA-4, PTPN22==.
- ==Triad : hyperthyroidism + infiltrative ophthalmopathy (exophthalmos — orbital fibroblasts express TSHR; GAG deposition, lymphocytes) + localized infiltrative dermopathy (pretibial myxedema)== ; ==thyroid acropachy==.
- Morphology : ==diffuse symmetric enlargement, beefy-red==; ==tall columnar hyperplastic follicular cells crowded into papillae (no fibrovascular cores), pale scalloped colloid ("scalloping" at periphery)==, lymphoid infiltrates with germinal centres.
- ==↑ diffuse radioiodine uptake==.

### Hypothyroidism — causes :

1. ==Iodine deficiency (most common worldwide)==
2. ==Hashimoto thyroiditis (most common in iodine-sufficient areas)==
3. Post-ablative (surgery, radioiodine), drugs (==lithium, amiodarone==, antithyroid)
4. ==Congenital — thyroid dysgenesis (most common), dyshormonogenesis (TPO defect, Pendred syndrome — SLC26A4 + deafness)== → ==cretinism (mental retardation, short stature, coarse facies, macroglossia, umbilical hernia)== — ==neonatal TSH screening==
5. Secondary (pituitary), tertiary (hypothalamic)

- ==Myxedema== (adults) : ==non-pitting edema (GAG/hyaluronic acid accumulation)==, cold intolerance, weight gain, bradycardia, ==delayed relaxation of reflexes==, hoarse voice, ==hypercholesterolemia==, ==pericardial effusion==, ==myxedema coma==.

### Thyroiditis :

| Type | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Hashimoto (chronic lymphocytic) thyroiditis== | ==Most common cause of hypothyroidism in iodine-sufficient areas==; ==women 45–65 y==; ==anti-TPO (most sensitive), anti-thyroglobulin==; ==CD8 cytotoxicity, Fas-FasL, Th1 (IFN-γ)==; ==diffuse, painless, firm, rubbery goitre==; ==dense lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate with well-formed germinal centres + Hürthle (oncocytic / Askanazy) cells (eosinophilic granular cytoplasm — mitochondria)==; ==transient hashitoxicosis== ; ==↑ risk of B-cell (MALT) lymphoma==, ==papillary carcinoma (debated)==; ==HLA-DR3, DR5, CTLA-4, PTPN22== ; ==IgG4-related variant (fibrous)== |
| ==Subacute granulomatous (de Quervain) thyroiditis== | ==Post-viral (coxsackie, mumps, adenovirus)==, women 40–50 y; ==painful tender thyroid, fever==, ==↑↑ ESR==; ==transient hyperthyroidism → hypothyroidism → recovery== ; ==↓ radioiodine uptake==; ==granulomatous inflammation with multinucleated giant cells engulfing colloid==; self-limited (6–8 weeks) |
| ==Subacute lymphocytic (painless / silent / postpartum) thyroiditis== | ==Postpartum (within 1 year)== or painless; anti-TPO +; ==lymphocytic infiltrate without germinal centres / Hürthle cells==; transient |
| ==Riedel thyroiditis== | ==Extensive fibrosis extending into adjacent neck structures — "woody / stony hard"==, mimics carcinoma; ==IgG4-related disease== (retroperitoneal fibrosis, sclerosing cholangitis) |
| Acute suppurative | Bacterial; ==pyriform sinus fistula== (left) |

### Goitre :

- ==Diffuse nontoxic (simple) goitre== → ==multinodular goitre== : ==iodine deficiency (endemic — sub-Himalayan belt, > 10% population)==, ==goitrogens (cabbage, cassava, Brassica — thiocyanates)==, dyshormonogenesis.
- Phases : ==hyperplastic (↑ TSH)== → ==colloid involution (colloid goitre)==.
- ==Multinodular goitre== — ==multilobulated asymmetric enlargement (up to 2000 g)==; ==colloid-rich follicles, hemorrhage, fibrosis, calcification, cystic change==; ==retrosternal extension (tracheal compression), toxic MNG (Plummer), incidental carcinoma==.

### Thyroid neoplasms :

- ==Solitary nodule — more likely neoplastic in : young patients, males, solid, "cold" (non-functioning) nodules on scan, history of radiation to neck==. ==Most solitary nodules are benign (> 90%)==.
- ==FNAC — first investigation== (==Bethesda system I–VI==).

| Tumour | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Follicular adenoma== | ==Encapsulated, solitary==; uniform follicles; ==RAS, PAX8-PPARG==; ==capsular & vascular invasion must be excluded (cannot be distinguished from follicular carcinoma on FNAC — Bethesda IV)== ; ==Hürthle cell (oncocytic) adenoma== |
| ==Papillary carcinoma== | ==Most common thyroid cancer (85%)==; ==25–50 y, women==; ==ionizing radiation (children — Chernobyl)==; ==BRAF V600E (most common, 45%), RET/PTC rearrangements (radiation)==; ==diagnosed by NUCLEAR features== : ==ground-glass (Orphan Annie eye) nuclei, intranuclear pseudo-inclusions (cytoplasmic invaginations), nuclear grooves (coffee-bean)==, overlapping; ==papillae with fibrovascular cores, psammoma bodies==; ==lymphatic spread — cervical nodes (often first presentation — "lateral aberrant thyroid")==; ==excellent prognosis (> 95% 10-year)==; variants : ==follicular variant, tall cell (aggressive, BRAF), diffuse sclerosing (young), columnar, hobnail==, ==NIFTP (non-invasive follicular neoplasm with papillary-like nuclear features — no longer "carcinoma")== |
| ==Follicular carcinoma== | 5–15%; ==iodine-deficient areas (↑ relative frequency)==; ==40–60 y==; ==RAS (most common), PAX8-PPARG t(2;3), PIK3CA, PTEN (Cowden)==; ==diagnosis requires capsular and/or vascular invasion (not by FNAC)==; ==hematogenous spread — bones (lytic), lungs, liver==; ==metastases take up radioiodine==; ==minimally invasive vs widely invasive== |
| ==Medullary carcinoma== | 5%; ==parafollicular C cells (neuroendocrine)== ; ==calcitonin (marker), CEA==; ==sporadic (80% — somatic RET)== & ==familial (MEN 2A, 2B, FMTC — germline RET; bilateral, multicentric; preceded by C-cell hyperplasia)==; ==polygonal / spindle cells in nests with amyloid stroma (procalcitonin — Congo red +)==; ==diarrhea (VIP, serotonin, calcitonin)==, Cushing (ACTH); ==prophylactic thyroidectomy in RET carriers== ; ==not radioiodine responsive== |
| ==Anaplastic (undifferentiated) carcinoma== | ==< 5%; elderly (> 65 y)==, iodine-deficient areas; ==TP53 (most), BRAF, RAS, TERT==; arise from differentiated carcinoma (dedifferentiation); ==giant / spindle / squamoid pleomorphic cells==; ==rapidly enlarging bulky neck mass, invasion of trachea, esophagus==; ==almost uniformly fatal (death < 1 year)== |
| ==Primary thyroid lymphoma== | ==Hashimoto background==; ==DLBCL / MALT==; elderly women, rapidly enlarging goitre |

:::mnemo Thyroid carcinoma
- ==Papillary — "P"== : ==P==opular (most common), ==P==alpable nodes, ==P==sammoma, ==P==ositive prognosis, Orphan Annie ("==P==ale" nuclei).
- ==Follicular — "F"== : ==F==ar (hematogenous) spread, ==F==NAC can't diagnose (invasion needed).
- ==Medullary — "M"== : ==M==EN 2, a==M==yloid, calcitonin (==M==arker).
- ==Anaplastic — "A"== : ==A==ged, ==A==ggressive, ==A==ll die.

## Parathyroid

| Disorder | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Primary hyperparathyroidism== | ==Adenoma (85–95% — single, chief cells, rim of compressed normal tissue, ↓ fat)==, ==hyperplasia (5–10% — all four glands; MEN1, MEN2A)==, ==carcinoma (< 1% — invasion, metastasis; HRPT2/CDC73)==; ==most common cause of asymptomatic hypercalcemia (outpatient)==; ==↑ Ca, ↑ PTH, ↓ PO₄, hypercalciuria==; =="stones, bones, abdominal groans, psychic moans"== — ==nephrolithiasis (most common symptom), nephrocalcinosis, osteitis fibrosa cystica (brown tumours), pancreatitis, peptic ulcer, constipation, depression, metastatic calcification==; ==cyclin D1 (PRAD1) rearrangement, MEN1== |
| ==Secondary hyperparathyroidism== | ==CKD (most common)==, vitamin D deficiency, malabsorption → ==↓ Ca== → parathyroid hyperplasia; ==↑ PTH, ↓/N Ca, ↑ PO₄ (CKD)==; renal osteodystrophy, ==calciphylaxis== |
| ==Tertiary hyperparathyroidism== | ==Autonomous hyperfunction after long-standing secondary (post-renal transplant)== — ↑ Ca, ↑ PTH |
| ==Hypoparathyroidism== | ==Surgical removal (thyroidectomy — most common)==, ==autoimmune (APS-1 — AIRE)==, ==DiGeorge==, Mg deficiency; ==tetany (Chvostek, Trousseau), seizures, prolonged QT, basal ganglia calcification, cataract== |
| ==Pseudohypoparathyroidism== | ==End-organ resistance to PTH (GNAS — Gsα)==; ==↑ PTH, ↓ Ca, ↑ PO₄==; ==Albright hereditary osteodystrophy (short stature, short 4th/5th metacarpals, round face, obesity)== (type 1a — maternal) ; ==pseudopseudohypoparathyroidism — AHO phenotype with normal labs (paternal)== |

- ==Hypercalcemia — inpatients : malignancy (PTHrP — most common); outpatients : primary hyperparathyroidism==.

## Endocrine Pancreas — Diabetes Mellitus

### Diagnostic criteria (ADA) :

1. ==Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL==
2. ==2-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL (75 g OGTT)==
3. ==HbA1c ≥ 6.5%==
4. ==Random glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with classic symptoms==

- ==Prediabetes== : FPG 100–125 (IFG), 2-h 140–199 (IGT), HbA1c 5.7–6.4%.

| Feature | ==Type 1 DM== | ==Type 2 DM== |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | ==Autoimmune destruction of β cells (T-cell mediated) → absolute insulin deficiency== | ==Insulin resistance + β-cell dysfunction (relative deficiency)== |
| Onset | ==Childhood / adolescence (< 20 y)== ; LADA in adults | ==Adults (> 40 y), obesity== (increasingly young in India) |
| Body habitus | Normal / thin | ==Obese (central)== |
| Genetics | ==HLA-DR3/DR4, DQ8 (strongest)==, CTLA-4, PTPN22, ==insulin gene VNTR==; ==concordance in twins 30–50%== | ==Polygenic; concordance in identical twins 60–80%== (TCF7L2 — most important) |
| Autoantibodies | ==GAD65, islet cell (ICA), IA-2, insulin, ZnT8== | Absent |
| Islet pathology | ==Insulitis (lymphocytic infiltrate — CD8 T cells)==, ↓ β cells | ==Islet amyloid (amylin — IAPP)==, subtle ↓ β-cell mass |
| Acute complication | ==Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)== | ==Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state (HHS)== |
| Insulin / C-peptide | ==↓↓ / absent== | ↑ early, ↓ late |

- ==Insulin resistance== : ==obesity (FFAs, adipokines — ↓ adiponectin, ↑ resistin, inflammation — TNF, IL-1 inflammasome), ↓ GLUT4 translocation, ↑ serine phosphorylation of IRS-1==; ==acanthosis nigricans==.
- ==MODY (monogenic) — AD; glucokinase (MODY 2 — mild), HNF1α (MODY 3 — most common, sulfonylurea-sensitive)==; neonatal diabetes (KCNJ11).
- ==Gestational DM== — macrosomia, ↑ future T2DM.

### Pathogenesis of complications :

1. ==Advanced glycation end products (AGEs)== → bind RAGE → inflammation, ROS, cross-link collagen (↑ basement membrane thickness)
2. ==Polyol (aldose reductase) pathway== → ==sorbitol accumulation → osmotic damage (lens — cataract; Schwann cells — neuropathy; pericytes — retinopathy)==
3. ==Protein kinase C activation== (diacylglycerol) → VEGF, TGF-β, PAI-1
4. ==Hexosamine pathway== → TGF-β, PAI-1
5. ==Nonenzymatic glycation of Hb (HbA1c)== — reflects 2–3 months control

### Morphology of chronic complications :

| Site | Lesions |
|---|---|
| ==Basement membranes== | ==Diffuse thickening of capillary BM (most consistent morphologic feature)== — diabetic microangiopathy |
| ==Large vessels== | ==Accelerated atherosclerosis — MI (most common cause of death)==, stroke, ==gangrene of lower limbs (100×)== ; ==hyaline arteriolosclerosis== |
| ==Kidney== | ==Diffuse & nodular (Kimmelstiel–Wilson) glomerulosclerosis, afferent & efferent hyaline arteriolosclerosis, pyelonephritis, papillary necrosis, Armanni–Ebstein== |
| ==Eye== | ==Retinopathy — non-proliferative (microaneurysms — earliest, dot-blot hemorrhages, hard exudates, cotton-wool spots) → proliferative (neovascularization — VEGF)==; ==cataract, glaucoma== |
| ==Nerves== | ==Symmetric distal sensorimotor polyneuropathy (glove & stocking) — most common==; autonomic; mononeuropathy (CN III — pupil sparing) |
| Infections | ==Mucormycosis, malignant otitis externa (Pseudomonas), emphysematous pyelonephritis / cholecystitis, Fournier gangrene, TB==, candidiasis |
| Pancreas (T1) | Insulitis, ↓ islets; (T2) ==amyloid==; ==infant of diabetic mother — islet hyperplasia (hypoglycemia)== |

## Adrenal Gland

### Cushing syndrome (hypercortisolism) :

| Cause | Frequency | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| ==Exogenous (iatrogenic) glucocorticoids== | ==Most common overall== | ==Bilateral cortical atrophy== (↓ ACTH) |
| ==Cushing disease (pituitary ACTH microadenoma)== | ==Most common endogenous (70%)==; women 20–40 y | ==Bilateral nodular / diffuse cortical hyperplasia==; ==suppresses with high-dose dexamethasone== |
| ==Ectopic ACTH== | 10–15% | ==Small cell lung carcinoma, carcinoid, medullary thyroid, pancreatic NET==; ==no suppression==, hypokalemia, hyperpigmentation; men |
| ==Primary adrenal (adrenal adenoma / carcinoma / micronodular hyperplasia)== | 10–20% | ==↓ ACTH, contralateral atrophy==; ==PPNAD (Carney complex — PRKAR1A)==, ==macronodular (ARMC5)== |

- Features : ==central obesity, moon facies, buffalo hump, purple abdominal striae, proximal myopathy, hypertension, hyperglycemia, osteoporosis, hirsutism, menstrual irregularity, psychiatric, infections, poor wound healing==.
- Tests : ==24-h urinary free cortisol, late-night salivary cortisol, overnight 1 mg dexamethasone suppression (screening)==; ==ACTH level== (dependent vs independent); ==high-dose dex suppression / CRH test== (pituitary vs ectopic); ==inferior petrosal sinus sampling==.
- ==Crooke hyaline change== — pituitary corticotrophs (cytokeratin).

### Hyperaldosteronism :

- ==Primary (Conn syndrome)== — ==bilateral idiopathic hyperplasia (60%), aldosterone-producing adenoma (35% — KCNJ5 mutation; "spironolactone bodies" after treatment)==, glucocorticoid-remediable (CYP11B1/B2 fusion), carcinoma; ==hypertension + hypokalemia (muscle weakness), metabolic alkalosis, ↓ renin, ↑ aldosterone (ARR > 20–30)==; ==no edema (aldosterone escape)==; ==most common cause of secondary (endocrine) hypertension==.
- ==Secondary== — ↑ renin : renal artery stenosis, CHF, cirrhosis, nephrotic, pregnancy.

### Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) :

| Enzyme | Frequency | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==21-hydroxylase (CYP21A2)== | ==> 90%== | ==↓ cortisol & aldosterone, ↑ androgens== → ==salt-wasting (hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hypotension — neonates), virilization (ambiguous genitalia in females — most common cause), precocious puberty in males==; ==↑ 17-OH progesterone (newborn screening)== |
| ==11β-hydroxylase (CYP11B1)== | 5% | ==↑ 11-deoxycorticosterone (mineralocorticoid) → hypertension, hypokalemia + virilization== |
| ==17α-hydroxylase (CYP17)== | Rare | ==↓ androgens & cortisol, ↑ mineralocorticoids → hypertension, hypokalemia, sexual infantilism / ambiguous male genitalia== |

- ==Bilateral adrenal hyperplasia (↑ ACTH — loss of cortisol feedback)==; ==AR==.

### Adrenal insufficiency :

| Type | Causes | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Acute== | ==Waterhouse–Friderichsen syndrome (meningococcemia — massive bilateral adrenal hemorrhage + DIC; also Pseudomonas, pneumococcus, H. influenzae)==; ==abrupt withdrawal of long-term steroids (most common)==; anticoagulation, postpartum | ==Shock, hypoglycemia, hyperkalemia== |
| ==Chronic primary (Addison disease)== | ==Autoimmune adrenalitis (most common in West — 21-hydroxylase antibodies; APS-1 (AIRE) & APS-2 (Schmidt — with thyroid, T1DM))==; ==TB (most common in India / developing countries — caseous necrosis, enlarged then fibrosed/calcified)==; ==AIDS (CMV, MAC)==, ==metastatic cancer (lung, breast)==, histoplasmosis, amyloidosis, adrenoleukodystrophy | ==Weakness, weight loss, hypotension, GI symptoms, hyperpigmentation (↑ ACTH/POMC → MSH — skin creases, buccal mucosa)==, ==hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hypoglycemia, eosinophilia==; ==ACTH stimulation (Synacthen) test== |
| ==Secondary== | Pituitary / hypothalamic disease, ==exogenous steroid withdrawal== | ==No hyperpigmentation==, ==no hyperkalemia (aldosterone preserved — RAAS)== |

### Adrenal cortical tumours :

- ==Adenoma== — ==most common adrenal mass (incidentaloma)==; ==yellow, well-circumscribed, lipid-rich clear cells==; usually non-functional; functional → Cushing or Conn.
- ==Adrenocortical carcinoma== — ==large (> 6 cm, > 100 g), invasive, hemorrhagic, necrotic==; ==functional (virilization, Cushing)==; ==Li–Fraumeni (TP53), Beckwith–Wiedemann==; ==Weiss criteria==; poor prognosis.

### Adrenal medulla :

| Tumour | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Pheochromocytoma== | ==Chromaffin cells==; =="rule of 10s" (historic) : 10% extra-adrenal (paraganglioma — now ~15–20%), 10% bilateral, 10% malignant, 10% familial (now ~25–40% germline!), 10% not hypertensive==; ==MEN2A/2B (RET), VHL, NF1, SDH (SDHB — malignant paraganglioma)==; ==paroxysmal/sustained hypertension, headache, palpitations, sweating (classic triad)==; ==Zellballen (nests of polygonal cells surrounded by sustentacular cells — S100 +)==; ==chromogranin, synaptophysin +==; ==chromaffin reaction (brown with dichromate)==; ==malignancy only by metastasis==; ==plasma free / urinary fractionated metanephrines (best)==, VMA; ==α-blockade (phenoxybenzamine) before β-blockade & surgery== |
| ==Neuroblastoma== | ==Most common extracranial solid tumour of childhood; most common tumour of infancy==; ==adrenal medulla (40%), paravertebral sympathetic chain==; ==< 2 years==; ==small round blue cells, Homer Wright pseudorosettes (neuropil centre)==, ganglionic differentiation (ganglioneuroblastoma → ganglioneuroma); ==N-MYC amplification (poor prognosis), 1p deletion, 17q gain, ALK, hyperdiploidy (good)==; ==↑ urinary VMA & HVA==; ==abdominal mass crossing midline, calcification==, ==periorbital ecchymoses ("raccoon eyes" — orbital metastasis), "blueberry muffin" skin (infants), Hutchinson (bone) vs Pepper (liver) syndromes==, ==opsoclonus–myoclonus ("dancing eyes, dancing feet")==; ==stage MS (4S) — < 18 months, spontaneous regression==; ==Shimada classification==; ==NSE, synaptophysin, PHOX2B + == |

## Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN)

| Syndrome | Gene | Components |
|---|---|---|
| ==MEN 1 (Wermer)== | ==MEN1 (menin) — 11q13== | ==3 Ps : Parathyroid (hyperplasia — 95%, first manifestation), Pancreas / duodenal NETs (gastrinoma — ZES, most common cause of death; insulinoma), Pituitary (prolactinoma)==; also adrenal adenomas, foregut carcinoids, angiofibromas, lipomas |
| ==MEN 2A (Sipple)== | ==RET (germline, gain of function) — 10q11== | ==Medullary thyroid carcinoma (100%), pheochromocytoma (50%), parathyroid hyperplasia (20–30%)==; ==cutaneous lichen amyloidosis, Hirschsprung== |
| ==MEN 2B (MEN 3)== | ==RET (M918T)== | ==Medullary thyroid carcinoma (aggressive, early — thyroidectomy in 1st year), pheochromocytoma, mucosal neuromas (lips, tongue — "blubbery lips"), marfanoid habitus, intestinal ganglioneuromatosis==; ==no hyperparathyroidism== |
| ==MEN 4== | ==CDKN1B (p27)== | MEN1-like |
