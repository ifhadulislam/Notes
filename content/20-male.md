### Carcinoma of penis :

- ==Squamous cell carcinoma (95%)==; ==40–70 y==; ==India, Asia, Africa (uncircumcised)==.
- Risk factors : ==lack of circumcision (smegma, phimosis — most important)==, ==HPV 16, 18 (50%)==, poor hygiene, smoking, ==PUVA==, lichen sclerosus.
- ==Glans / inner prepuce (most common)==; grey crusted papule → ulcerated, fungating; ==inguinal lymph nodes (superficial)==.
- ==Neonatal circumcision is protective==.

## Testis & Scrotum

### Non-neoplastic :

## Testicular Tumours

- ==Germ cell tumours (95%) — most common malignancy in men 15–34 y==; ==sex cord–stromal (5%)==; ==lymphoma — most common testicular tumour in men > 60 y (DLBCL, bilateral)==.
- Risk factors : ==cryptorchidism (most important)==, ==testicular dysgenesis (Klinefelter, androgen insensitivity)==, family history, ==previous contralateral GCT==, ==i(12p) — isochromosome 12p (in nearly all GCT)==.
- Precursor : ==germ cell neoplasia in situ (GCNIS — formerly ITGCN)== — except spermatocytic tumour & prepubertal teratoma/yolk sac.
- ==Painless testicular mass that does not transilluminate==; ==do not biopsy trans-scrotally — radical inguinal orchiectomy== (scrotal lymphatics → inguinal).
- Lymphatic spread : ==retroperitoneal para-aortic nodes first== ; hematogenous : lungs, liver (choriocarcinoma early).

## Prostate

### Prostatitis :

- ==Acute bacterial — E. coli== (catheterization, reflux); ==chronic bacterial (recurrent UTI)==; ==chronic abacterial / pelvic pain syndrome (most common)==; ==granulomatous (BCG instillation, TB, idiopathic)==.

### Benign prostatic hyperplasia (nodular hyperplasia) :

- ==Extremely common : 50% > 50 y; 90% > 80 y==.
- ==Dihydrotestosterone (DHT — 5α-reductase type 2 in stromal cells)== → growth factors (FGF) → ==proliferation of stromal and epithelial cells==.
- ==Transition zone & periurethral zone (inner)== → ==urethral compression==; ==not premalignant==.
- Microscopy : ==nodules of hyperplastic glands (two cell layers — luminal secretory + basal) with papillary infoldings, corpora amylacea; fibromuscular stromal nodules==.
- Clinical : ==LUTS — frequency, nocturia, hesitancy, poor stream, dribbling==, acute urinary retention, ==bladder trabeculation & diverticula (hypertrophy)==, hydronephrosis, UTI, stones.
- Treatment : ==finasteride / dutasteride (5α-reductase inhibitors)==, ==α1-blockers (tamsulosin)==, TURP.

### Carcinoma prostate :

- ==Most common visceral cancer in men (West)==; ==2nd leading cause of cancer death in men (West)==; ==> 50 y, African-American men (highest)==; ==latent / incidental carcinoma common at autopsy==.
- Risk factors : ==age, race, family history (BRCA2, HOXB13), androgens, diet (red meat, fat)==; ==ERG fusion (TMPRSS2-ERG — 50%)==, ==PTEN loss, AR amplification (castration-resistant)==.
- ==Peripheral zone (70%) — posterior — palpable on DRE (hard, nodular)==.
- Precursor : ==high-grade prostatic intraepithelial neoplasia (HGPIN)==.

### Histologic diagnosis :

1. ==Small, crowded, infiltrative glands with a single cell layer — ABSENCE of basal cell layer (p63, HMWCK / 34βE12 negative)==
2. ==Prominent nucleoli (most important cytologic feature)==
3. ==Perineural invasion (diagnostic of malignancy)==
4. ==Intraluminal crystalloids, blue mucin, pink amorphous secretions==
5. ==AMACR (P504S) — positive in carcinoma==

| Grading | Details |
|---|---|
| ==Gleason score== | ==Architectural patterns 1–5 (only 3–5 used now)==; ==score = primary + secondary pattern (6–10)== |
| ==ISUP Grade Group== | ==GG1 : ≤ 6 · GG2 : 3+4 = 7 · GG3 : 4+3 = 7 · GG4 : 8 · GG5 : 9–10== |

- ==PSA (kallikrein-3 — serine protease, liquefies semen)== — ==organ-specific, not cancer-specific==; ↑ in BPH, prostatitis, infarction, instrumentation, ejaculation; ==PSA > 4 ng/mL==; ==PSA density, velocity, free/total PSA (↓ free PSA → cancer)==, ==PCA3, PHI, 4Kscore==; ==PSMA PET==.
- Spread : ==local (seminal vesicles, bladder base)==, ==lymphatic — obturator nodes==, ==hematogenous — bone (lumbar spine, pelvis, femur) — osteoblastic (sclerotic) metastases, ↑ ALP==; ==Batson vertebral venous plexus==.
- Treatment : ==active surveillance (GG1)==, radical prostatectomy, RT, ==androgen deprivation (GnRH agonists/antagonists, abiraterone, enzalutamide)==, ==PARP inhibitors (BRCA2)==.
