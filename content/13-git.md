### Oral squamous cell carcinoma :

- ==95% of oral cancers==; India — ==most common cancer in men (lip & oral cavity)==; ==gingivobuccal sulcus ("Indian oral cancer" — tobacco quid)==; West — ==lateral border of tongue==.
- Risk factors : ==tobacco (smoked, chewed)==, ==alcohol (synergistic)==, ==areca nut==, ==HPV 16 (oropharynx — tonsil, base of tongue; p16 + ; better prognosis)==, sharp teeth, Plummer–Vinson, syphilis, sunlight (lip — lower lip).
- ==Field cancerization== — multiple primaries. ==Verrucous carcinoma== — tobacco chewers, exophytic, well differentiated, pushing margins, rarely metastasizes.
- Metastasis : ==cervical lymph nodes (level I–III)==.

### Barrett esophagus :

- ==Intestinal metaplasia — columnar epithelium with goblet cells== replacing squamous epithelium of distal esophagus; complication of ==chronic GERD==.
- Diagnosis : ==endoscopic salmon-pink velvety mucosa above GE junction + histologic goblet cells==; ==long segment ≥ 3 cm==.
- ==Premalignant for adenocarcinoma== (30–40× risk); progression via dysplasia — ==TP53, CDKN2A==.

### Esophageal carcinoma :

| Feature | ==Squamous cell carcinoma== | ==Adenocarcinoma== |
|---|---|---|
| Epidemiology | ==Most common worldwide & in India==; > 45 y; =="esophageal cancer belt" (Iran, China)== | ==Most common in West (USA)==; white men |
| Site | ==Middle third (50%)== | ==Distal third (lower)== |
| Risk factors | ==Tobacco, alcohol==, ==hot beverages==, ==achalasia==, ==Plummer–Vinson==, ==tylosis (palmoplantar keratoderma — RHBDF2)==, ==caustic (lye) strictures==, nitrosamines, HPV, poor diet (↓ vitamins) | ==Barrett esophagus (GERD)==, obesity, tobacco; ==↓ H. pylori (protective?)== |
| Genetics | TP53, CDKN2A, SOX2, cyclin D1 | TP53, CDKN2A, ERBB2 |

- Symptoms : ==progressive dysphagia (solids → liquids)==, weight loss; spread to mediastinum early (no serosa).

### Peptic ulcer disease :

| Feature | ==Duodenal ulcer== | ==Gastric ulcer== |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | ==4× more common== | Less |
| Site | ==First part of duodenum (anterior wall — perforation; posterior — bleeding from gastroduodenal artery)== | ==Lesser curvature (antrum–body junction)== |
| H. pylori | ==~70–90%== | ~60–70% |
| Acid | ==↑ acid== | Normal / ↓ (mucosal defence ↓) |
| Pain | ==Relieved by food== (night pain) | ==Worsened by food== |
| Malignancy | ==Never malignant== | Must biopsy to exclude cancer |

- Other causes : ==NSAIDs (most common in H. pylori-negative)==, ==Zollinger–Ellison==, smoking, steroids, ==cirrhosis, COPD, CKD, hyperparathyroidism==, blood group O (DU).
- Morphology : ==round-oval, sharply punched-out, clean base, straight walls, overhanging margins NOT heaped up== (vs carcinoma : heaped, irregular); ==4 zones== : necrotic fibrinoid debris → nonspecific inflammatory infiltrate → granulation tissue → ==fibrous scar (with endarteritis obliterans)==.
- Complications : ==bleeding (most common)==, ==perforation (most common cause of death — air under diaphragm)==, ==obstruction (gastric outlet)==, ==penetration (pancreas — ↑ amylase)==.

### Gastric adenocarcinoma :

- ==90–95% of gastric cancers==; ==Japan, Korea, Chile== high; declining with refrigeration.
- Risk factors : ==H. pylori (most important)==, ==nitrates / nitrosamines (smoked, pickled, salted foods)==, ==chronic atrophic gastritis, intestinal metaplasia==, ==partial gastrectomy (Billroth II)==, ==EBV (10%)==, ==blood group A==, Ménétrier, adenomatous polyps, ==smoking==, ==familial (CDH1 — diffuse)==, Lynch, FAP, Peutz–Jeghers.

| Feature | ==Intestinal type== | ==Diffuse type== |
|---|---|---|
| Precursors | ==Intestinal metaplasia, H. pylori, dysplasia== | ==No precursor lesion== |
| Genetics | APC, β-catenin, MSI | ==CDH1 (E-cadherin) loss==, RHOA |
| Morphology | ==Bulky, exophytic, glandular (mucin-secreting)==, ulcerated | ==Signet ring cells (mucin pushes nucleus)== — discohesive, infiltrative; ==linitis plastica ("leather bottle" stomach — thick rigid wall)== |
| Epidemiology | > 50 y, men, high-incidence areas | Younger, ==equal sex==, uniform worldwide |

- Spread / eponyms : ==Virchow node (left supraclavicular)==, ==Sister Mary Joseph nodule (umbilicus)==, ==Krukenberg tumour (bilateral ovarian — signet ring)==, ==Irish node (left axillary)==, ==Blumer shelf (rectovesical / rectouterine pouch)==.
- Paraneoplastic : ==acanthosis nigricans, Leser–Trélat sign==, Trousseau.
- ==Early gastric cancer== : ==confined to mucosa & submucosa, regardless of nodal status== — excellent prognosis.

### Appendicitis :

- Obstruction (==fecalith — most common==, lymphoid hyperplasia in children, worms, tumour) → ↑ pressure → ischemia → bacterial invasion.

### Colorectal carcinoma — pathways :

:::flow Adenoma–carcinoma sequence (chromosomal instability, ~80%)
Normal mucosa → loss of ==APC (first hit — "gatekeeper")== → ==β-catenin accumulation (WNT)==
Small adenoma → ==KRAS mutation== → large adenoma
==SMAD2/4 loss (18q — DCC)== → dysplasia
==TP53 loss (17p)== → carcinoma ; telomerase, other mutations → metastasis
:::

| Pathway | Genetics | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Chromosomal instability (APC/β-catenin)== | APC, KRAS, SMAD4, TP53 | ==Left-sided==, tubular adenoma → carcinoma |
| ==Microsatellite instability (MMR)== | ==MLH1 hypermethylation (sporadic) or germline MMR (Lynch)==, ==BRAF V600E (sporadic)== | ==Right-sided, mucinous, signet ring, TILs, Crohn-like reaction==; ==better prognosis==, sessile serrated lesions; respond to ==immunotherapy (PD-1)== |
| CIMP | Promoter hypermethylation | Serrated pathway |

- Risk factors : ==age > 50==, ==low-fibre, high red meat / fat diet==, obesity, ==IBD (UC > Crohn)==, ==family history==, polyposis syndromes, ==Streptococcus gallolyticus bacteremia==, smoking, alcohol; ==aspirin/NSAIDs protective (COX-2)==.

| Feature | ==Right-sided (proximal)== | ==Left-sided (distal)== |
|---|---|---|
| Gross | ==Exophytic, polypoid, fungating== | ==Annular, "napkin-ring / apple-core" constriction== |
| Presentation | ==Iron deficiency anemia (occult bleeding), fatigue, weakness== | ==Change in bowel habits, obstruction, hematochezia, thin "pencil" stools== |

- Spread : ==liver (most common distant site — portal vein)==, lung, peritoneum; ==CEA== for recurrence monitoring.
- Staging : ==TNM== (AJCC); ==Astler–Coller / Dukes : A (mucosa/submucosa), B (beyond muscularis), C (nodes), D (distant)== — ==most important prognostic factor : stage (nodal status)==.

### Carcinoid (neuroendocrine) tumours :

- ==Most common site : small intestine (ileum) > appendix (tip) > rectum==; ==small intestine — most aggressive / metastasize==; appendix — usually benign.

## Pancreas

### Acute pancreatitis :

Causes :

1. ==Gallstones (most common — 35–60%)==
2. ==Alcohol (2nd most common; most common in Indian men)==
3. Hypertriglyceridemia (> 1000 mg/dL), ==hypercalcemia==
4. ==Drugs== — azathioprine, valproate, ==didanosine==, thiazides, estrogens, steroids, L-asparaginase
5. ==Post-ERCP==, trauma, surgery
6. Infections — ==mumps==, coxsackie, CMV
7. ==Hereditary — PRSS1 (cationic trypsinogen gain-of-function), SPINK1 (trypsin inhibitor loss), CFTR==
8. Scorpion venom (Trinidad), ischemia, pancreas divisum, autoimmune

:::mnemo Causes of acute pancreatitis — "I GET SMASHED"
==I==diopathic · ==G==allstones · ==E==thanol · ==T==rauma · ==S==teroids · ==M==umps · ==A==utoimmune · ==S==corpion sting · ==H==ypercalcemia / ==H==ypertriglyceridemia · ==E==RCP · ==D==rugs.
:::

:::flow Pathogenesis
Pancreatic duct obstruction (gallstone) || Acinar cell injury (alcohol, drugs, viruses) || Defective intracellular transport
==Inappropriate intra-acinar activation of trypsinogen → trypsin==
Trypsin activates other proenzymes : ==phospholipase A₂ (cell membrane), elastase (vessel walls — hemorrhage)==, lipase (fat necrosis), kallikrein
Autodigestion : ==microvascular leakage, edema, fat necrosis (saponification), hemorrhage, acute inflammation==
SIRS → ==ARDS, shock, DIC, AKI==
:::

- Morphology : ==acute interstitial (edematous) pancreatitis== vs ==acute necrotizing (hemorrhagic) pancreatitis==; ==fat necrosis — chalky white areas (Ca soaps), ghost outlines of adipocytes==.
- Lab : ==serum lipase (more specific, stays longer)==, amylase (rises in 2–12 h, normal in 3–5 days), ==hypocalcemia (poor prognosis — fat saponification)==, hyperglycemia, ↑ CRP.
- Signs : ==Grey-Turner (flank), Cullen (periumbilical)== ecchymoses; ==Ranson criteria==, BISAP, APACHE II.
- Complications : ==pseudocyst (most common; no epithelial lining — granulation/fibrous wall; > 4 weeks)==, abscess, infected necrosis, ==splenic vein thrombosis==, ARDS, DIC.

### Chronic pancreatitis :

- ==Alcohol (most common in adults)==, ==tropical calcific pancreatitis (India — Kerala; SPINK1, young, malnourished — "fibrocalculous pancreatic diabetes")==, hereditary, CF, ==autoimmune (IgG4 — type 1; lymphoplasmacytic sclerosing, storiform fibrosis, obliterative phlebitis; "sausage-shaped" pancreas; steroid-responsive)==.
- ==Parenchymal fibrosis, ↓ acini, dilated ducts with protein plugs / calcified stones (intraductal calculi)==; islets relatively spared until late.
- Clinical : recurrent pain, ==steatorrhea (exocrine failure)==, ==diabetes (endocrine failure)==, ==calcification on X-ray==, pseudocyst.

### Pancreatic carcinoma (ductal adenocarcinoma) :

- ==85%==; 60–80 y; 4th leading cause of cancer death (West); ==5-year survival < 10%==.
- Risk factors : ==smoking (most important)==, chronic pancreatitis, diabetes, obesity, ==hereditary (BRCA2, CDKN2A (familial melanoma), PALB2, Lynch, Peutz–Jeghers)==.
- Precursors : ==PanIN (pancreatic intraepithelial neoplasia)==, ==IPMN (intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm — GNAS, KRAS)==, ==mucinous cystic neoplasm (ovarian-type stroma — women)==.
- Genetics : ==KRAS (> 90% — earliest)==, ==CDKN2A (p16 — 95%)==, ==SMAD4 (55%)==, ==TP53 (70–75%)==.
- Site : ==head (60%)== → ==painless obstructive jaundice, Courvoisier sign (palpable non-tender gallbladder)==; body/tail (15%) → late, pain, metastases.
- Morphology : ==hard, grey-white, poorly defined (desmoplastic)==; ==perineural & lymphovascular invasion==.
- Features : ==Trousseau sign (migratory thrombophlebitis)==, weight loss, epigastric pain radiating to back; ==CA 19-9==.
