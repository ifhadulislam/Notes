# Liver, Gallbladder & Biliary Tract

## Liver Architecture

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| ==Classic lobule== | Hexagon — ==central vein at centre, portal tracts at corners== |
| ==Acinus (Rappaport)== | Functional unit — ==zone 1 (periportal — best O₂)==, zone 2 (midzonal), ==zone 3 (centrilobular — least O₂, most CYP450)== |

| Zone | Injured by |
|---|---|
| ==Zone 1 (periportal)== | ==Phosphorus, eclampsia (periportal fibrin), cocaine (some), viral hepatitis (interface)==, iron (hemochromatosis — first) |
| Zone 2 | ==Yellow fever (Councilman bodies)== |
| ==Zone 3 (centrilobular)== | ==Ischemia/shock, right heart failure (nutmeg), acetaminophen, CCl₄, halothane, alcohol, Budd–Chiari== |

### Liver function tests — patterns :

| Pattern | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| ==Hepatocellular== | ==↑↑ ALT, AST== (ALT > AST in viral hepatitis) | Viral, drug, ischemic (> 1000 U/L — ischemic, acetaminophen, viral) |
| ==Alcoholic== | ==AST : ALT > 2 : 1==, ==↑ GGT== | Alcoholic liver disease |
| ==Cholestatic== | ==↑↑ ALP, GGT, conjugated bilirubin== | Obstruction, PBC, PSC, drugs |
| ==Synthetic failure== | ==↓ albumin, ↑ PT/INR (best acute marker)== | Cirrhosis, acute liver failure |

## Jaundice & Bilirubin Metabolism

:::flow
Senescent RBCs → heme → ==biliverdin → unconjugated bilirubin== (macrophages)
Bound to ==albumin== → hepatocyte uptake (OATP)
==Conjugation by UGT1A1 (glucuronidation)== → conjugated (water-soluble) bilirubin
Excretion into bile (==MRP2 — ATP-dependent; rate-limiting==) → intestine → urobilinogen (10–20% enterohepatic) / stercobilin
:::

| Feature | ==Pre-hepatic (hemolytic)== | ==Hepatic== | ==Post-hepatic (obstructive)== |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bilirubin | ==Unconjugated ↑== | Both ↑ | ==Conjugated ↑== |
| ==Urine bilirubin== | ==Absent (acholuric jaundice)== | Present | ==Present (dark urine)== |
| ==Urine urobilinogen== | ==↑== | ↑ / variable | ==Absent / ↓== |
| Stool | Dark | Normal / pale | ==Clay-coloured (pale)== |
| ALP | Normal | Mild ↑ | ==↑↑== |
| Pruritus | Absent | ± | ==Present (bile salts)== |

### Hereditary hyperbilirubinemias :

| Syndrome | Defect | Bilirubin | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Gilbert syndrome== | ==↓ UGT1A1 activity (TATA box promoter — A(TA)7TAA)== ; AR | ==Unconjugated== | ==Most common (3–10%)==; mild jaundice with ==fasting, stress, infection==; benign; ==irinotecan toxicity== |
| ==Crigler–Najjar type I== | ==Absent UGT1A1== ; AR | Unconjugated (↑↑ > 20 mg/dL) | ==Kernicterus, fatal in infancy==; ==phenobarbital — no response==; liver transplant |
| ==Crigler–Najjar type II (Arias)== | ==↓ UGT1A1== ; AR/AD | Unconjugated | ==Responds to phenobarbital==; milder |
| ==Dubin–Johnson syndrome== | ==MRP2 (ABCC2) — canalicular excretion== ; AR | ==Conjugated== | ==Black (dark) liver — epinephrine metabolite pigment in lysosomes (centrilobular)==; ==↑ urinary coproporphyrin I (> 80%)==; ==GB not visualized on oral cholecystogram==; benign |
| ==Rotor syndrome== | ==OATP1B1/1B3 (hepatic uptake / storage)== ; AR | Conjugated | ==Normal liver colour==; milder; total urinary coproporphyrin ↑; GB visualized |

:::mnemo Conjugated hereditary hyperbilirubinemia
"==D==ubin–Johnson is ==D==ark (black liver) · ==R==otor is ==R==egular (normal) liver".
:::

### Neonatal jaundice :

- ==Physiological jaundice== : appears ==after 24 h (day 2–3)==, peaks day 3–5, resolves by 2 weeks; immature UGT1A1, ↑ RBC turnover.
- ==Pathological== : ==within 24 h (hemolysis — Rh/ABO, G6PD, HS)==, > 2 weeks (cholestasis), conjugated > 20% of total.
- ==Biliary atresia== — ==most common cause of neonatal cholestasis requiring surgery / liver transplant in children==; ==progressive fibro-obliterative destruction of extrahepatic bile ducts==; ==ductular proliferation, bile plugs, portal fibrosis==; ==Kasai portoenterostomy before 60 days==.
- ==Neonatal hepatitis== — ==multinucleated giant cell transformation of hepatocytes==; α1-AT deficiency, CMV, idiopathic.

## Viral Hepatitis

| Feature | ==HAV== | ==HBV== | ==HCV== | ==HDV== | ==HEV== |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Virus | ==RNA (Picorna)== | ==DNA (Hepadna) — partially dsDNA, reverse transcriptase== | ==RNA (Flavi)== | ==Defective RNA (needs HBsAg)== | ==RNA (Hepe)== |
| Transmission | ==Fecal–oral== | ==Parenteral, sexual, perinatal (vertical — most common in endemic areas)== | ==Parenteral (IV drug use — most common)== | Parenteral | ==Fecal–oral (water-borne epidemics)== |
| Incubation | 2–6 weeks | ==2–26 weeks (longest)== | 4–26 weeks | Same as HBV | 2–8 weeks |
| Chronicity | ==Never== | ==5–10% adults; 90% neonates== | ==> 80% (most likely to become chronic)== | ==Superinfection — 80% chronic; coinfection — < 5%== | ==Only in immunocompromised (genotype 3)== |
| Fulminant | Rare | 1% | Rare | ==Superinfection — highest risk== | ==Pregnant women — 20% mortality (3rd trimester)== |
| HCC | No | ==Yes (even without cirrhosis — integration)== | Yes (with cirrhosis) | Yes | No |
| Vaccine | Yes | Yes | ==No== | HBV vaccine | Yes (China) |

:::pearl
- ==HEV — most common cause of acute viral hepatitis & acute liver failure in India==; epidemics (Delhi 1955–56).
- ==HCV — extrahepatic : mixed cryoglobulinemia, MPGN, porphyria cutanea tarda, lichen planus, B-cell NHL, insulin resistance==.
- ==HBV extrahepatic : PAN, membranous GN==.
- HCV genotype ==3 — most common in India== (genotype 1 worldwide); ==steatosis with genotype 3==.
:::

### HBV serology :

| Marker | Significance |
|---|---|
| ==HBsAg== | ==First marker to appear (before symptoms)==; > 6 months = ==chronic carrier== |
| ==Anti-HBs== | ==Immunity== (recovery or vaccination); appears after HBsAg disappears |
| ==HBeAg== | ==Active replication, high infectivity== |
| ==Anti-HBc IgM== | ==Acute infection — only marker during "window period"== |
| Anti-HBc IgG | Past or chronic infection (not after vaccination) |
| ==HBV DNA== | ==Viral load — most sensitive marker of replication== (precore mutants — HBeAg − but DNA +) |

:::flow HBV serologic course (acute, resolving)
==HBsAg== + HBeAg + HBV DNA appear (4–12 weeks)
==Anti-HBc IgM== rises with symptoms (↑ ALT)
HBeAg → anti-HBe (seroconversion — ↓ infectivity)
HBsAg disappears → ==window period (only anti-HBc IgM positive)==
==Anti-HBs== appears → recovery & immunity
:::

### Morphology of hepatitis :

| Acute hepatitis | Chronic hepatitis |
|---|---|
| ==Ballooning degeneration==, ==apoptotic hepatocytes (Councilman / acidophil bodies)==, ==lobular disarray==, ==spotty necrosis==, bridging necrosis (severe), cholestasis, Kupffer cell hyperplasia; ==mononuclear portal & lobular infiltrate== | ==Portal inflammation, interface hepatitis (piecemeal necrosis)==, ==bridging fibrosis → cirrhosis==; ==HBV : ground-glass hepatocytes (HBsAg in ER — Shikata orcein +), sanded nuclei (HBcAg)==; ==HCV : lymphoid aggregates / follicles in portal tracts, bile duct damage, macrovesicular steatosis== |

- Grading (inflammation) & staging (fibrosis) : ==METAVIR, Ishak, Batts–Ludwig==; non-invasive : ==FibroScan (transient elastography), APRI, FIB-4==.

### Autoimmune & other hepatitis :

- ==Autoimmune hepatitis== — ==young/middle-aged women==; ==type 1 : ANA, anti-smooth muscle (anti-actin)==; ==type 2 (children) : anti-LKM1, anti-LC1==; ==↑ IgG==; ==interface hepatitis with abundant plasma cells, rosettes, emperipolesis==; ==steroid-responsive==.
- ==Drug-induced liver injury== : ==acetaminophen (zone 3 necrosis, NAPQI; antidote N-acetylcysteine)==, ==isoniazid (hepatitis — slow acetylators)==, halothane, ==amiodarone (Mallory bodies, phospholipidosis)==, ==OCPs (cholestasis, hepatic adenoma, Budd–Chiari)==, methotrexate (fibrosis), valproate (microvesicular), ==anabolic steroids (peliosis hepatis, adenoma)==, ==chlorpromazine (cholestasis)==.

## Alcoholic & Fatty Liver Disease

| Lesion | Morphology |
|---|---|
| ==Hepatic steatosis (fatty liver)== | ==Macrovesicular — centrilobular first==; reversible on abstinence; large, yellow, greasy liver |
| ==Alcoholic (steato)hepatitis== | ==Hepatocyte ballooning, Mallory–Denk bodies (tangled cytokeratin 8 & 18 — eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions, ubiquitin +)==, ==neutrophilic infiltration (around ballooned cells — "satellitosis")==, ==pericellular / perivenular "chicken-wire" fibrosis (zone 3)==, ==megamitochondria== |
| ==Alcoholic cirrhosis== | ==Micronodular (Laennec) — nodules < 3 mm, "hobnail liver"==; later mixed / macronodular; brown, shrunken |

- Mechanisms : ==acetaldehyde (adducts, lipid peroxidation)==, ==↑ NADH → ↑ FA synthesis, ↓ β-oxidation==, ==CYP2E1 → ROS==, endotoxin (gut leak) → Kupffer TNF, ↓ GSH.
- ==Hepatic stellate (Ito) cells — main collagen producers (perisinusoidal space of Disse)== → myofibroblasts (TGF-β).
- ==NAFLD / MASLD== : metabolic syndrome, obesity, T2DM; ==steatosis → NASH (steatohepatitis — ballooning, Mallory bodies less common, lobular inflammation) → cirrhosis, HCC==; ==most common chronic liver disease worldwide==; ==PNPLA3 variant==; ALT > AST.

## Cirrhosis

- ==Diffuse process characterized by (1) bridging fibrous septa, (2) parenchymal nodules (regenerating hepatocytes encircled by fibrosis), (3) disruption of architecture of entire liver==.

| Type | Nodule size | Causes |
|---|---|---|
| ==Micronodular== | ==< 3 mm, uniform== | ==Alcohol==, hemochromatosis, biliary obstruction, Wilson, venous outflow obstruction, ICC |
| ==Macronodular== | ==> 3 mm, variable== | ==Post-necrotic : chronic viral hepatitis (HBV, HCV)==, Wilson, α1-AT, autoimmune hepatitis |
| Mixed | Both | — |

### Causes of cirrhosis :

1. ==Alcohol== (most common in West & North India — along with HBV)
2. ==Chronic viral hepatitis (HBV, HCV)==
3. ==NAFLD / NASH (MASLD)== — rising
4. ==Biliary== — PBC, PSC, biliary atresia, secondary biliary
5. ==Metabolic== — ==hemochromatosis, Wilson disease, α1-antitrypsin deficiency==, galactosemia, tyrosinemia, glycogen storage
6. ==Autoimmune hepatitis==
7. ==Vascular== — Budd–Chiari, cardiac cirrhosis
8. Drugs (methotrexate), ==Indian childhood cirrhosis (copper)==, cryptogenic

### Metabolic liver diseases :

| Disease | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Hereditary hemochromatosis== | ==HFE (C282Y homozygous) — AR, chromosome 6==; ↓ hepcidin → ↑ iron absorption; ==micronodular cirrhosis, DM, skin pigmentation (bronze diabetes), cardiomyopathy, arthropathy (2nd–3rd MCP), hypogonadism==; ==↑ transferrin saturation (> 45%), ↑ ferritin==; ==hepatocyte & bile duct iron (Prussian blue)==; ==HCC (200×)==; phlebotomy |
| ==Wilson disease (hepatolenticular degeneration)== | ==ATP7B — chromosome 13, AR== → ↓ copper excretion into bile, ↓ incorporation into ceruloplasmin; ==liver (fatty change, acute/chronic hepatitis, cirrhosis), brain (basal ganglia — putamen atrophy/cavitation; dysarthria, tremor, psychiatric), eye (Kayser–Fleischer rings — Descemet membrane), hemolysis==; ==↓ ceruloplasmin, ↑ 24-h urine copper (> 100 µg), ↑ hepatic copper (> 250 µg/g dry weight — gold standard)==; rhodanine/orcein stain; ==penicillamine, trientine, zinc== |
| ==α1-antitrypsin deficiency== | ==PiZZ== — ==PAS +, diastase-resistant globules in periportal hepatocytes==; neonatal hepatitis, cirrhosis, HCC, ==panacinar emphysema== |
| ==Indian childhood cirrhosis== | ==Copper (brass vessels for milk)==; ==Mallory hyaline, pericellular fibrosis== |

### Complications of cirrhosis / hepatic failure :

1. ==Portal hypertension== — ascites, varices, splenomegaly, caput medusae
2. ==Hepatic encephalopathy (↑ ammonia)== — ==asterixis (flapping tremor)==; ==Alzheimer type II astrocytes==
3. ==Hepatorenal syndrome== — ==functional renal failure (normal kidney morphology)== — splanchnic vasodilation, renal vasoconstriction
4. ==Hepatopulmonary syndrome== (intrapulmonary vasodilation — ==platypnea, orthodeoxia==) & portopulmonary hypertension
5. ==Coagulopathy== (↓ factors, ↑ PT), ==hypoalbuminemia==
6. ==Hyperestrogenism== — ==palmar erythema, spider angiomas (spider nevi), gynecomastia, testicular atrophy, hypogonadism==
7. ==Jaundice, fetor hepaticus==, ==spontaneous bacterial peritonitis (E. coli)==
8. ==Hepatocellular carcinoma==

## Portal Hypertension

| Site | Causes |
|---|---|
| ==Pre-hepatic== | ==Portal vein thrombosis== (neonatal umbilical sepsis, hypercoagulable, pancreatitis), splenic vein thrombosis, massive splenomegaly |
| ==Intrahepatic — presinusoidal== | ==Schistosomiasis (pipe-stem fibrosis)==, ==non-cirrhotic portal fibrosis (NCPF / idiopathic portal HTN — India)==, congenital hepatic fibrosis, sarcoidosis, PBC (early) |
| ==Intrahepatic — sinusoidal== | ==Cirrhosis (most common cause overall)== |
| Intrahepatic — postsinusoidal | ==Sinusoidal obstruction syndrome (veno-occlusive disease — pyrrolizidine alkaloids, HSCT, busulfan)== |
| ==Post-hepatic== | ==Budd–Chiari syndrome (hepatic vein thrombosis — PV, PNH, OCPs, pregnancy, HCC; IVC web — India)==, ==right heart failure, constrictive pericarditis== |

- ==Non-cirrhotic portal fibrosis (NCPF)== : ==young adults in India==, ==massive splenomegaly, well-tolerated variceal bleeds, normal LFTs==; ==obliterative portal venopathy (phlebosclerosis), no cirrhosis== ; arsenic, infections, immunologic.
- ==Extrahepatic portal vein obstruction (EHPVO)== — ==most common cause of portal HTN in Indian children==; ==portal cavernoma==.

## Biliary Diseases

| Feature | ==Primary biliary cholangitis (PBC)== | ==Primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)== |
|---|---|---|
| Patients | ==Middle-aged women== | ==Young men (30s)== |
| Ducts | ==Small intrahepatic (interlobular) bile ducts== | ==Intra- & extrahepatic, large ducts== |
| Antibodies | ==Anti-mitochondrial antibody (AMA-M2 — PDC-E2) in 95%== | ==p-ANCA (atypical, 65%)== |
| Lesion | ==Florid duct lesion — lymphocytic, granulomatous destruction of interlobular ducts== | ==Concentric "onion-skin" periductal fibrosis → fibro-obliterative "tombstone" scars== |
| Imaging | Normal | =="Beaded" appearance on MRCP / ERCP (strictures + dilatation)== |
| Associations | ==Sjögren, scleroderma (CREST), thyroiditis, RA, celiac== | ==Ulcerative colitis (70%)== |
| Complications | Pruritus (first), ==xanthelasma==, osteoporosis, ==↑ ALP, ↑ IgM==, cirrhosis | ==Cholangiocarcinoma (10–15%)==, cirrhosis, colon cancer |
| Treatment | ==Ursodeoxycholic acid==, obeticholic acid | Liver transplant |

- ==Secondary biliary cirrhosis== — prolonged extrahepatic obstruction (stones, strictures, biliary atresia, pancreatic Ca); ==bile lakes, bile infarcts, bile ductular proliferation==.
- ==Cholestasis morphology== : ==bile plugs in canaliculi, feathery degeneration of hepatocytes, bile lakes==.

## Liver Tumours

| Tumour | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Metastasis== | ==Most common malignant liver tumour== (colon, breast, lung, pancreas); ==multiple nodules with central umbilication== |
| ==Hemangioma (cavernous)== | ==Most common benign liver tumour==; subcapsular, blood-filled; avoid biopsy |
| ==Focal nodular hyperplasia (FNH)== | ==Young women==; ==central stellate scar== with abnormal thick vessels; ==polyclonal==, not neoplastic; ==not related to OCPs (mildly)== |
| ==Hepatocellular adenoma== | ==Young women on OCPs==, anabolic steroids, GSD; subtypes : ==HNF1α-inactivated (steatotic), β-catenin activated (→ HCC risk!), inflammatory (IL-6/JAK-STAT, obesity)==; ==rupture & intraperitoneal hemorrhage (especially in pregnancy)== |
| ==Hepatoblastoma== | ==Most common liver tumour of childhood (< 3 y)==; ==fetal & embryonal epithelial cells ± mesenchymal (osteoid)==; ==WNT/β-catenin==; ==FAP, Beckwith–Wiedemann==; ==↑ AFP== |
| ==Angiosarcoma== | ==Vinyl chloride, arsenic, Thorotrast== |
| ==Cholangiocarcinoma== | ==Liver fluke (Opisthorchis, Clonorchis)==, ==PSC==, Caroli disease, choledochal cyst, HBV/HCV; ==adenocarcinoma with desmoplasia (CK7 +, CK19 +)==; ==Klatskin tumour — hilar (junction of hepatic ducts)== ; ==CA 19-9== |

### Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) :

Risk factors :

1. ==Chronic HBV infection== (most common worldwide; Asia, Africa — HBx protein, integration) — HCC even without cirrhosis
2. ==Chronic HCV (with cirrhosis)== — most common in West / Japan
3. ==Alcoholic cirrhosis==, NAFLD/NASH
4. ==Aflatoxin B1 (Aspergillus flavus — TP53 codon 249 mutation)==
5. ==Hereditary hemochromatosis, α1-AT deficiency, Wilson (less), tyrosinemia (very high)==
6. Smoking, obesity, diabetes

- Morphology : ==unifocal / multifocal / diffusely infiltrative==; ==vascular invasion (portal vein — "tumour thrombus")==; ==trabecular (thick plates), pseudoglandular==; ==bile production — pathognomonic for hepatocellular origin==; ==Mallory bodies, hyaline globules==.
- Markers : ==↑ serum AFP (> 400 ng/mL in 50–75%)==, ==glypican-3, HepPar-1, arginase-1==; ==des-γ-carboxy prothrombin (PIVKA-II)==.
- Paraneoplastic : ==hypoglycemia (IGF-2), erythrocytosis (EPO), hypercalcemia==.
- ==Fibrolamellar carcinoma== : ==young adults (20–40 y), no cirrhosis / no risk factors==; ==large eosinophilic oncocytic cells in lamellar collagen bundles==; ==DNAJB1-PRKACA fusion==; ==AFP normal==, ↑ neurotensin, ↑ vitamin B12-binding capacity; ==better prognosis==.

## Gallbladder

### Cholelithiasis :

| Feature | ==Cholesterol stones (80%, West)== | ==Pigment stones== |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | ==> 50% cholesterol monohydrate== | ==Black (sterile gallbladder — calcium bilirubinate)==; ==brown (infected ducts — Asia; E. coli β-glucuronidase, Ascaris, Clonorchis)== |
| Appearance | ==Pale yellow, hard, faceted==; ==radiolucent (10–20% radiopaque)== | ==Black — small, multiple, radiopaque (50–75%)==; brown — soft, greasy |
| Risk factors | =="5 Fs" : female, fat, fertile, forty, fair==; ==OCPs/estrogens, pregnancy==, rapid weight loss, ==clofibrate==, ileal disease (↓ bile salts), ==Native Americans (Pima)==, ABCG8 | ==Chronic hemolysis (HS, sickle cell, thalassemia)==, ==cirrhosis==, ileal disease, ==infection (brown)== |

- Pathogenesis (cholesterol) : ==supersaturation + gallbladder hypomotility + accelerated nucleation (mucin) + mucus hypersecretion==.
- ==Cholesterolosis (strawberry gallbladder)== — ==lipid-laden foamy macrophages in lamina propria==.
- ==Mucocele / hydrops==, ==porcelain gallbladder (calcified wall — ↑ carcinoma risk)==.

### Cholecystitis :

- ==Acute calculous (90%)== — stone obstructing neck / cystic duct → chemical irritation (==lysolecithin==) → secondary infection; ==Murphy sign==.
- ==Acute acalculous (10%)== — ==critically ill (sepsis, burns, trauma, post-op), diabetes, vasculitis== — ischemia; higher mortality.
- ==Chronic cholecystitis== — almost always with stones; ==Rokitansky–Aschoff sinuses (outpouchings of mucosa through muscle wall)==, wall thickening, lymphocytes.
- ==Xanthogranulomatous cholecystitis== — mimics carcinoma.
- Complications : ==empyema, gangrene, perforation, cholecystoenteric fistula → gallstone ileus (Rigler triad)==, ==Mirizzi syndrome== (stone in Hartmann pouch compressing CHD), ascending cholangitis (==Charcot triad : fever, jaundice, RUQ pain; Reynolds pentad + shock + confusion==).

### Carcinoma of gallbladder :

- ==Most common malignancy of biliary tract==; ==women (2×), > 60 y==; ==India — North (Gangetic belt : UP, Bihar, West Bengal) — among highest incidence worldwide==.
- Risk factors : ==gallstones (95%)==, ==porcelain GB==, ==anomalous pancreaticobiliary duct junction==, ==chronic typhoid carriers==, ==adenomatous polyps > 1 cm==, choledochal cyst, heavy metals.
- ==Adenocarcinoma (most common)==; ==fundus (60%)==; ==infiltrating (diffuse thickening) or exophytic==; ==early direct spread to liver (segments IV, V)==; poor prognosis.
- Genetics : ==TP53, KRAS (less), ERBB2 amplification==.

:::green Rank boosters — liver
- ==Most common cause of acute liver failure : India — HEV / HBV; West — acetaminophen==.
- ==Reye syndrome== — ==aspirin in viral infection (influenza, varicella) in children== → ==microvesicular steatosis + encephalopathy==; mitochondrial dysfunction.
- ==Acute fatty liver of pregnancy== — ==LCHAD deficiency in fetus==, 3rd trimester, microvesicular.
- ==HELLP== — hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, low platelets (preeclampsia) — ==periportal fibrin deposition, hemorrhage==.
- ==Peliosis hepatis== — ==blood-filled cystic spaces== — anabolic steroids, ==Bartonella (bacillary)==, OCPs.
- ==Nodular regenerative hyperplasia== — ==non-cirrhotic portal HTN==, no fibrosis — reticulin stain.
- ==Choledochal cyst — Todani type I (most common, fusiform CBD dilation)==; ==Caroli disease (type V — intrahepatic, ARPKD association)==.
:::
