:::pearl Last-minute pearls
- ==Point of no return in cell injury== : mitochondrial dysfunction + membrane damage (amorphous densities).
- ==Hallmark of acute inflammation== : increased vascular permeability; ==of chronic== : mononuclear infiltrate + tissue destruction + repair.
- ==Single most important feature of malignancy== : metastasis.
- ==Most important prognostic factor== : breast — nodes; colon — stage; melanoma — Breslow thickness; sarcoma — grade; prostate — Gleason/grade group.
:::
