# Female Genital Tract & Breast

## Cervix

### Cervicitis :

- ==Acute== — ==Chlamydia trachomatis (most common bacterial STI), N. gonorrhoeae, HSV, Trichomonas (strawberry cervix)==, Mycoplasma genitalium.
- ==Chronic cervicitis== — ==nabothian cysts (blocked endocervical glands)==, squamous metaplasia, ==endocervical polyps (most common cervical growth)==.
- ==Transformation zone== — junction of ectocervical squamous and endocervical columnar epithelium; ==site of squamous metaplasia and > 90% of cervical neoplasia==.

### HPV & cervical neoplasia :

- ==High-risk HPV 16 (most common — SCC), 18 (adenocarcinoma, small cell)==, 31, 33, 45, 52, 58; ==low-risk 6, 11 → condyloma acuminatum==.
- ==E6 degrades p53 (via E6AP ubiquitin ligase)==; ==E7 binds RB → releases E2F==; also E7 ↑ cyclin; ==p16 overexpression (surrogate marker of high-risk HPV)==; viral ==integration== into host genome (high-risk).
- Cofactors : ==early first intercourse, multiple partners, smoking (most important cofactor), immunosuppression (HIV), long-term OCPs, high parity, Chlamydia==.

:::flow Natural history
HPV infection (mostly transient — cleared in 1–2 y)
==Koilocytes== — perinuclear halo, ==raisinoid (wrinkled) hyperchromatic nuclei==, binucleation
==LSIL (CIN I)== — dysplasia in lower 1/3 ; ==regresses in 60%==
==HSIL (CIN II, III)== — dysplasia in lower 2/3 / full thickness (CIS) ; ==p16 block positivity==
==Invasive carcinoma== — takes ~10–20 years (window for screening)
:::

### Cervical carcinoma :

| Type | Frequency | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Squamous cell carcinoma== | ==~75–80% (most common)== | ==HPV 16==; ==large cell non-keratinizing (most common)==; peak 45 y; ==2nd most common cancer in Indian women== |
| ==Adenocarcinoma== | ~15–20% (rising) | ==HPV 18==; ==adenocarcinoma in situ== ; less detected by Pap |
| Adenosquamous, neuroendocrine (small cell) | Rare | ==HPV 18==; aggressive |

- Clinical : ==post-coital bleeding (most common early symptom)==, intermenstrual bleeding, discharge; advanced — ==ureteric obstruction → hydronephrosis, uremia (most common cause of death)==, fistulae.
- Staging : ==FIGO (2018) — imaging & pathology allowed==; ==stage IIIC — pelvic / para-aortic nodes==.

### Screening & prevention :

1. ==Pap smear (exfoliative cytology)== — ==Bethesda system 2014== : NILM, ==ASC-US, ASC-H, LSIL, HSIL, AGC==, SCC; ==sample from transformation zone (Ayre spatula + cytobrush)==
2. ==HPV DNA testing (primary screening — preferred, from 30 y every 5 y)==; ==co-testing==
3. ==VIA (visual inspection with acetic acid — acetowhite areas)== & ==VILI (Lugol's iodine — Schiller test; abnormal areas iodine-negative / mustard-yellow)== — ==low-resource settings (India)==
4. ==Colposcopy & directed biopsy==
5. ==HPV vaccination== — ==bivalent (16, 18), quadrivalent (6, 11, 16, 18 — Gardasil), nonavalent (+31, 33, 45, 52, 58)==, ==Cervavac (India — qHPV)==; ==9–14 y — 2 doses (single dose also endorsed by WHO)==

:::green
- ==WHO 90-70-90 target (2030)== : 90% girls vaccinated by 15 y; 70% women screened by 35 & 45 y; 90% treated.
- ==Pap stain : Papanicolaou (EA-36 / OG-6 / hematoxylin)== — ==alcohol (95% ethanol) fixation==; ==liquid-based cytology (ThinPrep, SurePath)== — fewer unsatisfactory smears, allows HPV testing.
- ==Clue cells (bacterial vaginosis — Gardnerella), "cottage cheese" pseudohyphae (Candida), pear-shaped flagellate (Trichomonas)== on Pap.
:::

## Uterus — Endometrium

### Menstrual cycle histology :

| Phase | Days | Hormones | Histology |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Menstrual== | 1–4 | ↓ E & P | ==Fragmented glands, stromal breakdown ("blue balls" — stromal clusters), fibrin thrombi== |
| ==Proliferative== | 5–14 | ==Estrogen== | ==Straight → tubular glands, pseudostratified nuclei, mitoses in glands & stroma== |
| ==Early secretory (ovulation + 48 h)== | ==Day 16–17== | ==Progesterone== | ==Subnuclear vacuoles (earliest histologic evidence of ovulation — day 17)== |
| Mid secretory | Day 18–22 | Progesterone | ==Supranuclear vacuoles, intraluminal secretion== (peak day 21–22), ==stromal edema== |
| Late secretory | Day 23–28 | Progesterone ↓ | ==Saw-toothed glands==, ==predecidual change (around spiral arteries, day 23)==, stromal neutrophils & granular lymphocytes |

- ==Arias-Stella reaction== — ==hypersecretory glands with large hyperchromatic nuclei== — ==pregnancy (intrauterine or ectopic)==.
- ==Endometrial dating — premenstrual biopsy== for luteal phase defect (infertility).

### Abnormal uterine bleeding & endometrial lesions :

| Lesion | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Anovulatory cycle== | ==Most common cause of dysfunctional uterine bleeding== (menarche, perimenopause, PCOS) — unopposed estrogen |
| ==Acute endometritis== | Post-partum / post-abortion, ==retained products==; Group A strep, Staph |
| ==Chronic endometritis== | ==Plasma cells in endometrial stroma (diagnostic)==; IUD, PID, retained POC; ==TB (granulomas in late secretory — infertility, India)== |
| ==Endometrial polyp== | Dilated glands, thick-walled vessels; tamoxifen |
| ==Adenomyosis== | ==Endometrial glands & stroma within myometrium (> 2.5 mm / half low-power field below endometrium)==; ==diffusely enlarged, globular, boggy uterus==; multiparous women 40s; menorrhagia, dysmenorrhea |
| ==Endometriosis== | ==Endometrial glands & stroma outside uterus== — ==ovary (most common — "chocolate cyst / endometrioma")==, ==uterine ligaments, rectovaginal septum, pouch of Douglas==, pelvic peritoneum (==powder-burn, gunpowder lesions==); ==infertility, dysmenorrhea, dyspareunia, pelvic pain==; theories : ==regurgitation (Sampson — retrograde menstruation), metaplasia (coelomic), benign metastasis, extrauterine stem cells==; ==↑ estrogen (aromatase), PGE₂==; ==ARID1A — risk of endometrioid & clear cell ovarian carcinoma== |

### Endometrial hyperplasia :

- ==Unopposed estrogen== : obesity (peripheral aromatization), ==PCOS==, ==estrogen-secreting tumours (granulosa cell)==, estrogen therapy, anovulation, ==tamoxifen==.
- WHO 2014 : ==(1) hyperplasia without atypia (1–3% progress), (2) atypical hyperplasia / endometrioid intraepithelial neoplasia (EIN — 25–40% progress to carcinoma)==.
- ==↑ gland:stroma ratio (crowded glands)==; atypia = nuclear enlargement, rounding, loss of polarity, nucleoli. ==PTEN loss== (early event).

### Endometrial carcinoma :

- ==Most common invasive gynaecological cancer in developed countries==; ==postmenopausal bleeding (cardinal symptom)==.

| Feature | ==Type I (endometrioid)== | ==Type II (serous)== |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | ==80%== | 15% |
| Age | ==55–65 y (perimenopausal)== | ==65–75 y (older)== |
| Setting | ==Unopposed estrogen, obesity, DM, HTN, infertility, nulliparity, Lynch syndrome== | ==Endometrial atrophy== ; thin elderly |
| Precursor | ==Atypical hyperplasia (EIN)== | ==Serous endometrial intraepithelial carcinoma (SEIC)== |
| Genetics | ==PTEN (most common), PIK3CA, KRAS, ARID1A, MSI (MMR), CTNNB1== | ==TP53 (> 90%)==, ERBB2, PPP2R1A |
| Histology | Gland-forming, resembles endometrium; ==squamous morules== | ==Papillae, high-grade nuclei, psammoma bodies== ; ==clear cell carcinoma== also type II |
| Behaviour | ==Indolent, good prognosis== | ==Aggressive, spreads via fallopian tubes to peritoneum==, poor |

- ==TCGA molecular groups== : ==POLE ultramutated (best prognosis)==, MSI-hypermutated, copy-number low, ==copy-number high (p53 abnormal — worst)==.
- ==Protective : OCPs, smoking (anti-estrogenic), parity==.

### Myometrial tumours :

| Feature | ==Leiomyoma (fibroid)== | ==Leiomyosarcoma== |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | ==Most common tumour in women (30–50% of reproductive-age women)==; ==most common benign uterine tumour== | Rare; ==arises de novo (not from leiomyoma — practically)== |
| Genetics | ==MED12 (70%)==, HMGA2; ==FH (HLRCC — fumarate hydratase)== | Complex karyotype, TP53, RB |
| Gross | ==Well-circumscribed, firm, grey-white, whorled (trabeculated) cut surface==; ==multiple==; intramural (most common), submucosal (bleeding, infertility), subserosal | ==Soft, fleshy, hemorrhagic, necrotic, infiltrative==; solitary; postmenopausal |
| Microscopy | ==Bundles of uniform smooth muscle cells, "whorled"==; rare mitoses | ==Atypia, ↑ mitoses (≥ 10/10 HPF), coagulative tumour cell necrosis== — (2 of 3 = malignant) |
| Hormone | ==Estrogen-dependent — grow in pregnancy, regress after menopause== | — |

- ==Degenerations of fibroid== : ==hyaline (most common)==, cystic, myxoid, ==red (carneous) degeneration — pregnancy== (hemorrhagic infarction), calcific (postmenopausal), sarcomatous (< 0.1%).
- Variants : ==cellular, bizarre (symplastic), lipoleiomyoma, intravenous leiomyomatosis, benign metastasizing leiomyoma, disseminated peritoneal leiomyomatosis==.
- ==Endometrial stromal sarcoma (low grade — JAZF1-SUZ12, "tongue-like" myometrial invasion, worm-like plugs in vessels)==; ==carcinosarcoma (malignant mixed Müllerian tumour — MMMT)==.

| Feature | ==Complete mole== | ==Partial mole== |

|---|---|
| ==Invasive mole== | ==Hydropic villi penetrate myometrium== ; locally destructive; may embolize (lung) but ==no true metastasis== |

- ==Non-gestational (ovarian / testicular) choriocarcinoma== — germ cell origin; ==chemoresistant (poorer response)==.

## Ovary

### Non-neoplastic cysts :

- ==Follicular cysts (most common)==, ==corpus luteum cysts (hemorrhage)==, ==theca lutein cysts (hCG — mole, choriocarcinoma, multiple pregnancy)==.
- ==Polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS)== : ==hyperandrogenism, oligo-/anovulation, polycystic ovaries (Rotterdam : 2 of 3)==; ==↑ LH:FSH (> 2:1), insulin resistance==, obesity, hirsutism, ==endometrial hyperplasia / carcinoma risk==; ==enlarged ovaries with thick capsule ("oyster" ovaries), multiple subcapsular follicular cysts (> 20, "string of pearls")==, ==hyperthecosis==.

### Surface epithelial tumours :

| Tumour | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Serous tumours== | ==Most common ovarian epithelial tumour (benign & malignant)==; ==bilateral (malignant 65%)==; ciliated tubal-type epithelium; ==psammoma bodies==; ==CA-125 ↑, WT1 +, PAX8 +== |
| ==Low-grade serous carcinoma== | ==KRAS, BRAF==; from serous borderline tumour; indolent |
| ==High-grade serous carcinoma (HGSC)== | ==Most common ovarian cancer (70%)==; ==TP53 (~100%)==, ==BRCA1/2 (germline/somatic)==, HRD; ==origin from fallopian tube fimbria — STIC (serous tubal intraepithelial carcinoma)==; aggressive, peritoneal spread; ==PARP inhibitors (olaparib)== |
| ==Mucinous tumours== | ==Large, multiloculated, unilateral (95%)==; ==KRAS==; ==can be massive (> 20 kg)==; ==pseudomyxoma peritonei — usually from appendix (low-grade appendiceal mucinous neoplasm)==; ==CK7 + / CK20 + (primary)== — exclude GI metastasis (bilateral, CK20 +, CDX2 +) |
| ==Endometrioid carcinoma== | ==Endometriosis-associated (15–20%)==; ==PTEN, ARID1A, CTNNB1, MSI==; synchronous endometrial carcinoma (15–30%) |
| ==Clear cell carcinoma== | ==Endometriosis-associated==; ==ARID1A, PIK3CA==; ==hobnail cells, clear glycogen-rich cytoplasm==; ==hypercalcemia, thrombosis==; ==Napsin A + , HNF-1β +== |
| ==Brenner tumour== | ==Transitional (urothelial-like) epithelium nests with "coffee-bean" nuclei== in fibrous stroma; usually benign, unilateral |

- ==Risk factors for ovarian carcinoma== : ==nulliparity, BRCA1 (40–50% lifetime) / BRCA2 (10–20%), Lynch syndrome==, family history, endometriosis, ==talc==, HRT; ==protective : OCPs (most important), multiparity, breastfeeding, tubal ligation, salpingectomy==.

## Fallopian Tube & PID

- ==Pelvic inflammatory disease (PID)== — ==ascending infection : N. gonorrhoeae, C. trachomatis (most common)==, polymicrobial (post-abortion/postpartum — staph, strep, anaerobes); ==acute salpingitis → pyosalpinx → tubo-ovarian abscess → hydrosalpinx (chronic)==.
- Complications : ==infertility (tubal), ectopic pregnancy (most important risk factor — previous PID/salpingitis), chronic pelvic pain, Fitz-Hugh–Curtis (perihepatitis — "violin-string" adhesions)==.
- ==Salpingitis isthmica nodosa== — diverticula of tube (infertility, ectopic).
- ==Ectopic pregnancy== — ==ampulla (most common site)==; ==Arias-Stella reaction in endometrium==, ==decidual cast==; rupture at 2–6 weeks.

### Genital tuberculosis :

- ==Almost always secondary (hematogenous from lungs)==; ==fallopian tubes involved in nearly 100%== → ==endometrium (50–60%)==, ovaries, cervix.
- ==Important cause of infertility in India== (~5–10%); amenorrhea, ==Asherman syndrome (intrauterine adhesions)==.
- Morphology : ==tubercles, caseation, tobacco-pouch tubal blockage==, ==beaded tubes, "lead-pipe" tubes on HSG==; ==endometrial biopsy in premenstrual phase (granulomas best seen in late secretory endometrium)==; ==menstrual blood culture / CBNAAT==.

## Breast

### Inflammatory & benign lesions :

| Lesion | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Acute mastitis / breast abscess== | ==Lactation (first month) — S. aureus (abscess), streptococci (cellulitis)== via nipple cracks |
| ==Periductal mastitis (subareolar abscess, Zuska)== | ==Smokers==, keratinizing squamous metaplasia of lactiferous ducts → keratin plug → ==recurrent subareolar abscess, fistula== |
| ==Duct ectasia (plasma cell mastitis)== | ==Perimenopausal multiparous women==; ==dilated ducts with inspissated secretions, periductal plasma cells==; ==nipple retraction (mimics carcinoma)== |
| ==Fat necrosis== | ==Trauma / surgery==; ==painless firm mass, skin retraction, calcification (mimics carcinoma)== ; foamy macrophages, giant cells |

### Fibrocystic changes :

| Category | Lesions | Relative risk of carcinoma |
|---|---|---|
| ==Non-proliferative== | ==Cysts (blue-domed), apocrine metaplasia, fibrosis, adenosis== | ==No increase (1.0)== |
| ==Proliferative without atypia== | ==Usual ductal hyperplasia (epithelial hyperplasia), sclerosing adenosis, papilloma, complex sclerosing lesion (radial scar), fibroadenoma (complex)== | ==1.5–2×== |
| ==Proliferative with atypia== | ==Atypical ductal hyperplasia (ADH), atypical lobular hyperplasia (ALH)== | ==4–5×== (both breasts) |
| Carcinoma in situ | ==DCIS (8–10×, same breast), LCIS (8–10×, both breasts)== | — |

- ==Sclerosing adenosis== — ↑ acini compressed by stroma, ==myoepithelial layer preserved==; mimics carcinoma (radiologically calcified).
- ==Radial scar== — ==central nidus of entrapped glands in hyalinized stroma with radiating ducts== — mimics carcinoma.
- ==Intraductal papilloma== — ==most common cause of bloody (serous/sanguineous) nipple discharge== in women < 50 y; ==large ducts beneath areola==; fibrovascular cores with myoepithelial layer.

### Fibroadenoma vs phyllodes :

| Feature | ==Fibroadenoma== | ==Phyllodes tumour== |
|---|---|---|
| Age | ==Young (< 30 y — most common benign breast tumour in young women)== | ==Older (6th decade)== |
| Size | Small (2–3 cm), =="breast mouse" — highly mobile== | ==Larger, rapid growth== |
| Histology | ==Intracanalicular (compressed slit-like ducts) / pericanalicular (round ducts)==; stroma bland, low cellularity | ==Leaf-like (phyllodes) projections lined by epithelium into cystic spaces; hypercellular stroma with atypia & mitoses== |
| Genetics | ==MED12 (stromal)== | MED12, TERT promoter |
| Behaviour | Benign; ==juvenile fibroadenoma (adolescents)==; grows in pregnancy | ==Benign, borderline, malignant==; ==local recurrence (wide excision)==; malignant — ==hematogenous metastasis (not lymphatic)== |

### Carcinoma breast — epidemiology & risk factors :

- ==Most common cancer in women worldwide & India== (overtook cervix); ==most common cancer overall worldwide (GLOBOCAN 2020)==.

Risk factors :

1. ==Female sex, ↑ age== (most important)
2. ==Germline mutations== — ==BRCA1 (17q21 — triple-negative, medullary-like, ovary), BRCA2 (13q12 — male breast, ER +), TP53 (Li–Fraumeni), PTEN (Cowden), CHEK2, ATM, PALB2, CDH1 (lobular), STK11==
3. ==Family history — first-degree relatives==
4. ==Prolonged estrogen exposure== — ==early menarche (< 11 y), late menopause (> 55 y), nulliparity, late first full-term pregnancy (> 30 y)==, no breastfeeding
5. ==Postmenopausal obesity== (aromatization), alcohol, ==HRT (estrogen + progestin)==
6. ==Proliferative breast disease with atypia, carcinoma in situ==
7. ==Radiation to chest (Hodgkin lymphoma — mantle field)==
8. ==Contralateral breast carcinoma / endometrial carcinoma==, ↑ breast density

- ==Most common site : upper outer quadrant (50%)==; ==left > right==.

### Carcinoma in situ :

| Feature | ==DCIS (ductal)== | ==LCIS (lobular)== |
|---|---|---|
| Detection | ==Mammographic microcalcifications== | ==Incidental (no calcification, no mass)== |

| Subtype | Markers | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Luminal A== | ==ER + , PR + , HER2 −, low Ki-67== | ==Most common (40–55%)==, best prognosis, endocrine therapy |
| ==Luminal B== | ER +, (PR low), HER2 ± , ==high Ki-67== | Worse than A |

### Prognostic factors :

1. ==Lymph node status (most important prognostic factor in absence of distant metastasis)== — ==axillary nodes; sentinel node biopsy==
2. ==Tumour size==
3. ==Distant metastasis== (bone > lung > liver > brain)
