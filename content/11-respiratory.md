### Alveoli — lined by :

1. ==Type I pneumocytes== — ==95% of alveolar surface==, flat, gas exchange; cannot divide.
2. ==Type II pneumocytes== — cuboidal; produce ==surfactant== (==dipalmitoyl phosphatidylcholine / lecithin==; ==lamellar bodies==); ==stem cells for type I== (repair).

- ==Alveolar macrophages (dust cells)==; ==pores of Kohn== (collateral ventilation, spread of bacteria in lobar pneumonia).
- ==Clara (club) cells== — non-ciliated bronchiolar cells : CC10 protein, CYP450 detoxification, progenitors.

## Emphysema

- ==Irreversible enlargement of air spaces distal to terminal bronchioles (acinus) with destruction of walls== ==without obvious fibrosis==.

### Types :

:::cols
#### 1. ==Centrilobular / centriacinar==

- ==M/c type== (95%).
- ==Central acini affected, distal acini spared== (respiratory bronchioles).
- Associated with ==smoking==.
- Usually affects ==upper lobes== of lung (apical segments).

:::

:::cols
#### 2. ==Panacinar==

- ==Dilatation of the entire acinus== (RB → alveoli).
- Associated with ==α1-antitrypsin deficiency==.
- Usually affects ==lower lobes== of lung (bases).

:::cols
#### 3. ==Paraseptal / distal acinar==

- ==Distal acini affected, central acini spared== (alveolar ducts & sacs, along pleura and septa).
- Associated with ==spontaneous pneumothorax== in young adults (bullae).

### Pathogenesis — protease–antiprotease & oxidant hypothesis :

:::flow
Smoke / irritants → ==neutrophils & macrophages== recruited (IL-8, LTB4, TNF)
==Neutrophil elastase, MMPs (macrophage elastase — MMP-12)== || ==Oxidants in smoke + ROS== → inactivate ==α1-antitrypsin==
Protease > antiprotease → ==destruction of elastic tissue in alveolar walls==
Loss of radial traction on small airways → ==collapse during expiration== → air trapping
:::

:::figs
resp/emph-histo.png | Histology : broken alveoli / floating septae (arrow)
resp/emph-gross.png | Gross : centriacinar emphysema — dilated vs normal airspaces
:::

### Note : α-1-antitrypsin deficiency :

- Genetics :
    - ==Chromosome 14== : gene for α-1-antitrypsin (==α-1-AT==; SERPINA1); ==autosomal codominant==.
    - Genotype → Normal : ==PiMM==. Diseased : ==PiZZ== (Glu342Lys — misfolding, retained in ER).
- Organs affected :
    - Lungs : ==panacinar emphysema== (loss of antiprotease; smoking accelerates).
    - Liver : ==cirrhosis== (biopsy → ==PAS +ve, diastase-resistant globules== in ==periportal hepatocytes== — toxic gain of function); neonatal hepatitis, HCC.

:::green Emphysema — rank boosters
- ==Pink puffers== : emphysema — dyspnea, pursed-lip breathing, barrel chest, ==↓ DLCO==, hyperinflated lungs, ==flat diaphragm==.
- ==Blue bloaters== : chronic bronchitis — cyanosis, hypercapnia, cor pulmonale, edema.
- ==Bullous emphysema== : bullae > 1 cm — subpleural, apex; rupture → pneumothorax.
- ==Compensatory hyperinflation== (after lobectomy) and ==obstructive overinflation== (foreign body — ball-valve) are ==not true emphysema== (no destruction).
- ==Interstitial emphysema== — air in connective tissue (mediastinum, subcutaneous) — ventilator, trauma, whooping cough.
:::

## Chronic Bronchitis

- ==Persistent productive cough for at least 3 consecutive months in each of 2 consecutive years==, in the ==absence of other identifiable causes== (D/d : tuberculosis).
- ==90% patients → smokers==; also air pollution (SO₂, NO₂), cold damp climate.

### Reid index (RI) :

:::cols

- ==↑ed in chronic bronchitis== (> 0.5) : D/t ==submucosal gland hyperplasia== (mucus hypersecretion — earliest feature).

### Morphology :

1. ==Hypertrophy of submucosal mucous glands== (trachea, large bronchi) — ↑ Reid index
2. ==Goblet cell hyperplasia== (small airways — ==chronic bronchiolitis==, fibrosis → obstruction)
3. Chronic inflammation (lymphocytes), squamous metaplasia, dysplasia (→ carcinoma)

## Bronchial Asthma

- Chronic inflammatory disorder of airways → ==episodic reversible bronchoconstriction==, wheeze, ↑ airway responsiveness.

| Feature | Atopic (extrinsic) | Non-atopic (intrinsic) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Environmental allergens (dust mite, pollen) | Viral infections, pollutants, aspirin, cold, exercise |
| Mechanism | ==Type I hypersensitivity (Th2 — IL-4, IL-5, IL-13), IgE== | Not IgE-mediated; airway hyperreactivity |
| Family history | ==Positive== (atopy) | Negative |
| Serum IgE | ==↑== | Normal |

### Morphology (airway remodelling) :

1. ==Thickened basement membrane== (subepithelial collagen IV deposition) — ==most characteristic==
2. ==Hypertrophy / hyperplasia of bronchial smooth muscle==
3. ↑ submucosal glands, goblet cells; ==eosinophil infiltration==; ↑ vascularity
4. ==Mucus plugs== — occlude bronchi (status asthmaticus) ; overdistended lungs

### Sputum findings :

| Finding | Description |
|---|---|
| ==Curschmann spirals== | ==Mucus plugs with whorled shed epithelium== |
| ==Charcot–Leyden crystals== | ==Crystalloids of eosinophil galectin-10 (lysophospholipase)== |
| ==Creola bodies== | Clusters of shed epithelial cells |

:::pearl
- ==Aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease== : asthma + ==nasal polyps== + aspirin sensitivity (COX block → ↑ leukotrienes).
- ==ADAM33== (airway remodelling), ==IL-13==, ==TSLP== genes; ==hygiene hypothesis==.
- ==Status asthmaticus== — severe, unremitting; ==mucus plugs== at autopsy.
:::

## Bronchiectasis

- ==Permanent dilation of bronchi and bronchioles== caused by ==destruction of muscle and elastic tissue==, from chronic necrotizing infection.

### Causes :

1. ==Congenital / hereditary== :
    - ==Cystic fibrosis== (most common in children / West)
    - ==Primary ciliary dyskinesia== — ==Kartagener syndrome (situs inversus + sinusitis + bronchiectasis + infertility)== — ==dynein arm defect (DNAH5, DNAI1)==
    - Immunodeficiency (hypogammaglobulinemia)
2. ==Post-infectious== — ==necrotizing pneumonia (TB — most common in India)==, measles, pertussis, adenovirus
3. ==Bronchial obstruction== — tumour, foreign body, mucus impaction
4. ==Allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis (ABPA)== — ==central / proximal bronchiectasis==
5. Others : RA, SLE, IBD, post-transplant (bronchiolitis obliterans)

### Morphology :

- ==Lower lobes, bilateral==, vertical air passages; airways dilated up to 4× (==cylindroid, fusiform, saccular==); ==can be followed to pleural surface==.
- Clinical : ==copious foul-smelling purulent sputum==, hemoptysis, clubbing; ==HRCT — signet ring sign, tram-track==.
- Complications : lung abscess, brain abscess, amyloidosis (AA), cor pulmonale.

## Pneumonias

| Type | Organism | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Community-acquired typical== | ==S. pneumoniae (most common)==, H. influenzae (COPD), Moraxella, ==S. aureus (post-influenza)==, ==Klebsiella (alcoholics)==, Pseudomonas (CF) | Lobar / bronchopneumonia |
| ==Atypical== | ==Mycoplasma (most common)==, Chlamydophila, Legionella, viruses (influenza, RSV, SARS-CoV-2) | ==Interstitial pneumonia==, patchy; ==cold agglutinins (Mycoplasma)== |
| ==Hospital-acquired== | ==Gram-negative (Klebsiella, Pseudomonas, E. coli), MRSA== | Ventilator-associated |
| ==Aspiration== | ==Anaerobes (oral flora)== + aerobes | ==Right lower lobe (posterior segment of upper lobe / superior lower lobe when supine)== — abscess |

### Lobar pneumonia — stages (Laennec) :

| Stage | Time | Gross | Microscopy |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Congestion== | Day 1–2 | Heavy, boggy, red | Vascular engorgement, ==intra-alveolar fluid with few neutrophils, many bacteria== |
| ==Red hepatization== | Day 2–4 | ==Red, firm, airless, liver-like== | ==Massive exudation of neutrophils, RBCs, fibrin== filling alveoli |
| ==Grey hepatization== | Day 4–8 | ==Greyish-brown, dry== | ==Disintegration of RBCs==, persistence of fibrinosuppurative exudate |
| ==Resolution== | Day 8+ | — | ==Enzymatic digestion of exudate==; macrophages; restoration |

- Complications : ==fibrinous pleuritis → organization (pleural adhesions)==, ==empyema==, lung abscess, bacteremia (meningitis, endocarditis, arthritis), organization → ==carnification==.

| Feature | Lobar pneumonia | Bronchopneumonia |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution | ==Entire lobe, uniform== | ==Patchy, centred on bronchioles==, bilateral, basal |
| Age | Adults (20–50 y) | ==Extremes of age==, debilitated |
| Organism | ==S. pneumoniae (90%)==, Klebsiella | ==Staph, Strep, H. influenzae, Pseudomonas== |

### Interstitial (atypical) pneumonia :

- Inflammation ==confined to alveolar septa== (widened, edematous; ==mononuclear infiltrate==); ==alveolar spaces free of exudate==; ==hyaline membranes== in severe cases (DAD).
- Mild symptoms disproportionate to X-ray changes ("==walking pneumonia==").

## Pulmonary Suppuration — Lung Abscess

Causes :

1. ==Aspiration of infective material== (most common) — alcoholics, ==unconscious==, anesthesia, seizures, dental sepsis → ==anaerobes (Bacteroides, Fusobacterium, Peptostreptococcus)==
2. ==Antecedent primary bacterial infection== — ==S. aureus, Klebsiella, Pseudomonas== (post-influenza)
3. ==Septic embolism== — IE of right heart (IV drug users), thrombophlebitis — ==multiple abscesses==
4. ==Neoplasia== — post-obstructive (bronchogenic carcinoma — ==10–15% of abscesses==)
5. Others — penetrating trauma, spread from subphrenic abscess

- Site (aspiration) : ==right lung (more vertical main bronchus)== — ==posterior segment of right upper lobe, apical segment of right lower lobe==.
- Morphology : cavity with ==air–fluid level==, necrotic debris surrounded by fibrous wall.
- Complications : empyema, ==brain abscess / meningitis==, ==secondary AA amyloidosis==, hemorrhage.

## Pulmonary Tuberculosis — Lung Specifics

- Primary : ==Ghon focus (subpleural — lower upper lobe / upper lower lobe) + hilar node = Ghon complex==.
- Secondary : ==apical (Simon focus)== — ==cavitation==; ==fibrocaseous TB==; tuberculous bronchopneumonia; miliary TB.
- ==Pleural involvement== : ==serous effusion (lymphocytic, ADA ↑)==, ==tuberculous empyema==, obliterative fibrous pleuritis, ==pleural thickening==.
- Complications : ==hemoptysis (Rasmussen aneurysm)==, aspergilloma, bronchiectasis, broncho-pleural fistula, ==laryngeal & intestinal TB (swallowed sputum)==, amyloidosis, cor pulmonale.

## Atelectasis & Neonatal Respiratory Distress

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ==Resorption (obstruction)== | Complete airway obstruction → air resorbed | Mucus plug (post-operative), foreign body, tumour; ==mediastinum shifts toward atelectatic lung== |
| ==Compression== | Pleural cavity filled with fluid / air | Pleural effusion, pneumothorax; ==mediastinum shifts away== |
| ==Contraction (cicatrization)== | Local / generalized fibrosis | ==Irreversible== |
| ==Patchy (microatelectasis)== | Loss of surfactant | ==Neonatal RDS==, ARDS |

### Hyaline membrane disease (Neonatal RDS) :

- ==Deficiency of surfactant (immaturity of type II pneumocytes)== — ==prematurity (< 28 weeks: 60%)==; ==maternal diabetes== (insulin suppresses surfactant), ==caesarean section== before labour (no glucocorticoid surge), male sex, twins.
- Morphology : lungs ==solid, airless, reddish-purple (liver-like)==, sink in water; ==poorly developed atelectatic alveoli==; ==eosinophilic hyaline membranes (fibrin + necrotic epithelium) lining respiratory bronchioles, alveolar ducts==; ==little neutrophil inflammation==.
- Prevention : ==antenatal corticosteroids (betamethasone)== ; ==L/S ratio ≥ 2== (amniotic fluid), ==phosphatidylglycerol== presence = lung maturity.
- Complications (O₂ / ventilation — ROS) : ==retinopathy of prematurity (VEGF)==, ==bronchopulmonary dysplasia (↓ alveolar septation — "alveolar hypoplasia")==, ==PDA, IVH, NEC==.

### ARDS (diffuse alveolar damage) :

- Causes : ==sepsis (most common)==, ==diffuse pulmonary infections (viral — COVID-19)==, ==gastric aspiration==, trauma, pancreatitis, transfusion (TRALI), fat embolism.
- Stages : ==exudative (hyaline membranes — day 3–7)== → ==proliferative (type II pneumocyte hyperplasia, organization)== → fibrotic.
- Berlin definition : acute (< 1 week), bilateral opacities, ==PaO₂/FiO₂ ≤ 300==, not cardiac.

| Disease | Key pathology |
|---|---|
| ==Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (UIP pattern)== | ==Patchy interstitial fibrosis, temporal heterogeneity, fibroblastic foci, honeycombing== — ==subpleural, lower lobes==; > 50 y men, smokers; ==TERT, MUC5B==; nintedanib, pirfenidone |
| ==Nonspecific interstitial pneumonia (NSIP)== | ==Uniform (temporally homogeneous)== inflammation / fibrosis; CTD-associated; better prognosis |

| ==Hypersensitivity pneumonitis== | ==Type III + IV==; ==farmer's lung (Saccharopolyspora / thermophilic actinomycetes)==, bird fancier's (pigeon), bagassosis (sugarcane), byssinosis (cotton — "Monday chest tightness"); ==poorly formed non-necrotizing granulomas, bronchiolocentric== |

|---|---|---|

## Lung Tumours

### Classification (WHO) :

| Type | Frequency | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Adenocarcinoma== | ==Most common overall (~40%)==; ==most common in women, non-smokers, young== | ==Peripheral==; glandular, mucin; ==TTF-1, Napsin A +==; mutations : ==EGFR (Asian non-smoking women)==, ==ALK (EML4-ALK)==, ROS1, ==KRAS (smokers)==, BRAF, MET, RET; ==scar carcinoma==; ==adenocarcinoma in situ (lepidic, < 3 cm, formerly BAC)== |
| ==Squamous cell carcinoma== | ~25–30%; ==strongest smoking association (with SCLC)==; men | ==Central (hilar)==; ==keratin pearls, intercellular bridges==; ==p40, p63, CK5/6 +==; ==cavitation==, ==PTHrP → hypercalcemia==; ==SOX2, FGFR1 amplification, TP53, CDKN2A==; arises from ==squamous metaplasia → dysplasia → CIS== |
| ==Small cell carcinoma (SCLC)== | ~15%; ==almost exclusively smokers== | ==Central==; ==small round blue cells, scant cytoplasm, nuclear molding, salt-and-pepper chromatin, no nucleoli, crush artifact (Azzopardi effect)==, ==high mitotic rate==; ==neuroendocrine (chromogranin, synaptophysin, CD56, INSM1)==; ==TP53 + RB1 loss (nearly 100%)==, L-MYC; ==most aggressive, early metastasis==; ==ectopic ACTH, ADH, Lambert–Eaton==; ==chemo-radiosensitive, not surgical== |
| ==Large cell carcinoma== | ~10% | Undifferentiated, ==diagnosis of exclusion==; large cells, prominent nucleoli; ==gynecomastia (hCG)== |
| ==Carcinoid tumour== | 1–5% | ==Low-grade neuroendocrine==; ==young (< 40 y), not smoking-related==; ==central bronchus — polypoid "collar-button / mushroom" lesion==; ==organoid / trabecular nests, salt-and-pepper chromatin==; carcinoid syndrome rare; typical (< 2 mitoses/2 mm²) vs atypical |

### Risk factors :

1. ==Tobacco smoking== — ==most important (85–90%)== — dose (pack-years) related; ==benzo[a]pyrene, nitrosamines (NNK)==
2. ==Radon== — ==most common cause in non-smokers==; uranium miners
3. ==Asbestos== (synergistic with smoking)
4. Arsenic, chromium, nickel, vinyl chloride, ==bis-chloromethyl ether==, mustard gas
5. Air pollution (PM2.5), indoor biomass fuel (India — women)
6. Genetic — ==CYP1A1== polymorphisms, ==CHRNA== (nicotinic receptor)

### Local effects & syndromes :

| Syndrome | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| ==Pancoast (superior sulcus) tumour== | Apical tumour → ==Horner syndrome (ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis — cervical sympathetic chain)==, ==C8–T1 brachial plexus (ulnar pain)==, rib destruction |
| ==Superior vena cava syndrome== | ==SCLC (most common cause)== — facial swelling, dilated neck veins |
| Recurrent laryngeal nerve | Hoarseness (left) |
| Phrenic nerve | Diaphragmatic paralysis |
| Pericardial / pleural | Effusion (malignant, hemorrhagic) |

:::green Rank boosters — lung tumours
- ==Most common lung tumour overall : metastasis== (breast, colon, kidney — ==cannonball==).
- ==Most common benign lung tumour : hamartoma== — =="popcorn" calcification==, coin lesion, ==mature cartilage + fat + respiratory epithelium==.
- ==Lung adenocarcinoma in never-smokers : EGFR exon 19 deletion / L858R== → gefitinib, osimertinib.
- ==PD-L1 ≥ 50%== → pembrolizumab first line.
- ==Mesothelioma== : asbestos (latency 25–45 y; ==smoking NOT synergistic==); ==encases lung like a rind==; ==epithelioid (most common), sarcomatoid, biphasic==; ==calretinin, WT1, D2-40, CK5/6 +==; CEA, TTF-1 − (vs adenocarcinoma); ==long microvilli (EM)==; ==BAP1 loss==, ==CDKN2A deletion==.
- ==Solitary fibrous tumour of pleura== — ==STAT6 +== (NAB2-STAT6), hypoglycemia (IGF-2 — Doege–Potter).
:::
