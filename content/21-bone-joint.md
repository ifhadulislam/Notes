# Bones, Joints & Soft Tissue

## Bone — General

- Cells : ==osteoblasts (RUNX2, osterix; ALP; make osteoid — type I collagen)==, ==osteocytes (sclerostin, FGF23 — mechanosensors)==, ==osteoclasts (monocyte–macrophage lineage, multinucleate; TRAP +; Howship lacunae)==.
- ==RANK–RANKL–OPG== : osteoblasts/stromal cells express ==RANKL== → binds ==RANK== on osteoclast precursors (+ M-CSF) → osteoclastogenesis; ==OPG (decoy receptor) inhibits== ; ==denosumab (anti-RANKL)==.
- ==Woven bone (haphazard collagen — fetal, fracture callus, tumours, Paget)== vs ==lamellar bone (parallel, stronger — adult)==.
- Bone healing (fracture) — see Repair chapter : ==hematoma → soft callus (procallus, ~1 week) → hard (bony) callus (2–3 weeks) → remodelling (months)==.

### Developmental disorders :

| Disorder | Defect | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| ==Achondroplasia== | ==FGFR3 gain-of-function (AD; 80% new mutations, ↑ paternal age)== | ==Most common skeletal dysplasia — short limbs (rhizomelic), normal trunk, large head, trident hands==; ==impaired endochondral ossification==, ==normal intelligence== ; thanatophoric dysplasia — lethal FGFR3 |
| ==Osteogenesis imperfecta (brittle bone disease)== | ==COL1A1 / COL1A2 (type I collagen)== — AD (most) | ==Multiple fractures, blue sclerae, hearing loss (otosclerosis), dentinogenesis imperfecta, joint laxity==; ==type II — lethal in utero== ; mimics child abuse |
| ==Osteopetrosis (marble bone disease)== | ==Defective osteoclast function — carbonic anhydrase II (with RTA, cerebral calcification), CLCN7, TCIRG1== | ==Dense but brittle bones ("bone-in-bone", Erlenmeyer flask)==; ==pancytopenia (marrow obliteration), extramedullary hematopoiesis, cranial nerve palsies (narrow foramina), osteomyelitis==; ==HSCT (malignant infantile AR)== |
| ==Mucopolysaccharidoses== | Lysosomal | ==Short stature, chondrocyte vacuoles, dysostosis multiplex== |

## Osteomyelitis

### Acute pyogenic osteomyelitis :

- ==Hematogenous (children — long bones, metaphysis)==, contiguous, direct implantation.

| Setting | Organism |
|---|---|
| ==Most common overall== | ==Staphylococcus aureus (80–90%)== |
| ==Sickle cell disease== | ==Salmonella== (also S. aureus) |
| ==Neonates== | ==Group B Streptococcus, E. coli== (also S. aureus) |
| ==IV drug users== | ==Pseudomonas (vertebrae, sternoclavicular)== |
| Puncture wound through shoe | ==Pseudomonas== |
| Diabetic foot, decubitus | ==Polymicrobial, anaerobes== |
| Cat / dog bite | Pasteurella multocida |
| Children (< 4 y, unvaccinated) | Kingella kingae, H. influenzae |

:::flow Pathogenesis & morphology
Bacteria lodge in ==metaphysis (sluggish flow in sinusoidal vessels near growth plate)== → acute inflammation
Pressure, ↑ bone resorption → ==necrosis — dead bone = SEQUESTRUM==
Pus spreads through Haversian & Volkmann canals → ==subperiosteal abscess== (children — loosely attached periosteum)
==New reactive bone around dead bone = INVOLUCRUM== ; ==sinus tracts (cloaca)== to skin
Infants — spreads through epiphysis into joint → ==septic arthritis== (vessels cross growth plate)
:::

- ==Brodie abscess== — ==small intraosseous abscess walled off by reactive bone== (subacute; distal tibia).
- ==Sclerosing osteomyelitis of Garré== — jaw, extensive new bone.
- Complications : ==pathological fracture, secondary amyloidosis (AA), endocarditis, sepsis, SCC in sinus tract (Marjolin), chronic osteomyelitis==.
- ==X-ray changes appear after 10–14 days==; ==MRI — earliest==.

### Tuberculous osteomyelitis :

- ==1–3% of TB==; hematogenous; ==spine (Pott disease — thoracolumbar, T10–L1) — most common site==, knees, hips.
- ==Destroys intervertebral disc space (early) & vertebral bodies (anterior)== → ==gibbus (kyphosis)==, ==cold abscess (psoas abscess)==, ==paraplegia (Pott paraplegia)==.
- ==Caseating granulomas==, ==less reactive new bone (vs pyogenic)==.
- ==Tuberculous arthritis== — ==monoarticular, hip / knee==; ==Phemister triad (juxta-articular osteoporosis, peripheral erosions, gradual joint-space narrowing)==.

### Mycetoma (Madura foot) :

- Chronic granulomatous infection of ==subcutaneous tissue → bone==; ==triad : tumefaction (painless swelling), sinuses, grains (granules)==.
- ==Eumycetoma (fungi — Madurella mycetomatis — black grains)== vs ==actinomycetoma (bacteria — Nocardia, Actinomadura, Streptomyces — yellow/white/red grains; respond to antibiotics)==; barefoot farmers, thorn pricks; ==Splendore–Hoeppli phenomenon== around grains.

## Metabolic Bone Disease

| Disease | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Osteoporosis== | ==↓ bone mass with normal mineralization==; ==postmenopausal (↓ estrogen → ↑ RANKL, IL-1, IL-6)== & senile; ==vertebral (crush) fractures, hip (femoral neck), distal radius (Colles)==; ==thin trabeculae, normal osteoid==; ==serum Ca, PO₄, ALP normal==; ==DEXA T-score ≤ −2.5== ; secondary : steroids, hyperthyroidism, heparin, immobilization |
| ==Rickets / osteomalacia== | ==Defective mineralization (↓ vitamin D, ↓ PO₄)== → ==excess unmineralized osteoid (wide osteoid seams)==; ==↓ / N Ca, ↓ PO₄, ↑ ALP, ↑ PTH==; rickets — ==growth plate disorganization (failure of provisional calcification), rachitic rosary, bowing, craniotabes, Harrison sulcus==; osteomalacia — ==Looser zones (pseudofractures)== |
| ==Primary hyperparathyroidism (bone)== | ==Osteitis fibrosa cystica (von Recklinghausen disease of bone)== — ==subperiosteal resorption (radial side of middle phalanges — most specific), "salt-and-pepper" skull, brown tumours (hemosiderin + osteoclasts + fibrous tissue — reactive), dissecting osteitis (tunnelling resorption)==; ==↑ Ca, ↓ PO₄, ↑ ALP, ↑ PTH== |
| ==Renal osteodystrophy== | ↑ PO₄, ↓ Ca, ↑ PTH; osteitis fibrosa + osteomalacia + adynamic + ==metastatic calcification== ; =="rugger-jersey" spine== |
| ==Paget disease (osteitis deformans)== | ==> 40 y, Anglo-Saxon==; ==SQSTM1 (p62)==, ? paramyxovirus; three phases : ==osteolytic → mixed osteoclastic–osteoblastic → osteosclerotic (burnt-out)==; ==mosaic pattern of lamellar bone (cement lines — "jigsaw puzzle")==, ==huge osteoclasts (up to 100 nuclei)==; ==isolated ↑↑ ALP (normal Ca, PO₄)==, ↑ hydroxyproline; ==skull (↑ hat size), tibia (saber shin), pelvis, spine==; ==hearing loss, high-output cardiac failure (AV shunts), osteosarcoma (1%)==, fractures (chalk-stick); bisphosphonates |

:::mnemo Ca / PO₄ / ALP / PTH patterns
| Disorder | Ca | PO₄ | ALP | PTH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Osteoporosis | N | N | N | N |
| Osteomalacia / rickets | ↓/N | ↓ | ↑ | ↑ |
| Primary hyperparathyroidism | ↑ | ↓ | ↑ | ↑ |
| Secondary HPT (CKD) | ↓ | ↑ | ↑ | ↑↑ |
| Paget disease | N | N | ↑↑ | N |
:::

- ==Osteonecrosis (avascular necrosis)== : ==steroids (most common non-traumatic), femoral neck fracture, alcohol, sickle cell, Gaucher, decompression sickness, SLE==; ==subchondral wedge-shaped infarct, "crescent sign"==; ==Legg–Calvé–Perthes (femoral head, boys 4–8 y)==, ==Kienböck (lunate)==, ==Freiberg (2nd metatarsal)==, ==Osgood–Schlatter (tibial tubercle — traction apophysitis)==.

| Tumour | Age | Site | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==Osteoma== | Adults | ==Skull, facial bones (sinuses)== | ==Gardner syndrome (multiple)== |
| ==Osteoid osteoma== | ==Young (10–20 y), males== | ==Cortex of diaphysis — femur, tibia== | ==< 2 cm radiolucent nidus with surrounding dense sclerosis==; ==nocturnal pain relieved by aspirin / NSAIDs (PGE₂)== |
| ==Osteoblastoma== | Young | ==Posterior elements of vertebrae== | ==> 2 cm==; ==dull pain, not relieved by aspirin== |
| ==Osteosarcoma== | ==Bimodal : 10–20 y (75%) & elderly (Paget, radiation)== | ==Metaphysis of long bones — around knee (distal femur > proximal tibia), proximal humerus== | ==Most common primary malignant bone tumour (excluding myeloma)==; ==malignant cells producing osteoid (lace-like) — diagnostic==; ==Codman triangle (periosteal elevation), sunburst ("sun-ray") appearance==; ==RB (retinoblastoma survivors — 1000×), TP53 (Li–Fraumeni), MDM2/CDK4 (low-grade)==; ==hematogenous lung metastasis==; ↑ ALP; ==telangiectatic variant (ABC-like)==; ==parosteal (surface, low-grade, posterior distal femur — MDM2)== |
| ==Osteochondroma (exostosis)== | ==Adolescents (most common benign bone tumour)== | ==Metaphysis near growth plate (distal femur, proximal tibia)== | ==Bony stalk with cartilage cap — medullary cavity continuous with host bone==; ==grows away from the joint==; ==EXT1/EXT2 (hereditary multiple exostoses)== → chondrosarcoma (5–20% in multiple) |
| ==Enchondroma== | 20–50 y | ==Small bones of hands & feet (medullary)== | ==Hyaline cartilage==; ==Ollier disease (multiple)==, ==Maffucci syndrome (multiple + soft-tissue hemangiomas — high sarcoma risk)==; ==IDH1/IDH2== |
| ==Chondroblastoma== | ==10–20 y (before growth plate closure)== | ==Epiphysis (distal femur, proximal tibia, proximal humerus)== | ==Chondroblasts with "chicken-wire" calcification, nuclear grooves==; ==H3F3B (H3.3 K36M)== |
| ==Chondromyxoid fibroma== | Young | Metaphysis | Rare |
| ==Chondrosarcoma== | ==> 40–60 y== | ==Axial skeleton — pelvis, shoulder girdle, ribs, proximal femur (medullary)== | ==Malignant cartilage — "popcorn / ring-and-arc" calcification==; ==2nd most common malignant bone-matrix tumour==; ==IDH1/2, EXT==; ==chemo- & radioresistant (surgery)==; grade most important; ==dedifferentiated, clear cell (epiphysis), mesenchymal== variants |
| ==Giant cell tumour (osteoclastoma)== | ==20–40 y (after fusion), women== | ==Epiphysis (extending to metaphysis) — distal femur, proximal tibia (around knee — 50%), distal radius== | ==Lytic, eccentric, "soap-bubble" appearance, expands to subchondral bone==; ==numerous osteoclast-type giant cells (up to 100 nuclei) in sheets of mononuclear stromal cells (neoplastic — RANKL +)==; ==H3F3A (H3.3 G34W)==; ==locally aggressive, recurs; "benign" lung metastasis (2–4%)==; ==denosumab== |
| ==Aneurysmal bone cyst== | < 20 y | Metaphysis, vertebrae | ==Blood-filled spaces separated by septa with giant cells, "blue bone"==; ==USP6 rearrangement==; "fluid–fluid levels" |
| ==Simple (unicameral) bone cyst== | Children | ==Proximal humerus / femur metaphysis== | ==Fallen-fragment sign== (pathological fracture) |
| ==Fibrous dysplasia== | Children/young | Ribs (most common), femur, craniofacial | ==Curvilinear "Chinese-letter" woven bone trabeculae without osteoblastic rimming in fibrous stroma==; =="ground-glass"==, ==shepherd's crook deformity (femur)==; ==GNAS== — ==McCune–Albright (polyostotic + café-au-lait "coast of Maine" + precocious puberty)==; ==Mazabraud (+ intramuscular myxomas)== |
| ==Non-ossifying fibroma (fibrous cortical defect)== | ==Children (most common bone lesion)== | Metaphysis, eccentric, cortical | ==Storiform spindle cells, giant cells, foamy macrophages==; self-limiting |
| ==Ewing sarcoma / PNET== | ==< 20 y (10–15 y), boys, white== | ==Diaphysis of long bones (femur), pelvis== | ==Small round blue cells, Homer Wright rosettes (PNET), PAS + (glycogen)==; ==CD99 (MIC2) membranous +, FLI-1, NKX2.2==; ==t(11;22)(q24;q12) EWSR1-FLI1 (85%)==; =="onion-skin" periosteal reaction==; ==fever, ↑ ESR, leukocytosis (mimics osteomyelitis)==; ==radiosensitive== |
| ==Multiple myeloma== | ==> 60 y== | ==Axial (vertebrae, skull, ribs, pelvis)== | ==Most common primary malignant tumour of bone overall==; ==punched-out lytic lesions== |
| ==Metastases== | Adults | ==Axial skeleton (vertebrae — most common), proximal femur, humerus== | ==Most common malignant tumour of bone overall==; ==osteolytic : kidney, lung, thyroid, GIT, melanoma, myeloma==; ==osteoblastic : prostate (most), breast (mixed), carcinoid, SCLC, Hodgkin==; ==children — neuroblastoma, Wilms, rhabdomyosarcoma== |

:::pearl
- Periosteal reactions : ==Codman triangle & sunburst — osteosarcoma==; ==onion-skin (lamellated) — Ewing==; ==soap-bubble — GCT, ABC, ameloblastoma==; ==ground-glass — fibrous dysplasia==; ==popcorn calcification — chondroid tumours, pulmonary hamartoma==.
- ==Bone tumours below 5 y — metastatic neuroblastoma; 5–20 y — osteosarcoma / Ewing; > 40 y — metastasis / myeloma / chondrosarcoma==.
:::

## Joints

### Osteoarthritis vs Rheumatoid arthritis :

| Feature | ==Osteoarthritis (degenerative)== | ==Rheumatoid arthritis== |
|---|---|---|
| Primary lesion | ==Articular cartilage degeneration (chondrocyte injury)== | ==Synovium (immune-mediated synovitis)== |
| Age | ==> 50 y==; obesity, trauma | 30–50 y; ==F : M = 3 : 1== |
| Joints | ==Weight-bearing — knees, hips, spine; DIP (Heberden nodes), PIP (Bouchard nodes)==, 1st CMC | ==Small joints of hands & feet symmetrically — MCP, PIP (spares DIP)==, wrists, cervical (atlantoaxial) |
| Morning stiffness | ==< 30 min==; pain worsens with use | ==> 1 hour==; improves with activity |
| Pathology | ==Fibrillation, fissuring, eburnation (polished ivory bone), subchondral cysts, osteophytes (bone spurs), joint mice (loose bodies)== | ==Pannus (inflamed proliferating synovium + granulation tissue) → erodes cartilage & bone==; ==villous synovial hyperplasia, lymphoid follicles, plasma cells, fibrin (rice bodies)==; ==fibrous / bony ankylosis== |
| Deformities | Varus knee | ==Ulnar deviation, swan-neck, boutonnière, Z-thumb== |
| Systemic | None | ==Rheumatoid nodules (palisading granulomas around fibrinoid necrosis — extensor surfaces), vasculitis, ILD, pleuritis, pericarditis, Felty syndrome (RA + splenomegaly + neutropenia), Sjögren, Caplan, anemia, AA amyloidosis, scleritis== |
| Lab | Normal | ==RF (IgM anti-IgG Fc — 80%), anti-CCP (most specific)==, ↑ ESR/CRP; ==synovial fluid : inflammatory (WBC 5,000–50,000, neutrophils)== |
| Genetics | — | ==HLA-DR4 (shared epitope), PTPN22==; ==smoking — citrullination (PAD)== |

### Gout & pseudogout :

| Feature | ==Gout== | ==Pseudogout (CPPD)== |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal | ==Monosodium urate== | ==Calcium pyrophosphate dihydrate== |
| Shape | ==Needle-shaped== | ==Rhomboid / rod-shaped== |
| ==Birefringence== | ==Strongly negative (yellow when parallel to slow ray of compensator)== | ==Weakly positive (blue when parallel)== |
| Joints | ==1st MTP (podagra)==, ankle, knee | ==Knee (most common)==, wrist |
| Other | ==Tophi (urate with foreign-body giant cells — ear helix, olecranon, Achilles; dissolve in formalin → use alcohol fixation)==; ==urate nephropathy, stones==; ==Lesch–Nyhan (HGPRT)==, ==tumour lysis==, diuretics, alcohol, purine-rich food | ==Chondrocalcinosis== (radiograph); ==hemochromatosis, hyperparathyroidism, hypothyroidism, hypomagnesemia, hypophosphatasia== |

- ==Septic arthritis== — ==N. gonorrhoeae (young sexually active — migratory polyarthritis, tenosynovitis, pustules)==, ==S. aureus (most common overall)==, ==H. influenzae (children < 2 y)==, Salmonella (sickle cell); ==synovial WBC > 50,000, neutrophils, low glucose==.
- ==Lyme arthritis== — Borrelia burgdorferi (large joints).
- ==Seronegative spondyloarthropathies (HLA-B27)== : ==ankylosing spondylitis (sacroiliitis, "bamboo spine", enthesitis, uveitis, aortitis)==, reactive arthritis (==post-urethritis (Chlamydia) or dysentery — "can't see, can't pee, can't climb a tree"==), psoriatic (==DIP, "pencil-in-cup"==), enteropathic.

### Tumour-like joint lesions :

- ==Ganglion cyst== — wrist (dorsal), ==no true lining==; ==synovial cyst (Baker cyst — popliteal)==.
- ==Tenosynovial giant cell tumour== — ==localized (giant cell tumour of tendon sheath — fingers, most common)== & ==diffuse (pigmented villonodular synovitis — knee, hemosiderin, foamy cells; CSF1 translocation)==.

| Tumour | Key features |
|---|---|
| ==Lipoma== | ==Most common soft-tissue tumour of adults==; mature fat; HMGA2 |
